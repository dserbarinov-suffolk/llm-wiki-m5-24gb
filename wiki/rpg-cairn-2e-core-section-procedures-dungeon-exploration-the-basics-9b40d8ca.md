## Statements

- The dungeon exploration cycle (see below) is divided into a series of Turns , Actions , and their consequences. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00258))_
- On their turn , a character can move a distance equal to their torchlight's perimeter (about 40ft), and perform one action . Players can use their action to move up to three times that distance though that will increase the chance of triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00259))_
- The Warden should present obvious information about an area and its dangers freely and at no cost. Moving quickly or without caution may increase the chance of encountering a wandering monster, springing a trap, or triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00260))_
- Although the term "dungeon" is used here, it can mean any dangerous locale (mansions, farmhouses, adventure site, etc). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00261))_
