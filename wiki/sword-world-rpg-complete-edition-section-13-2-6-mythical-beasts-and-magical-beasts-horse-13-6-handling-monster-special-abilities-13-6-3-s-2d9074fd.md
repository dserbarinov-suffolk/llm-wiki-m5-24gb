## Statements

- This is an ability that many monsters have. If an attack with this ability is successful, then in subsequent rounds, the character will remain strangled, and the only thing he or she can do is try to escape. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08792))_
- To escape, make a success roll using adventurer level + strength bonus as the baseline score. The attack points of the attack is the target score (or the attack points of the hit , if using the optional rule to give monsters variance in attack points). If you succeed, you can break free, but if you fail, you'll automatically suffer damage at the end of the round. This is treated as normal damage, so it's possible to reduce it due to armor. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08793))_
- While being strangled, a character suffers a -4 penalty to evasion and is normally unable to make sound. Depending on the monster, the penalty may be smaller, or it might be possible to still make sound, but if this is the case it'll be specified in the description . Unless otherwise noted, the evasion penalty is 4 and magic is also considered not possible. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08794))_
- If a character is strangled and another is trying to rescue him or her by attacking, please refer to the optional rule for grappling (see p. 265) for how to handle it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08795))_
