# Principles

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- - Always clarify what is about to happen. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00574))_
- - Regularly restate a character's surroundings, where they are in physical space, and what their senses are detecting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00575))_
- - Do not tell characters how they should feel, and only tell them what's happening right now, before their eyes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00577))_
- - Do not tell characters how they should feel, and only tell them what's happening right now, before their eyes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00577))_
- - Provide hints about what lies beyond the next corridor, including odors, sounds, and elevation. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00578))_
- Remind them who has already moved, who has yet to take an action, and so on. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00579))_
- - The cost of dungeon exploration is paid in turns. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00580))_
- Players should always be made aware of the cost. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00580))_
- If the party spends too long in one place, acts without caution, makes noise, or enters a new area, they risk a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00580))_
