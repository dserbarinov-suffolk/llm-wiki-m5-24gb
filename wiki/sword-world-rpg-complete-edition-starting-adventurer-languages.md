# Starting Adventurer Languages

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Immediately after creation, an adventurer has the following language abilities. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.158)
- All adventurers can speak common and racial . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.158)
- Those with an intelligence of 6 or greater can also read their mother tongue . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.158)
- Those with an intelligence of 12 or above can additionally read common. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.158)
- In the case of humans, grassrunners, and half-elves raised in human society, the mother tongue referred to here is the regional language of the region where the adventurer was born and raised. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.158)
- If the adventurer is an elf, dwarf, or half-elf raised in an elven society, mother tongue is the racial language of the race in which the adventurer was born and raised ( elven or dwarven ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.158)
