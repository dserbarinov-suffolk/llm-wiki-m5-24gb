# Islands of Azarn (Language = Eastern/Mārdan)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The Islands of Azarn are an archipelago located in the southeastern part of the Alecrast continent. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
- Though once a single nation, it is now divided into three kingdoms. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
- For detailed information about the Islands of Azarn, please refer to Sword World RPG Scenario Collection #4 "The Rainbow Crystal Palace". (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
