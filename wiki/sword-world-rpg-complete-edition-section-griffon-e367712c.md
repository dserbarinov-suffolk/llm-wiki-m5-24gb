## Statements

- The griffon is a magical beast with the body of a lion, the head of an eagle, and the wings of an eagle, and it can fly. They have a strange habit of collecting shiny things, and a large amount of gold, silver, and jewels are stored in a griffon's cave on the cliff. They mainly feed on small animals and do not attack humans unnecessarily, but if an adventurer is ostentatiously wearing jewelry, they may attack in order to steal it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-06615))_
