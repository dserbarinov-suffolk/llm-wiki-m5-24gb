# Instant Death

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- When damage reduces you to 0 hit points and there is damage remaining, you die if the remaining damage equals or exceeds your hit point maximum. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00433))_
- Massive damage can kill you instantly. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00433))_

## Technical atoms

> If she takes 18 damage from an attack, she is reduced to 0 hit points, but 12 damage remains.
_(source: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00434))_

> For example, a cleric with a maximum of 12 hit points currently has 6 hit points. If she takes 18 damage from an attack, she is reduced to 0 hit points, but 12 damage remains. Because the remaining damage equals her hit point maximum, the cleric dies.
_(source: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00434))_
