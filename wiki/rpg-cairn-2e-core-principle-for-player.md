# Principles for Players

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about principles for players:

## Statements

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Players / Agency

- Attributes and related saves do not define your character. They are tools. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00026))_

- Don't ask only what your character would do; ask what you would do, too. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00027))_

- Be creative with your intuition, items, and connections. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00028))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Players / Exploration

- Asking questions and listening to detail is more useful than any stats, items, or skills you have. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00033))_

- There is no single correct way forward. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00035))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Players / Talking
