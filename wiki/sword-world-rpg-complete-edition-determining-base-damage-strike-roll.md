# Determining Base Damage (Strike Roll)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Damage calculation takes three steps: ① Determine base damage , ② Add bonus damage , and ③ Subtract the monster's defense points . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.44)
- The first step is to determine base damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.44)
- The numbers are listed in a row, but of course there is no need to memorize them at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.44)
- For example, if your character's strike power is 14, you only need to look at key number column 14. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.44)
- This process is called a strike roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.44)
- His weapon is a broadsword (a one-handed weapon) with required strength 14, so his strike power is 14 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- The result of his 2D roll is 6, so the number on row 6 under key number column 14 on the Rating Table is 4 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Base damage is 4 points . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- This means your attack hit the target, but failed to deal any damage!. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Maybe it hit the hardest part of their armor, or maybe it only hit the hilt of a spear or ax, but no damage was dealt. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- In other words, you have landed a greater strike than would normally be expected. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Then, roll 2D again and look under the same key number on the Rating Table. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- This is your base damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- This process can continue as long as you keep rolling greater than or equal to the critical target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- In theory, this could deal an infinite amount of damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- We'll make a note of the 6 and roll the dice a second time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Ducard II's critical target is 10. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Suppose that Ducard Ⅱ , having hit the goblin with an attack, rolls a 10 when determining base damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- The number on row 10 under key number column 14 on the Rating Table is 6 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- The number on row 12 is 8 , so we'll make a note of the 8 and roll a third time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- This did not reach the critical target, so the base damage check ends here. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- If the third roll were a 2 (double ones), the base damage would instead be 6+8+0= 14 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If your hit check is a success and you're able to hit your target, the damage dealt to your opponent can be determined next.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
The player should then roll 2D.
```

<a id="atom-3"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
Now that he's hit the goblin, Ducard II must determine how much damage his strike dealt.
```

<a id="atom-4"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When making a strike roll, if your 2D roll is equal to or greater than the critical target (usually 10 , 9 if using the thief skill), the strike is critical .
```

<a id="atom-5"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If the second 2D roll is also greater than or equal to the critical target, you can make a third roll to add even more damage.
```

<a id="atom-6"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If a critical occurs, but you roll double ones on the second and subsequent damage checks, 0 is added to the previous result (i.e., the previous result remains the same ), and the base damage check will end.
```
