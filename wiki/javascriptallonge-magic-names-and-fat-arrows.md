## Key supported claims

- Fat arrow functions bind this and arguments from their enclosing scope, unlike regular functions. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.75-77)
- Fat arrow functions are lightweight and often used with constructs like mapping or callbacks. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.75-77)
- In a fat arrow function, arguments[0] refers to the outer environment's argument, not the inner one. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.75-77)
- A contrived example demonstrates a fat arrow function returning a multiplication table row. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.75-77)
- The mapWith method is used with ordinary collections elsewhere. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.75-77)
