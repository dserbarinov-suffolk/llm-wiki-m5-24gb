# [ ⑩ Weather Prediction [Intelligence] ] (No equipment restriction, not retriable)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Normally you would use a target score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- If a ranger succeeds on a success roll against a set target score using ranger skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score, the ranger can roughly predict the weather (sunny, cloudy, rain, snow, storm, strong wind.... (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- From these combinations, you can roughly predict the weather. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- Rangers can measure the humidity using their own senses, and they also use small knowledge such as birds that eat small insects such as flies fly close to the ground = no updraft = thick clouds = rain . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- Rangers have the ability to predict the weather. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- etc.) The game master must decide whether to use a difficulty or a target score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- This is because failure means that the weather is unpredictable, and this is also very clear to the characters as well. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If you attempt to use this ability and fail the success roll, you simply cannot predict the weather; it does not predict the opposite.
```
