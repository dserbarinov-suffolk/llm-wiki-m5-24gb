# 5th Level Ancient Magic List [ Apport ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- However, even though it's their possession, the caster must have a complete memory of it, and moreover must be able to identify specifically where it's kept. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- This is a spell that allows the caster to teleport their possessions into the palm of their hand. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- If the duration expires or if the spell is canceled, the target immediately returns to its original location. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- Also, the size of the target is limited to something you can hold in your hand (for example, you can summon a sword, but not armor). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- As a result of this, spirit magic that depends on that spirit power will no longer be usable. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- This spell has the effect of negating any one of the four major spirit powers of earth, water, fire, or wind. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- However, only one kind of spirit power can be erased at a time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- This spell cannot be expanded to block two or more kinds of spirit power from working at the same time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- However, this has no effect on greater spirits (behemoth, kraken, efreet, djinn). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- If that spirit is present, it will also disappear from existence and return to the spirit world. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- Also, if a spell effect is present due to the negated spirit power (either gnome, undine, salamander, or sylph) within the spell's area of effect, it will be negated. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- This spell is what creates that formidable warrior. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- Resist=None A skeleton warrior, also known as a dragontooth warrior, is a kind of golem created from a dragon's tooth, and resembles a human skeleton with a sword and shield. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- Only the caster can give commands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- The caster can give commands to the skeleton warrior in high ancient. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- For example, commands such as kill anyone who enters this room or kill that man are very simple and can be carried out, but a command such as go to the next village and kidnap the chief's daughter cannot be carried out. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- They cannot understand overly complex commands, but they handle simple commands accurately. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- The dragon's tooth, which is the source of the skeleton warrior, is a very valuable item as one of the treasures of the ancient kingdom. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- You'd have to pay a whopping 5000 silver coins to purchase it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- During the duration, all words that the caster hears are subject to a true-or-false check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- However, while you can discover lies, you cannot know truths. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- By casting this spell, the caster can discover parts that are untrue, exaggerated, and dramatized in conversations they overhear. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.75)
- Therefore, anything that's not in view, such as behind a wall, will not become visible even if you use this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- However, even if movement speed changes, the caster's agility itself does not change. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- If the spell's effect is removed while you're flying, you'll naturally fall. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- The speed of flying in the sky is what would be movement speed 50, and during full movement, 50 km per hour is the maximum speed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- Casting this spell allows the caster to fly freely. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- Its strike power is 20. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- Expansion=Final score, distance, area, damage certainty Resist= Reduced effect This spell creates a sudden storm containing countless pieces of ice the size of pebbles, in a space within a 5 meter radius centered on a point, dealing cold damage to everything within range. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
