## Statements

- Magic can also be contracted as opposed to expanded. However, this does not reduce the amount of mental power consumed. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01665))_
- You can make the area of effect narrower than originally intended. The shape (usually a circle) cannot be changed. You also cannot cast it in such a way that it hits the enemy directly in the eyes. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01670))_
- Similar to area contraction, you can also reduce the volume of the targeted object or space. Again, the shape cannot be changed, and you cannot directly aim it at the enemy's eyes. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01672))_
- Magic with a duration can be optionally shortened. However, you cannot set a time that causes the effect to wear off in the middle of a round. You may only set it to wear off at the end of a round. You must also decide how long you want the magic to last before you cast it. Magic that's already been cast cannot be canceled at will. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01674))_
- Magic power can be optionally reduced. This will reduce the final score and, if it's damage magic, also reduce bonus damage (it's not possible to only reduce the final score or only reduce bonus damage). You must declare how many points you'll reduce before you roll the dice. If used on multiple targets, you must reduce it by the same amount for all of them. Also, if the side casting the magic rolls double sixes, the resisting side will also need double sixes, no matter how much magic power was declared to be decreased (if it's a monster, it cannot resist). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01676))_
- All magic that can expand their effects can also contract their effects. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01678))_
- These characteristics are presented here as magic types . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01681))_
- A spell that deals damage to the target's life force or mental power. Spells of the damage type always have a clearly defined strike power . If you manage to break through the target's resist, use the spell's strike power to determine damage by making a strike roll using the Rating Table, same as if you had hit with a weapon in weapon combat. The key number is, of course, the indicated strike power . If you roll 10 or greater during the strike roll, a critical will occur and deal even more damage, same as in weapon combat. Conversely, If you roll double ones, it does not deal any damage at all. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01683))_
- Most magic of the damage type can still deal damage even if it's resisted by the target, only with a reduced effect. In this case, you must make the strike roll after subtracting 10 from strike power. Also, at this time, no matter how high you roll during your strike roll, a critical will not occur. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01684))_
- In either case, the caster's magic power can be added as bonus damage to the result of the strike roll. Also, the target's adventurer level (or the monster's monster level) is subtracted from the damage. However, there's an exception if you roll double ones during your strike roll. In this case, no matter how much magic power your character (monster) has, they cannot deal damage to the target at all. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01685))_
- Also, even magic of the damage type may in rare cases take no effect if resisted. At this time, if the target successfully resists, you cannot make a strike roll. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01686))_
- You may only set it to wear off at the end of a round. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01674))_
- You must also decide how long you want the magic to last before you cast it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01674))_

5.1.8.2
Combined Expansion and Contraction
Although  it's not  often  done,  it's possible  to  perform  magic  expansion and contraction  at  the  same  time.  For example,  you  could extend the  area  of effect  while reducing magic  power.  The mental  power  required  at  this  time  is decided by expansion . It cannot be reduced by contraction. For example, if you double the  area and reduce  magic power by -4 , the mental power consumed will still be double .
