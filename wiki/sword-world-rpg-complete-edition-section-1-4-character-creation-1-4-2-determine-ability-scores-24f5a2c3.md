- Once ability scores are determined, sub-ability scores are never used during the game. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00326))_

- Our rolls are 9, 7, 5, 8, 6, 8, 10, and 4. These can be considered average, with neither extremely high nor low rolls. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00330))_
