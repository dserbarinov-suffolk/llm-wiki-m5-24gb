### Chapter 6: / 6.3 Ranger Skill / [ ② First-Aid [-] ] (No equipment restriction)

- The ranger skill is also limited by equipment, just like the thief skill, if you want to make full use of its abilities. The armor you wear must have a required strength of up to half your own strength, and must be ring mail or non-metal _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04000))_
- The ranger skill has some similar abilities to the thief skill. However, while the thief skill can be used both outdoors and indoors, the ranger skill is mostly useful only outdoors. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04001))_
- However, while the thief skill can be used both outdoors and indoors, the ranger skill is mostly useful only outdoors. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04001))_

### Chapter 6: / 6.3 Ranger Skill / [ ① Footprint Tracking [Intelligence] ] (No equipment restriction, not retriable)

- This is the ability to track footprints left on the ground. The game master determines the difficulty by taking into account factors such as the condition of the ground and the amount of time that has passed since the footprints were made. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04020))_
- If an adventurer's life force falls to 0 or less due to wounds, it will not recover naturally and there is a risk of death if left untreated. First-aid provided by a ranger will restore your life force to 1 point, allowing you to regain consciousness. It cannot fail, but it will take about 30 minutes of game time, and it cannot be done while in a state where you can't focus, such as in the middle of combat, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04021))_

### Chapter 6: / 6.3 Ranger Skill / [ ③ Camouflage [Intelligence] ] (Equipment restriction)

- This is the ability to hide behind rocks, hide in bushes, or use such things to hide something. Since it's an ability that uses natural terrain, it cannot be used indoors. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04023))_
- Checks are made in the same way as the thief skill's Hide (see p. 114). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04024))_

### Chapter 6: / 6.3 Ranger Skill / [ ⑥ Stealthy Step [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

- Rangers can walk silently and unnoticed. This is handled the same way as the thief skill's ability. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04030))_

### Chapter 6: / 6.3 Ranger Skill / [ ⑬ Follow [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

- This handled the same way as the thief skill's ability of the same name. However, it can only be used in nature. It cannot be used indoors or in a city. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04046))_
