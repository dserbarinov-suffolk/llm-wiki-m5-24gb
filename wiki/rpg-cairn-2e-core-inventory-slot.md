# Inventory Slots

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about inventory slots:

## Statements

- Most items take up one slot unless otherwise indicated. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00114))_
- Petty items do not take up any slots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00115))_
- A bag of coins worth less than 100gp is petty and does not occupy a slot. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00116))_
