# [ ⑭ Disguise [Dexterity] ] (No equipment restriction)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Things like height and physique can be changed to a certain extent, but there are still limits. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- This is the ability to change one's face and appearance using special makeup. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- Especially when disguised as someone whose characteristics the thief doesn't know in detail, it's best to add a difficult modifier. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- The game master should add modifiers to success rolls to account for the unnaturalness of the disguise (such as disguising as the opposite sex, etc.). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
