Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Each of their action announcements are attack the opponent (with a weapon) . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- Ducard II cannot carry out his intended attack on the goblin. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- However, IIDash's action order = agility is the same as the goblin's, so he would've been slashing at the goblin at the same time . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- Suppose that, as in the first example, IIDash is still hit badly enough to fall down as a result of the goblin's attack check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** example

```
Suppose that Ducard II has an agility of 13, which is equal to that of a goblin (let's call him IIDash from now on, since he is a bit faster). In this case, both attacks are considered to be carried out simultaneously. During the course of the game, each attack check is made in the order of goblin → IIDash (or vice versa). Otherwise, it would be difficult to progress the game.
```
