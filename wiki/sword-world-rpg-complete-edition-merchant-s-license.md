# Merchant's License

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Those who are approved receive a permit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- Without it, one cannot legally open a shop. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- If someone conducts business in public without a license, they will be punished. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- While merchants don't have a guild, to be recognized as a merchant and conduct business, they must register with the king. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)

Of course, a Merchant's License issued in one country isn't valid everywhere, but if you wish to trade in another country, having a license typically means you can bypass strict screening and simply pay a standard fee to proceed.
