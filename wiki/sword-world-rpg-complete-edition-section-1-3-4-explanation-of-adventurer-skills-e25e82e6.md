# 1.3.4 Explanation of Adventurer Skills

## Statements

- Adventurer skills are the most important thing for an adventurer in Sword World RPG. This is because which adventurer skills a character has acquired will determine what that character can do, and will reveal that character's characteristics. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00221))_
- Below is a brief description of each adventurer skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00222))_
- Those with this skill are adept warriors, who fight with weapons. They are experts in combat, able to use all kinds of weapons and armor. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00224))_
- A wandering swordsman who makes a name for himself solely on the strength of his sword, and eventually establishes a kingdom. Such a story is also possible in Forcelia. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00225))_
- One who has this skill will be called a thief . As indicated, they bring along abilities that are useful for theft, such as pickpocketing, lock-picking, and so on. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00227))_
- However, not everyone with the thief skill makes a living off such thefts. The abilities of the thief are also useful in adventures. For example, there's the ability to detect and disable traps that have been set, and the ability to stay ahead of footsteps, listening and observing what's going on. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00228))_
- Thieves also have some fighting ability. Their fighting style is based on lightness, and although they cannot use heavy weapons or armor, they can use light weapons and armor that does not impede their movement, allowing them to fight as well as a fighter. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00229))_
- For this reason, many of them have made a name for themselves as adventurers . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00230))_
- Those with the sage skill are commonly referred to as sages. They're knowledgeable and well-versed in all fields of study (academics in Forcelia are not differentiated, and one is rarely versed in only one specific field). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00233))_
- Although they are often thought to be confined to their study rooms, many of them do go out on adventures , because they believe that the best way to learn is to see and hear firsthand . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00234))_
- An expert in outdoor activities. Those with this skill are adept at outdoor living, and dealing with a variety of natural hazards. These abilities are, of course, essential for adventure . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00236))_
- Rangers are also excellent with projectile weapons and have the same offensive capabilities as fighters, especially when it comes to projectiles. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00237))_
- Those who play musical instruments and sing songs for a living are called bards. Thus, those with the bard skill have the ability to play an instrument and sing songs. Many legends and folklore are often woven into songs passed down from ancient times, and natural bards are familiar with these legends and folklore. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00239))_
- Bards also have the ability to sing magical songs called spellsongs . These songs have magical effects on all who hear them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00240))_
- The five adventurer skills of sorcerer, shaman, priest, dark priest, and dragon priest are sometimes collectively referred to as rune master skills . This is because these five classes use runes and have the ability to handle magic. Rune masters are viewed with fear by the general public because of their magical abilities. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00242))_
- Bards also use magical abilities through their spellsongs but are not considered rune masters. There is a great difference in the spells that rune masters handle, both in quality and quantity. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00243))_
- The ancient kingdom of Kastuul, which fell more than 500 years ago, is sometimes referred to as the magical kingdom . As its nickname suggests, Kastuul is said to have accomplished various miracles through the power of magic. It is this magical power that is known as high ancient and is the magic that the sorcerers of today have inherited. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00245))_
- With the fall of Kastuul, much of high ancient was lost, but some has survived to the present day. The skill that can handle it is the sorcerer skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00246))_
- Many sorcerers are also sages. The rules do not require one character to acquire both of these skills, but if they are acquired at the same time, it gives them a significant advantage. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00247))_
- It is said that when creating Forcelia, the gods divided the world into three: the spirit world , the fairy world , and the material world . The spirit world is the world of spirits who command various powers. The material world is the world in which physical beings like us humans live, and the fairy world is the world that connects the two. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00249))_
- Silent spirit are runes used to communicate with spirits and draw out the powers they control. The skill that can use silent spirit is called the shaman skill, and those who have this skill are referred to as shamans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00250))_
- The powers controlled by spirits are diverse, ranging from the four major powers of earth , water , fi re , and wind to the spirit of humans , and there are many different types of spirit magic used by shamans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00251))_
- Those who exercise their power as agents of the gods are priests. In the ancient days of myth, the gods who created the world were later divided into light and darkness and waged a great war. A great war that engulfed all the ancient races- including the legendary dragons of old --ended with all the gods losing their bodies. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00253))_
- Gods who have lost their physical bodies cannot engage with the material world , which is the world for those with bodies. Instead, they interact with the world through priests. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00254))_
- Priests, as agents of the gods, can use holy prayer and cast holy magic . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00255))_
- The gods are divided into two main groups. The gods of light and the gods of darkness . This is the same classification as the two factions that waged war in ancient times. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00257))_
- Gods who belonged to the gods of darkness are now considered wicked gods . Each of the gods teaches and guides people, but the teachings and guidance of the gods of darkness are extremely individualistic and deny human reason, because they are far removed from human morality , so to speak. Another hindrance to belief in the wicked gods, is the fact that the majority of people in Forcelia today believe in the teachings of the gods of light . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00258))_
- However, even the wicked gods have their believers. As agents of the wicked gods, there are dark priests who use daemon scream and cast dark magic , which is essentially equivalent to Divine Magic. Players may not choose the dark priest skill as one of their character's skills. This is because it would be significantly more difficult to have a character practice the teachings of the gods of darkness , when examining a roleplaying game from the role-playing perspective. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00259))_
- Dragon roar is a system of magic that imbues one's own body with the characteristics of a dragon, and the ultimate goal is to transform oneself into a dragon. Therefore, they worship dragons and try to live like them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00262))_
