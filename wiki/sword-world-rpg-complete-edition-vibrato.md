# Vibrato

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This spell is effective on objects that break due to vibrations, such as thin glass, ceramics, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.111)
- These objects will shatter on the fourth round after the spellsong begins. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.111)
