# Character

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about character:

## Statements

- Complete DEX and WIL loss renders the character unable to act until they are restored through extended rest or by extraordinary means. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00225))_
- If two characters need to take an action together, whoever is most at risk should save (usually the character with the lowest relevant Attribute). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00176))_
- Characters may be powerful, but they are also vulnerable to harm in its many forms. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00013))_
- Don't ask only what your character would do; ask what you would do, too. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00027))_
- Player Characters have just three Attributes: ( Strength (STR) , Dexterity (DEX) , and Willpower (WIL) ). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00101))_
- A character with a low STR is not necessarily hopelessly weak; they can still attempt to lift a heavy door or survive a deadly fight! _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00103))_
- Characters have a total of ten inventory slots but can only carry four or five items comfortably without the help of bags, backpacks, horses, carts, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00108))_
- A character cannot fill more than ten slots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00112))_
- Characters roll a d20 and compare the results to the appropriate attribute. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00174))_
- If a character is forced to add Fatigue to their inventory but they have no free slots, they must drop an item from their inventory. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00185))_
- On their turn , a character can move a distance equal to their torchlight's perimeter (about 40ft), and perform one action . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00261))_
- The players then declare their character's intended movements and actions . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00265))_
- The Warden resolves the actions of each character simultaneously, along with any actions that are already in progress. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00266))_
- A character can spend a turn performing an exhaustive search of one object or location in an area, revealing any relevant hidden treasure, traps, secret doors, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00280))_
