# [ Lesser Daemon ] Grunel

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- They seem to have been especially proficient at enchanting, and often carry a magical sword. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.228)
- If a grunel has a magic sword, add a bonus of 1 point to both its attack points and strike points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.228)
- A grunel is a humanoid lesser daemon with steel-blue skin. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.228)
- If it uses its tail to entangle, treat this as a strangling attack (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.228)
- They can also use dark magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.228)
- They have the ability to use ancient magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.228)
- A zalbard's method of attack is the sharp claws on its hands, and it also has the power to breathe magical fire from its mouth. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- In addition, fire and heat attacks cannot wound a zalbard. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- The zalbard is a lesser daemon that's said to be the model for the gargoyle. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Its eyes are also bright red, and glow suspiciously. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- They have the ability to use dark magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Its appearance is almost identical to that of a gargoyle, but its skin is fiery red. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Those attacked by the fire will suffer damage equal to a strike power 10 strike roll plus 5 points of bonus damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- If you succeed on a resistance roll, strike power becomes 0. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- It has a bright red open mouth straight across its face, but no other features. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- To shapeshift or shapeshift back requires one full round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- This daemon has the ability to completely copy any opponent's appearance after just one look, including their clothes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- A doubleburg is almost the same size as a human, and its entire body is pitch black. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Even if it shapeshifts, a doubleburg's score data will not change at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- For example, even though it looks like it's wielding a weapon, it's actually a part of the doubleburg's own body that's been shapeshifted. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- However, it cannot copy its opponent's abilities and memories. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Frequency=Very rare Intellect=Human Reaction=Adversarial, or command- based Attack Points=Fangs: 13 (6)/Claws: 14 (7) Strike Points=17+Poison/16. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Poison (toxicity score 13, strike power 20, bonus damage 6) Habitat=Underground labyrinths, secluded ruins. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229)
- Its sharp fangs contain a powerful poison, and those who fail to resist will immediately suffer damage equal to a strike power 20 strike roll plus 6 points of bonus damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229-230)
- It has wings reminiscent of a giant bat, can reach over 3 meters in height with its neck extended, and uses its long neck to attack by biting. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229-230)
- It also uses its strong tail as a weapon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.229-230)
- The Maligdorai is a daemon whose upper body resembles an owl and lower body the hind legs of a goat, and its entire body is covered in feathers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- As well as wings on its back, it has jointless tentacle-like viscera sprouting from the base of each wing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- The maligdorai can show illusions to surrounding creatures, dealing damage to their minds (these illusions have no effect on monsters that don't have mental points). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- If the target succeeds, they will escape the effect and can act freely, but if the target fails to resist, they will see an illusion of what they fear as an enemy deep within their heart attacking them, and as a result will suffer strike power 10 + bonus damage 7 damage to their mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- They will also be unable to take any actions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- A maligdorai's most frightening ability is illusion . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- To recover from this, you must defeat their master, the maligdorai. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- Those being manipulated cannot recover their mental power through sleep or through magic such as Transfer Mental Power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- Also, spells that affect the mind have no effect on those in this state. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- Those who lose all their mental power due to an illusory attack will from then on be turned into a being that can be freely manipulated by the maligdorai. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- Illusions cannot be used in the round immediately after suffering damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- If its focus is interrupted or it's unable to focus due to making physical attacks, suffering damage, etc., then it cannot maintain its illusions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
- A maligdorai can also show illusions to multiple creatures within its vision at the same time, but it must focus in order to perform the illusions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.230)
