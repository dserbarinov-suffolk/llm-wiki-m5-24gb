# Shields

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In the case of shields, there are only two types: small shields (required strength 1) and large shields (required strength 13). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.23)
- Therefore, it is safe to choose the less expensive small shield (Table 1-10: Shields). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.23)
- However, in terms of increasing a character's defensive ability, the effects of small and large shields are basically the same in this game. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.23)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If a character's strength is 13 or greater, a large shield can be chosen.
```
