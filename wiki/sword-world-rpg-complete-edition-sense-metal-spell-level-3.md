# [ Sense Metal ] (Spell Level=3)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- You can determine the quality of a metal by its color, its purity by its strength, and the amount by what you can see in range. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107-108)
- Area=A space within a 30 meter radius from the caster Duration=18 rounds Effect=Sense the type, purity, and amount of surrounding metals and veins Type=Detection Expansion=Duration, area Resist=None Sense Metal is a spell that allows you to sense metals or veins containing metals in the surrounding area, as well as their type, purity, and amount. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107-108)
- For the duration of the spell, the priest of Braki will see their surroundings in a unique color. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107-108)
- No material can block the effects of this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107-108)
