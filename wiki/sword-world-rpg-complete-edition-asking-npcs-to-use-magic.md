# Asking NPCs to Use Magic

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- You may want to have a Remove Curse cast on you when you're cursed, a Resurrection cast when someone dies, or have a magical item appraised using Analyze Enchantment . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- In some cases, adventurers may need to rely on NPCs to save them from situations that are beyond their control. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- High ranking (high level) rune masters are not easy to come by. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- First, the game master must decide whether there are enough rune masters to do this. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- Even in a large country, there may or may not be at least one rune master who has reached 10th level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- Once you have found such a rune master, you must next determine whether or not your request will be granted. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- They surely won't hear your requests too easily, and it's questionable whether they'd even allow you to meet them in the first place. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- The higher-ranking rune masters are , the busier they are. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- Basically, you can expect to meet any rune master up to 2 levels higher than the adventurers' adventurer level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- If you want to meet someone at a higher level, you'll need some connections on the side. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- Of course, it would be difficult to ask a single rune master to do all sorts of things again and again, and if the adventurers conclusively incur an NPC's displeasure, no amount of money will make them say yes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- through a ritual, you have to double, triple.... (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- The same goes for having a ritual performed . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- And of course, a certain price is required in order to have the magic cast. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- The market price is NPC's rune master skill level x base mental power cost for magic x 20 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- For such requests, you usually give 10% to 30% as advance payment. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- At this time, the final score required for resurrection is 19. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- Think about when you use Resurrection to bring back a friend who lost their life on an adventure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- However, assuming the priest's intelligence bonus is +2 , a 2D roll of 7 or greater is required for a successful resurrection. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- You successfully find a 10th level priest and the negotiations are settled. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- This is only possible if you're part of a group of people who believe in dragons (mostly considered savages). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- Dragon priests are based on a unique belief in dragons. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- By increasing their level, they can have deeper connections to dragons, and they can gain access to new powers (spells) immediately. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)
- If you roll 6 or greater on a success roll, it'll be a success. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- The money required at this time is 18,000 gamels. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- To be even more certain, the adventurers request an additional +3 to their final score via ritual. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- However, in order to do so, you must donate 9000 x 5 (because the eventual final score is +4) = 45,000 gamels to the priest (temple). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- In this case, the average advance payment is between 4,500 and 13,500 Gamels. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- Trying to force people to pay is , of course, a criminal act. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- These are the going rates when an adventurer makes a request to an NPC . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
- Adventurers themselves cannot go around charging these same rates. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.155)
