## Key supported claims

- Aeneas reigned over the Latins for three years, followed by Ascanius for thirty-three years, and Silvius for twelve years, with Posthumus ruling for thirty-nine years. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- The sons of Liethali obtained the country of the Dimetae, including the city of Menavia, and held it until expelled by Cunedda and his sons. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- A siege of a tower resulted in the sea swallowing all the attackers after a year of preparation, except for one ship wrecked with thirty men and women. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- A magician's prediction led to his death, and the child born to his wife was named Brutus, who accidentally killed his father with an arrow. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- From the first arrival of the Saxons into Britain to the fourth year of king Mermenus, 428 years are computed. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
