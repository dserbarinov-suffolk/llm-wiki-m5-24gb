## Statements

- This represents the amount of damage dealt by the monster. If a character fails to evade their attack, these strike points become damage and reduce the character's life force. A character can reduce this damage through armor (defense rolls) and adventurer level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05943))_
- Monsters that have multiple attack methods also have multiple strike points written next to them. The arrangement of attack points also carries over to strike points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05944))_
