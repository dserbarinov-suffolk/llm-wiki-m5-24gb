## Statements

- This is the ability to appropriately treat illnesses and injuries and aid in their recovery. Healers can help a wounded person recover their life force naturally. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04313))_
- Normally, natural damage recovery is 1 point per day (see p. 54). However, having a healer increases it dramatically. When a healer is nursing, use the Rating Table with healer skill level as the key number. The patient can recover life force equal to the result plus the healer's intelligence bonus. If the result when using the Rating Table is double ones , there will be no recovery at all (the treatment method was poor, and the result was not good). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04314))_
- It takes at least one hour to use this ability. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04315))_

### Chapter 7: / 7.5 [ ② Treatment [Intelligence]] / [ ② Seamanship [Intelligence] ]

- Sailors have the ability to know direction by looking at the stars on the sea at night, and to identify dangerous areas where reefs are likely to exist. The game master should ask for a success roll check for such actions using sailor skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score. The difficulty or target score is determined by the game master each time. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04359))_

### Chapter 7: / 7.5 [ ② Treatment [Intelligence]] / [ ④ Swim [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

- Sailors have the ability to swim. Follow the rules for swim under common adventurer abilities -- replace adventurer level with sailor skill level --and make a check. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04363))_

### Chapter 7: / 7.5 [ ② Treatment [Intelligence]] / [ ⑤ Weather Prediction [Intelligence] ] (Not retriable)

- Sailors are able to sensitively feel and predict changes in the weather while out at sea. Please see the ranger ability of the same name. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04365))_

### Chapter 7: / 7.5 [ ② Treatment [Intelligence]] / [ ⑥ Climb [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

- Sailors have the ability to climb in order to perform actions such as ascending masts or stretched nets. This is handled in the same way as that of the thief skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04367))_

### Chapter 7: / 7.5 [ ② Treatment [Intelligence]] / [ ⑥ Climb [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction) / [ ⑦ Ropework [Dexterity] ]

- Sailors have the ability to tie ropes at will so that they do not unravel or, conversely, quickly unravel. A sailor can untie a knot by succeeding on a success roll using sailor skill level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score. At this time, the game master should decide the difficulty or target score, taking into consideration the tightness of the knot and the time it'll take. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04369))_
- A rope that a sailor tied to keep it from coming undone should have a difficulty equal to the sailor's skill level if someone attempts to untie it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04370))_
