## Statements

- There is no bonus to evasion speed. When facing monsters with fixed strike points, there is 1 point of damage reduction . When facing opponents who use weapons like adventurers and determine damage through strike rolls, there is an effect that increases their critical target by +1 , but there is no direct damage reduction. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09478))_
