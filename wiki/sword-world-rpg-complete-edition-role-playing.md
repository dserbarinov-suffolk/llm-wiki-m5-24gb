### What is a Role-Playing Game (RPG)?

- Role-Playing means to act out a role . A role-playing game (RPG), as the name suggests, progresses by having each participant in the game play out an assigned role . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00023))_
