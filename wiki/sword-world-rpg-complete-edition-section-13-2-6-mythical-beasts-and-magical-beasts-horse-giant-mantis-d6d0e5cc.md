## Statements

- This is a praying mantis about the size of a human, with quick movements, it unleashes two sickles to kill and eat animals. It is also able to fly over a distance of several tens of meters. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-06934))_
- When attacking a human, a giant mantis will use its sickles to capture their arms and attempt to block their movement. Anyone hit by a giant mantis's attack will have one arm trapped. You must roll the dice to determine which arm is trapped. Roll 1D, and if it's 1-3, it'll be the right arm, but if it's 4-6 it'll be the left arm. Ancient magic cannot be used while one arm is trapped. Also, on subsequent rounds, you cannot escape from the giant mantis, so your evasion points will be -4. In order to escape from a sickle that's trapped your arm, you must succeed on a resistance roll against target score 10 (or attack points from when the attack hits, if using optional rules) using adventurer level + strength bonus as the baseline score. If you cannot escape, you will suffer 8 points of damage from strangling (see p. 236). This damage can be reduced by armor and adventurer level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-06935))_
