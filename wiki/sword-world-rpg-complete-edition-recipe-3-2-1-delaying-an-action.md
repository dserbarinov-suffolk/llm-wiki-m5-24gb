## Applicability And Rationale

- Characters (including NPCs and monsters) do not have to act based on their agility and can instead delay their actions. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01103))_
- In such a case, a character may lower their action order to 0 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01105))_
- In other words, they will act only at the very end of the round. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01105))_
- In this case, an intended attack on an enemy that is about to withdraw can be changed to another target that you're engaged with. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01107))_
- At this time, an attacker can choose any new target to switch to (from among those they're engaged with). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01108))_
