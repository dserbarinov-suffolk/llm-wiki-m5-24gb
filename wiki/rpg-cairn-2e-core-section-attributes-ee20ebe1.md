# Attributes

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- Each of the three Attributes are used in different circumstances. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00164))_
- - Strength (STR) : Used for saves requiring physical power, like lifting gates, bending bars, resisting poison, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00165))_
- - Dexterity (DEX) : Used for saves requiring poise, speed, reflexes, dodging, climbing, sneaking, balancing, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00166))_
- - Willpower (WIL) : Used for saves to persuade, deceive, interrogate, intimidate, charm, provoke, manipulate spells, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00167))_
