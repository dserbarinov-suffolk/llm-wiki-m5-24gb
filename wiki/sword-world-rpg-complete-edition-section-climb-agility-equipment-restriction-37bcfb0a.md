## Statements

- Sailors have the ability to climb in order to perform actions such as ascending masts or stretched nets. This is handled in the same way as that of the thief skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04329))_
- Sailors have the ability to tie ropes at will so that they do not unravel or, conversely, quickly unravel. A sailor can untie a knot by succeeding on a success roll using sailor skill level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score. At this time, the game master should decide the difficulty or target score, taking into consideration the tightness of the knot and the time it'll take. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04331))_
- A rope that a sailor tied to keep it from coming undone should have a difficulty equal to the sailor's skill level if someone attempts to untie it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04332))_
- Sailors have the ability to climb in order to perform actions such as ascending masts or stretched nets. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04329))_
