# Throwing Multiple Darts or Daggers

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- The number thrown at the same time becomes a penalty to attack power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- This optional rule increases the chance that a high-level character will defeat a low-level character or monster in one blow. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- There must always be one target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- Never forget the limitation of projectiles: they cannot be used while in an engagement . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
