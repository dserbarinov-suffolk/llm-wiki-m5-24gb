## Key supported claims
- The Antikythera mechanism is an ancient Greek hand-powered orrery, often described as the oldest known analog computer. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
- The device could track the positions of the Sun and Moon in the zodiac, the phase of the Moon, and predict solar and lunar eclipses using the Saros cycle. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
- The inscriptions suggest the device may have originated in Corinth or its colonies, possibly linking it to the school of Archimedes in Syracuse. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
