## Key supported claims

- An ancient mechanical device, likely the oldest known analog computer. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
- A gear system tracks celestial events, including positions of the Sun and Moon, lunar phases, and eclipse predictions. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
- Gear-cutting precision may indicate an established workshop tradition, though no similar devices or workshops have been found. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
- X-ray imaging techniques revealed hidden inscriptions acting as a user manual, providing insights into its operation. (raw/antikythera-mechanism.md)
