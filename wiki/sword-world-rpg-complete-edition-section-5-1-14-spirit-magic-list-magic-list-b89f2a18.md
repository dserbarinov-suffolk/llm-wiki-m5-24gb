### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / Magic List / [ Windstorm ] (Djinn/Greater Wind Spirit)

- This spell creates a tornado in a 10 meter radius around a point in space. Anyone except the caster within the spell's area of effect, or who attempts to enter the tornado during the spell's duration must attempt to resist. Those who successfully resist will move at half their normal movement speed (there is no change in their action order) and can take actions with a -2 penalty on success rolls such as attack, evasion, etc. However, those who fail to resist are slammed to the ground and can only move 3 meters per round while crawling on the ground for the duration of the spell. When attacking, evading, etc. in this state, all will suffer a -4 penalty. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03232))_
- Additionally, the tornado creates a vacuum that cuts the skin and flesh of those within its area of effect, dealing strike power 10 damage. The cutting damage continues to have an effect until the target leaves the area of effect. Damage is determined by making a strike roll each round. Only the first attempt to resist this spell counts. Objects weighing less than 10 kilograms or equipment held in the hand or not securely attached to the body will be blown out of the area of effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03233))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / Magic List / [ Crack ] (Behemoth/Greater Earth Spirit)

- Duration=Instant Effect=Trap the target between a crack in the ground, dealing strike power 30 damage Type=Damage (Earthquake-type) targets, Expansion=Final score, distance, target volume, damage certainty Resist=Negated effect This is a spell that creates deep cracks, causing the ground underfoot to cave in, and swallowing the target in a deep chasm created this way. If a target falls into the cracks, they will first be buried in the ground up to their chest, and then be caught between the closing bedrock. Then, by being crushed between the bedrock, they will suffer strike power 30 damage. The effect of the spell is momentary, but once something is swallowed up by the crack, it cannot climb out of it on its own. It remains trapped in the crack until someone else pulls it out, which takes one round. When trapped in the ground, there is a 4 penalty to attack power (points) and evasion speed (points). If you successfully resist the spell, you will avoid being swallowed by the crack and will not be affected. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03236))_
- If a target falls into the cracks, they will first be buried in the ground up to their chest, and then be caught between the closing bedrock. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03236))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / Magic List / [ Valkyrie Blessing ] (Valkyrie/Mind Spirit)

- When you chant this spell, a faint glow of light inhabits the target's armor or shield, covering the target's entire body in protective light. The protective light absorbs all damage up to 50 points in total, whether it's caused by weapons or by magic. The protective light thus fades each time you take damage, and disappears when it's dealt more than 50 points of damage. When the protection runs out, any damage beyond that (such as suffering 20 damage when the protection has only 10 remaining) will not reach the target. Any damage is first absorbed by this protective light, so until it vanishes, virtually no damage can be dealt directly to the body. When damage is dealt to this protection, reductions due to armor or adventurer level are not taken into account. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03248))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / Magic List / [ Plant Shell ]

- When you chant this spell, plants will grow along the perimeter of a 5 meter radius circle centered on the place the caster touches, creating a 2 meter tall dome of natural plants on the next round. The dome serves as a tent that protects from the elements and as a temporary shelter when a suitable campsite cannot be found. The inside of the dome is covered with a carpet of soft fresh grass, making it moderately warm and moderately cool. The outer walls of the dome are tough and densely intertwined with living plants, protecting those inside from attacks by wild beasts and less powerful monsters. When entering or exiting the outer wall, the entrance can be partially opened or closed with one command from the caster. Additionally, the dome itself blends in well with the terrain, especially in forests and jungles, making it extremely difficult to locate the dome itself. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03260))_
