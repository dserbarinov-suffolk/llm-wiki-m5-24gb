From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- When the outcome is uncertain, the dice determine the results. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00091))_
- The GM calls for an ability check when a character or monster attempts an action (other than an attack) that has a chance of failure. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00091))_
- For every ability check, the GM decides which of the six abilities is relevant to the task at hand and the difficulty of the task, represented by a Difficulty Class. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00092))_
- The Typical Difficulty Classes table shows the most common DCs. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00093))_
