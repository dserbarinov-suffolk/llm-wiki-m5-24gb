# Representing Special Monsters with Character Data

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Among monsters, there are some that have inherently strong individual differences. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.234)
- These monsters, like adventurers, come in a variety of strengths, so it' d be more natural to describe them by their ability scores and skill levels like adventurers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.234)
- Here, in order to represent monsters such as featherfolk, mermen, dark elves, noble lizardmen, and centaurs as characters, we'll show how to calculate the sub-ability scores of each monster ( Table 13-2: Determining Monster SubAbility Scores ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.234)
- These rules were not designed with the idea of playing monsters as characters in mind. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.234)
