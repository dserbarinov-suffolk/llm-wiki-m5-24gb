# Consuming Mental Power

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- When using magic, an adventurer must temporarily reduce their mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- Each magic has a score called base mental power cost . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- A character with shaman skill level 2 uses the Fire Bolt spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- The base mental power cost for Fire Bolt is 5 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- The character must consume 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5, rounding up to 3 points of mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- For example, a character with 5 points of mental power remaining can use magic that costs 5 points of mental power, but cannot use magic that costs 6 or more points of mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- The character cannot regain consciousness unless they recover their mental power to 1 point or more in some way. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
