## Statements

- This spell has the effect of commanding a sylph to deliver air vibrations (sounds) to the caster without weakening them. As a result, you can specify a point in space within 500 meters and hear every conversation and sound exchanged within a radius of 5 meters from that point. Conversely, you can also transmit your voice to the location. Since it can be used to narrow the area of effect, it's also possible to communicate with just a single target. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02717))_

- Brownies are spirits that live in old houses and castles that were built over 50 years ago. They may clean the house while the family members are sleeping. This spell has the effect of giving a command to a brownie, specifying a chore the caster would like them to do while they're asleep. However, the requested job must be a simple one. If you request something too complicated, it's too much for them and you can't expect a good result. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02759))_
