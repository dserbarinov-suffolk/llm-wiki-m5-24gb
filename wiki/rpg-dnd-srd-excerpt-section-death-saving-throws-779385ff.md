# Death Saving Throws

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Unlike other saving throws, this one isn't tied to any ability score. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00438))_
- Unlike other saving throws, this one isn't tied to any ability score. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00438))_
- On your third success, you become stable (see below). _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00439))_
- The successes and failures don't need to be consecutive; keep track of both until you collect three of a kind. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00439))_
- If the roll is 10 or higher, you succeed. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00439))_
- The number of both is reset to zero when you regain any hit points or become stable. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00439))_
- If you roll a 20 on the d20, you regain 1 hit point. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00440))_
- When you make a death saving throw and roll a 1 on the d20, it counts as two failures. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00440))_
- If the damage is from a critical hit, you suffer two failures instead. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00441))_
- If you take any damage while you have 0 hit points, you suffer a death saving throw failure. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00441))_
