# Frightened

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- - A frightened creature has disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of its fear is within line of sight. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00557))_
- - A frightened creature has disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of its fear is within line of sight. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00557))_
- - The creature can't willingly move closer to the source of its fear. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00558))_
- - The condition also ends if an effect removes the grappled creature from the reach of the grappler or grappling effect, such as when a creature is hurled away by the thunder-wave spell. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00562))_
