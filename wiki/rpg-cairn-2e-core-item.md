## Statements

### Character Creation / Inventory / Inventory Slots

- Most items take up one slot unless otherwise indicated. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00113))_

- Petty items do not take up any slots. Bulky items take up two slots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00114))_

### Core Rules / Combat / Rounds

- During the fi rst round of combat , each PC must make a DEX save in order to act. Special circumstances, abilities, items, or skills may negate this requirement. PCs that fail their save lose their turn for this round. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00205))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Dungeon Exploration Cycle

- The players record any loss of resources and any new conditions (i.e. item use, deprivation , etc). The cycle then begins again. If appropriate, the Warden should roll on the Dungeon Events table. Keep common sense in mind when interpreting the results! _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00265))_
