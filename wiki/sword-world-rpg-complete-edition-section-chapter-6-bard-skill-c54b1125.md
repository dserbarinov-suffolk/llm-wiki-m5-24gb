### Chapter 6: / Bard Skill / [ ① Sing [Mental Power] ]

- This is the art of singing songs. Although they're called songs, unlike modern pop songs, most of them are melodies attached to legends and folklore. Heroic tales and love poems are used as subjects. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04166))_
- This is the art of playing a musical instrument. The higher the bard's level, the more skillful they will be at playing an instrument. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04168))_
- Although they're called songs, unlike modern pop songs, most of them are melodies attached to legends and folklore. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04166))_

### Chapter 6: / Bard Skill / [ ⑦ Medicines [Intelligence]] ((Appraisal only) Not retriable)

- This ability represents knowledge of poisons and medicines. Sages can appraise unknown medicines and poisons, and can mix magical potions and poisons with various effects. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04170))_
- To create medicines or poisons, the game master should set a target score depending on the type, and ask for a success roll check. The target score for a poison which is able to kill people even in small amounts and be tasteless and odorless, is around 20. Regardless of the result or dice roll, you will need materials and at least one week to attempt to create a medicine. You can make up to 10 doses at most. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04172))_
- In Alecrast, the creation and use of poison is considered shameful and inhumane . Therefore, game masters should not allow PCs to create or use poisons. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04173))_
- For detailed rules regarding poison, please also refer to 12.2.1: Poisons (see p. 159). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04174))_
- Bards can earn bard skill level x bard skill level x 2D silver coins by singing and playing an instrument for about an hour in a suitable location (such as a tavern, etc.). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04175))_
- Therefore, game masters should not allow PCs to create or use poisons. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04173))_
- Bards can earn bard skill level x bard skill level x 2D silver coins by singing and playing an instrument for about an hour in a suitable location (such as a tavern, etc.). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04175))_

### Chapter 6: / Bard Skill / [ ⑦ Medicines [Intelligence]] ((Appraisal only) Not retriable) / [ ③ Languages (Spoken) [Intelligence] ]

- This ability is almost the same as the sage skill's ability of the same name, but it only applies to speaking, not reading. In other words, bards can converse in languages other than their native language, but they cannot read (or write, of course) text. Unlike the sage skill, there is no restriction on the languages that can be learned. However, of course, you cannot learn runes. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04177))_
- For detailed rules regarding languages, please refer to 12.1: Rules Regarding Language (see p. 157). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04178))_
- Musical instruments are always required to sing spellsongs. Being able to sing is also a necessary condition, but there is an exception if you choose a flute as your instrument. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04181))_
- A bard can learn one spellsong per skill level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04182))_
- For detailed rules regarding spellsongs, please refer to 5.3: Spellsongs (see p. 109). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04183))_
- This ability is almost the same as the sage skill's ability of the same name, but it only applies to speaking, not reading. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04177))_
