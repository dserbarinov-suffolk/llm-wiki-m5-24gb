- The fact that there are countless unseen masses of rock floating in the sky is considered a matter of course among sages. These rocks fall to the ground at a rate of about once every hundred years. These rocks floating in the sky are called meteorites. The spell Meteor Strike is a spell that allows the caster to control a meteorite (a very small one) at will, and make it fall to the ground. The meteorite will fall the moment you finish the spell, causing a large explosion.
