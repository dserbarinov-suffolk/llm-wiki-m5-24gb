# Island of Damned (Language = Native Language)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This is an island floating far south of the Alecrast continent. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
- Several nations exist in a state of turmoil, and wars appear to be ongoing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
- The island suffered an invasion by daemons, which led to it being named "Accursed Island." There appears to be limited trade with the continent. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
- For detailed information about the Accursed Island, please refer to the "Lodoss World Guide". (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.288)
