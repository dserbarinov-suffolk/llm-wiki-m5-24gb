# Ability Scores

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Ability scores apply not only to humans, but also to the various non-human races that become adventurers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- There are six types of ability scores: dexterity , agility , intelligence , strength , life force , and mental power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- This is especially important when wielding weapons in combat. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- It relates to a variety of tasks that involve the use of one's hands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Dexterity represents the dexterity of an adventurer's hands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- In combat, it is important when evading enemy attacks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Agility represents an adventurer's physical balance and agility. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- It's related to working with the whole body. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- It also represents the ability to perceive things, such as finding hidden objects and sensing abnormalities spur of the moment. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Intelligence represents the speed of an adventurer's mind and the amount of knowledge they hold, or in other words, their so-called smarts. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Above all, it is important when using magic, a major attraction in fantasy. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Strength represents an adventurer's muscular strength. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- If you want to use large weapons, heavy armor, etc., high strength is required. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- The higher this number, the more robust the adventurer is . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Life force represents how well an adventurer can withstand physical injury. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Mental power is also related to magic resistance. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Mental power represents the adventurer's willpower and perseverance. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Using magic is hard work, and one cannot hope to cast much magic without a high mental capacity. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
