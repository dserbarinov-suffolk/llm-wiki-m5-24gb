## Statements

- Always clarify what is about to happen. Does that flaming barrel look stable or like it might explode at any minute? _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00600))_
- Regularly restate a character's surroundings, where they are in physical space, and what their senses are detecting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00601))_
- Do not tell characters how they should feel, and only tell them what's happening right now, before their eyes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00603))_
- Provide hints about what lies beyond the next corridor, including odors, sounds, and elevation. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00604))_
- Ensure that the players understand their character's place in the Dungeon Cycle. Remind them who has already moved, who has yet to take an action, and so on. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00605))_
- The cost of dungeon exploration is paid in turns. If the party spends too long in one place, acts without caution, makes noise, or enters a new area, they risk a roll on the Dungeon Events table. Players should always be made aware of the cost. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00606))_
