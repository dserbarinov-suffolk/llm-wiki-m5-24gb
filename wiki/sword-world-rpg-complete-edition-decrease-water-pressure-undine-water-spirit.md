# [ Decrease Water Pressure ] (Undine/Water Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- All water pressure exerted on the body by the spell will be alleviated regardless of its strength, and reduced to a point where normal actions are no longer a problem. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.92)
- Targets under this spell are saved from the effects of water pressure when diving deep underwater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.92)
- In addition, to actually dive, it is essential to use Water Breathing in order to be able to breathe underwater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.92)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If you cast this spell, you can fight underwater without any penalties at all.
```
