# Central Region (Language = Mārdan/Eremire Gundar/Saine)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The languages in this region are divided between East and West. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Eremire is known as the "Kingdom of Craftsmen." With a population of less than one million, craftsmen in this region have formed large guilds. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- This has led to the sharing and development of technology, resulting in the production of consistently high-quality goods. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Having just experienced civil unrest, its national power is somewhat weakened. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- It is an agricultural nation focused on farming, livestock, and forestry. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Nevertheless, the country is preparing for war. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Saine, known as the "Kingdom of Lakesides," has a population of around 700,000. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- This is due to increased activity from their neighbor Lomar and deteriorating relations with Orphan after the nobles who led the civil war sought exile in the Kingdom of Orphan. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- North of the Kingdom of Eremire lies the Khan Desert, known as the "Desert of Malice." Despite being a desert, the Khan Desert is home to creatures adapted to the dry climate and nomadic peoples. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- These assassins, skilled in spirit magic abilities, are greatly feared. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- They are known for their extreme secrecy and rarely reveal their true nature or beliefs (they appear to worship their own deities). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- They have been known to send assassins after those who try to forcefully uncover their secrets or who accidentally witness important rituals. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
