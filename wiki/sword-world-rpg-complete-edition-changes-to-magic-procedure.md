# Changes to Magic Procedure

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- You can unify the rules of magic, allowing for a more integrated approach to casting. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- However, in this case, the master's work becomes quite complicated. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- If they fail, the magic will be cast with its full effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- Using that as the target score, everyone on the side on which it was cast must attempt a mental power resistance roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- In other words, you roll 2D and add it to your magic power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- The magic procedure is resolved the same way regardless of whether a monster or an adventurer is casting the spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
