## Statements

- This spell is not a spell that creates golems. It's only a spell that gives commands to a golem. Moreover, the target golem must be in a state where no commands have been given. This spell is ineffective on a golem that's already following a command. Golems in a state without commands are extremely rarely found, but may be found in the ruins of the ancient kingdom. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02656))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 9th Level Ancient Magic List [ Command Golem ] / [ Control Weather ]

- This is a spell that can change the weather within its area of effect. Weather changes begin on the round you cast the spell, and the spell is completed in 10 rounds. Even if someone is within the area of effect, they cannot resist this spell. This spell is non-removable , but if two Control Weather spells are cast within the same area of effect, the one with the higher final score will take effect, and the one with the lower final score will be negated. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02666))_
- Weather you can change to includes sunny, cloudy, rainy, foggy, or snowy. It has nothing to do with the weather, season, etc. before the spell was cast. As the spell ends, the weather will return to its natural state. However, effects caused by this spell will remain and cannot be removed. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02667))_
- Below is a brief description of the extent of the weather. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02668))_
- Sunny ： The sky within the area of effect will be completely clear of clouds. Depending on the angle of the sun, the sun will shine during the day and the ground will be warm. At night, you'll be able to see the moon and stars. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02669))_
- Cloudy ： The sky within the area of effect is covered by clouds, hiding the sun. Since direct sunlight is blocked, the area becomes darker and the temperature on the ground decreases. At night, the moon and stars will be hidden, making it a dark night. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02670))_
- Rainy ： Rain clouds cover the sky, the conditions will be the same as cloudy. In addition, rain will begin to fall at about 10 mm per hour, wetting the ground. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02671))_
- Foggy ： Visibility within the area of effect is 5 meters or less for fog. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02672))_
- Snowy ： Snow clouds cover the sky, the conditions will be the same as cloudy. In addition, snow will begin to fall at about 10 mm per hour. The surrounding temperature will drop to 0°celsius. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02673))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 9th Level Ancient Magic List [ Command Golem ] / [ Disintegrate ]

- Targets who have this spell cast on them and fail to resist will, after a moment of brightness, be broken down into the smallest unit of matter they were composed of, literally disappearing. It's impossible to regenerate a target that has become like this. If it was a living thing, it can never be revived again, and cannot be revived by Resurrection . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02684))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 9th Level Ancient Magic List [ Command Golem ] / [ Petrification

- Those who fail to resist this spell, no matter what they're made of, will instantly be turned to stone. Only creatures with natural life (humans, plants, animals, mythical beasts, magical beasts, fae, youma, and daemons) can be targets of this spell, with the exception of magical creatures such as spirits, undead, and golems. A petrified target will cease all life activity until they're released, so they will not age. If it's removed with Dispel Magic , etc., they will be revived in the same state they were before being petrified. However, if a petrified statue is destroyed in the meantime, it will be revived while broken apart at the time the spell is removed, so it will naturally die instantly (or suffer wounds or irreversible injury). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02695))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 9th Level Ancient Magic List [ Command Golem ] / [ Rune Shield ]

- This is a spell that creates a magical barrier that glows white around the caster. This barrier has the effect of completely blocking magic from others. You can attempt to remove it with Dispel Magic , but all other possible spells will be blocked by this barrier, and cannot have any effect. However, it cannot stop _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02706))_
- attacks other than magic. During the duration of this spell, the caster can still use other spells, but any spells that also affect the caster will be blocked by the Rune Shield and nullified just the same. Any spell effects that were applied to the caster will also be lost. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02708))_
