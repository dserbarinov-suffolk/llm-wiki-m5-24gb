- This spell has the effect of instilling fear in the target. An opponent who suffers the effect of this spell will feel an immeasurable fear towards the caster. For those affected, please determine the specific effect according to Table 5-5: Fears. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03079))_
