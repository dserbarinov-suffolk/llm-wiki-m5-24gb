- The Warden describes the current point or region on the map and how the path, weather, terrain, or party status might affect travel speed . The party plots or adjusts a given course towards their destination. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00349))_
- Each party member chooses a single Wilderness Action . The Warden narrates the results and then rolls on the Wilderness Events table. The party responds to the results. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00350))_
- The players and the Warden record any loss of resources and new conditions (i.e. torch use, deprivation , etc), and the cycle repeats. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00351))_
