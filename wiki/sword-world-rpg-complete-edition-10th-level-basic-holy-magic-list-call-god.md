# 10th Level Basic Holy Magic List [ Call God ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- However, the effect of the spell is temporary, and moreover, humans' frail bodies and minds cannot withstand the great power of a god, so after the spell's duration has expired, the caster's soul is destroyed, and even if you use spells such as Resurrection etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- you will not be able to bring them back to life again. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- Even gods who have lost contact with the material world will be able to use their full power if summoned with this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- Those that have had their mental power (points) taken away by other undead (wights, lesser vampires, etc.), those created by magic (skeleton, zombies, etc.), those created by their own will (haunts, undead kings, etc.), and there are also things such as ghouls whose causes are still unknown. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- There are several cases in which a normal life form becomes undead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- In either case, if you become undead, your soul will become abnormal and you will not be able to be resurrected even if Resurrection is used normally. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- This spell saves the souls of those who have become undead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- If the spell is successful, the undead will cease all activity at that point. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- If you then cast Resurrection , it is also possible to restore their life. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- One priest can only attempt one Save Soul against a given undead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.104)
- At that stage they will be able to use all their previous memories and skills. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- The soul of the target of this spell will be reborn as a baby in another location within one week. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- Reborn babies have their ability scores preserved (they have the same ability scores after becoming adults). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- When they remember their previous personality depends on the final score from when the spell was cast (see Table 5-8: Reincarnation Memory). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- This spell is cast immediately after an individual's death. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- You can help them recover their memory through constant supervision, so you can roll 2D once every year, and if you roll a 10 or greater, they will be able to regain their personality. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- When you cast this spell, the hand of the gods will help the caster and their companions escape to a safe location (the caster's home, a temple, a familiar city, etc.). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- By expanding the effect of this spell, you can simultaneously increase the area of effect as well as the number of people it targets. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- If you double the mental power consumed , it will target 20 people within a 14 meter radius, and if you triple it, it will target 30 people within a 17 meter radius. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- The caster can remove anything from the target of this spell, even within the area of effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
- Conversely, you cannot force this spell to take effect on those who do not agree to it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.105)
