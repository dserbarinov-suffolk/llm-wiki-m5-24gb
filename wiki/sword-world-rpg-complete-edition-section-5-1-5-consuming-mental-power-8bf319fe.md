## Statements

- Adventurers cannot use magic endlessly. When using magic, an adventurer must temporarily reduce their mental power. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01660))_
- Each magic has a score called base mental power cost . This base mental power cost, divided by the corresponding rune master skill level _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01661))_
- A character with shaman skill level 2 uses the Fire Bolt spell. The base mental power cost for Fire Bolt is 5 . The character must consume 5 ÷ 2 = 2.5, rounding up to 3 points of mental power. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01664))_
- A character can reduce their mental power to 0, but they cannot make it negative. For example, a character with 5 points of mental power remaining can use magic that costs 5 points of mental power, but cannot use magic that costs 6 or more points of mental power. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01665))_
- If a character's mental power reaches 0 , they will lose consciousness . The character cannot regain consciousness unless they recover their mental power to 1 point or more in some way. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01666))_
