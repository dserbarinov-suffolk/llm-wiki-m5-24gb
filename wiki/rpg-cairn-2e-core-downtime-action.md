# Downtime Actions

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about downtime actions:

## Statements

- The party takes the Make Camp action. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00618))_
- All actions are declared before being resolved. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00207))_
- A PC is limited to one Downtime Action at a time. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00363))_
- The characters then declare their intended actions. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00579))_
- The result of each side's actions occur simultaneously . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00200))_
- It is now the opponents' turn to attack or take an action. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00434))_
- Each character can choose one Wilderness Action per watch . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00302))_
- The results of a side's individual actions occur simultaneously. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00415))_
- Provide a character with a quest, opportunity, or plan of action. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00630))_
- The Warden can also create custom actions based on the needs of play. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00374))_
- A character cannot perform an action if it would put their safety at risk. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00363))_
- The players then declare their character's intended movements and actions . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00260))_
- The last watch of the day is typically reserved for the Make Camp action. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00334))_
- Remind them who has already moved, who has yet to take an action, and so on. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00564))_

## Technical atoms

> - If two characters need to take an action together, whoever is most at risk should save (usually the character with the lowest relevant Attribute).
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00171))_

> On their turn, a character may move up to 40ft and take up to one action.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00204))_
