# Rules Regarding Language

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In Alecrast, there are not many written languages, and there are quite a few languages that only have spoken words. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.157)
- The characters and intelligent monsters of Forcelia each have a means of communicating, that is, a language. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.157)
- There are not many races other than humans that have the habit of writing and reading. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.157)
- In Forcelia (as in our world), there are multiple language systems. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.157)
- Language includes spoken words and written words. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.157)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If you have a written language, you can not only have conversations with it, but also read it, but with a language that only has spoken words, you cannot read it, and can only have conversations with it.
```
