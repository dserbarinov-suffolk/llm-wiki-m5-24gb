# 12.2.3 Herbs

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Below is a list of herbs known in Forcelia . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Each has a provided rarity. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- If an adventurer is lucky enough to obtain these items and sell them, a merchant will buy them for half the prices provided here. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- The prices are for one day's worth for one adult. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- You basically have to take them every day to receive a bonus on illness progression/healing checks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Each herb has a base trade value . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This is a perennial plant that's often found in high mountains. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- The flowers can only be stored for 2 to 3 days. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- One flower is enough for one day for an adult. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Each plant has 2 to 3 flowers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- A large amount of bark can be removed from one tree and can also be stored. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- The bark of this tree is a type of poison that causes feelings of vomiting. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This is a tall tree that has strong fire spirit power and can be found in areas with high average temperatures. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- However, it can be dangerous if used too much. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This is a vine-like shrub that can be found in highlands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Its seeds are ground and mixed with oil to make an ointment that's used to treat skin diseases. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Seeds can be stored for long periods of time as long as they're in good condition. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- When used on illnesses that cause severe inflammation of the skin, such as green slime rot, it provides a +2 bonus on progression/healing checks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Dozens of seeds can be harvested from one plant, and 2 to 3 seeds are enough for one adult per day. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- One henruda plant is enough for one adult for one day. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This is an herb that grows in the wilderness and is the staple food of the cockatrice, a magical beast. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This herb is useful as a preventative against monsters with the ability to petrify and Petrification magic, but it's also a wonder drug for the dreaded stone skin . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This herb cannot be stored and if it's not used within three days of being collected, it will have no effect at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- One mandrake is enough for one adult to take for one week. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- If used against an illness that stimulates going berserk, you'll receive a +2 bonus on illness progression/healing checks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- The roots of young mandrake are prized as an ingredient in sedatives. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- Depending on the refining process, mandrake can also be used as an ingredient for aphrodisiacs, poisons, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.167)
- This is an amber stone that comes from the body of the mythical beast lynx. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.168)
- If you crush, brew, and drink this stone, you will receive a +4 bonus on progression/healing checks for illnesses that affect the mind (such as dance macabre). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.168)
