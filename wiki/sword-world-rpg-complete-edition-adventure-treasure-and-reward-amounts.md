# Adventure Treasure and Reward Amounts

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- However, the rewards obtained from an adventure should comply with the level of risk. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- What kind of rewards should be given for an adventure is ultimately up to the game master. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- The opposite can also result in players losing their motivation. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- To determine the value (upper limit) of the treasure that can be obtained in terms of silver coins, multiply the adventurer levels of all the player characters, then multiply the total by 500. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- Of course, depending on the contents of the scenario and the actions of the player characters, it's fine to introduce unexpected treasure, or to even have an adventure that results in a deficit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- Game masters should be aware of how much property and equipment the player characters have. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)

## Technical atoms

If you could obtain a large amount of treasure through an easy adventure, that would take the fun out of the game.
