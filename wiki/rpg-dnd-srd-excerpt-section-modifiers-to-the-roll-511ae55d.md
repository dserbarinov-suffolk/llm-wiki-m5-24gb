# Modifiers to the Roll

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- When a monster makes an attack roll, it uses whatever modifier is provided in its stat block. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00360))_
- Weapons that have the finesse or thrown property break this rule. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00361))_
- The ability modifier used for a melee weapon attack is Strength, and the ability modifier used for a ranged weapon attack is Dexterity. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00361))_
- Some spells also require an attack roll. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00362))_
- The ability modifier used for a spell attack depends on the spellcasting ability of the spellcaster. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00362))_
- You add your proficiency bonus to your attack roll when you attack using a weapon with which you have proficiency, as well as when you attack with a spell. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00363))_

## Technical atoms

> When a character makes an attack roll, the two most common modifiers to the roll are an ability modifier and the character's proficiency bonus.
