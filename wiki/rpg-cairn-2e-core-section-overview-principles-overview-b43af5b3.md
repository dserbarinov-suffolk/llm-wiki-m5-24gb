## Statements by subsection

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Neutrality

- The Warden's role is to act as a neutral arbiter and portray the rules, situations, non-player characters (NPCs), and narrative clearly. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00009))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Classless

- A character's role or skills are not limited by a single class. Instead, the equipment they carry and their experiences define their specialty. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00011))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Death

- Characters may be powerful, but they are also vulnerable to harm in its many forms. Death is always around the corner, but it is never random or without warning. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00013))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Fiction First

- Dice do not always reflect an obstacle's difficulty or its outcome. Instead, success and failure are based on in-world elements and arbitrated by the Warden in dialogue with the players. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00015))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Player Choice

- Players should always understand the reasons behind the choices they've made, and information about potential risks should be provided freely and frequently. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00019))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Shared Objectives

- Players trust one another to engage with the shared setting, character goals, and party challenges. Therefore the party is typically working together towards a common goal, as a team. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00023))_
