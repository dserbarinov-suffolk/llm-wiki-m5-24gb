## Statements

- Area=One Duration=18 rounds Effect=Binds the target with invisible rope Type=Target score Expansion=Final score, duration, distance, targets Resist=Negated effect This spell creates an invisible magical rope, with the effect of binding the target. If the target fails to resist, they will be bound by this rope and become completely unable to move. The poor victim will be unable to take any actions. However, since they can still move their mouth, they can speak and use holy magic. Not even an ogre can break this magical rope. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02497))_
- Note that if the target succeeds on a resistance roll against this spell, it'll have no effect. When making a check, you may only make a resistance roll against this spell once per round in which the spell is cast. At this time, the result lasts for the duration of the spell. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02498))_
- This spell works on targets slightly larger than a human (such as an ogre or a manticore), but has no effect on targets of a larger size than that (such as a dragon or a roc). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02499))_

### [ Rune Rope ] / 7th Level Ancient Magic List [ Dispel Order ]

- This spell nullifies the effects of the types of spells that give commands, such as Quest , Geas , etc. It also removes puppet and golem-type magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02509))_
- This spell can also cancel commands given to a golem. Once a golem has had its commands canceled, it becomes neutral and can be given new commands using the 9th level spell Command Golem . However, the final score of commands given during the ancient kingdom period is very high, so you'll need a final score of at least 30 or greater to cancel a golem's commands. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02510))_
- Once this spell fails, you cannot attempt it again until your magic power increases. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02511))_
