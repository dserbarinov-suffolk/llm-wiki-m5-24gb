- This is a spell that regenerates missing or non-functioning body tissues and restores their function. This spell is also effective in restoring vision and hearing. One week is required for regeneration and functional recovery. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03560))_

- Braki Mole, woodpecker, squirrel, small bird (they have the characteristic of nest-building) _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03578))_
