- Treat it as a vegetation wall. Without expansion, defense points and destruction points are both 15. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10691))_
- Spirit Wall: Dryad has the power to regenerate even if it's destroyed. Each round, Spirit Wall: Dryad regenerates 1 defense point and 1 destruction point. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10692))_
