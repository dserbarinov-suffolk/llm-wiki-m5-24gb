# [ Firebolt ] (Salamander/Fire Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This magical fire will always hit, and the target will suffer strike power 10 damage (strike power 0 and no criticals if they successfully resist). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.85)
- A line of fire up to 10 meters will reach out from that fire and catch the target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.85)
- To use this spell, there must be a fire (or a controlled salamander) large enough to burn a torch within at least 10 meters around the caster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.85)
