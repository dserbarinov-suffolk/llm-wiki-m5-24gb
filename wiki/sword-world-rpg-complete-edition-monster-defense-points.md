# Monster Defense Points

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Monsters can repel and hold off attacks with their thick skins and hard scales. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- This indicates the amount of damage a monster can reduce. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Please note that a monster's defense points are treated differently from a character's defense power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- A goblin has 4 defense points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- The goblin can prevent 4 points of damage with its skin. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
