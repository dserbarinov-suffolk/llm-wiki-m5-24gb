# [ Berserk ] (Spell Level=3)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Even the caster is not safe from the target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147-148)
- Also, if no one is around, they will wound themselves. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147-148)
- They also become indifferent to wounds and pain and ignore the risks of injury or life. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147-148)
- Base Mental Power Cost=15 Distance=Touch Area=One Duration=18 rounds Effect= Make the target go berserk Type=Mind Expansion=Final score, duration Resist=Negated effect Those who have this spell cast on them and fail to resist will go berserk and feel an urge to destroy everything nearby. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147-148)
- As a result, they will destroy property within sight and blindly attack creatures, regardless of friend or foe. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147-148)
- At this time, their attack power and bonus damage will be +2, and their evasion speed will be -4. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147-148)
- While under the influence of this spell, you will not fall unconscious even if your life force (points) drops to 0 or less. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.148)
- However, a death check must be made. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.148)
