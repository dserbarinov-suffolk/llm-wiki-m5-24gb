# Variant: Skills with Different Abilities

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- In such cases, the GM might ask for a check using an unusual combination of ability and skill, or you might ask your GM if you can apply a proficiency to a different check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00109))_
- Similarly, when your half-orc barbarian uses a display of raw strength to intimidate an enemy, your GM might ask for a Strength (Intimidation) check, even though Intimidation is normally associated with Charisma. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00109))_
- In some situations, though, your proficiency might reasonably apply to a different kind of check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00109))_
- In this case, your GM might allow you to apply your proficiency in Athletics and ask for a Constitution (Athletics) check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00109))_
- Normally, your proficiency in a skill applies only to a specific kind of ability check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00109))_
- Proficiency in Athletics, for example, usually applies to Strength checks. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00109))_
