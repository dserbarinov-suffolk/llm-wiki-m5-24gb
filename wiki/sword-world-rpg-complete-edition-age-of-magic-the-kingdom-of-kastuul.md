# The Age of Magic = The Kingdom of Kastuul

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Though many ancient races died in the Final War and lost their power, some races managed to survive. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- Among the early humans, there were even some who were said to be half-divine. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- Humans excelled in all forms of magic, reportedly because they inherited not only physical form but also a portion of the gods' power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- As this kingdom has already perished, it is now known as the Ancient Kingdom. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- They survived through a dark age lasting hundreds or even thousands of years, eventually establishing a kingdom. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- This was the Kingdom of Kastuul. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- Humans with low magical ability, or those completely lacking it, were either treated as slaves or banished from the peaceful kingdom to become savages living in dangerous wilderness. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
- This era is known as "The Age of Magic." It is said that three systems of magic - ancient magic, spirit magic, and holy magic - created a brilliant culture, enabled cities to float in the sky, and even commanded grown dragons as servants. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.281)
