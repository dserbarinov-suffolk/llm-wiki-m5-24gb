# Adventurer Death Checks

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Any adventurer who falls to 0 or negative life force is now at risk of dying . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- The baseline score is life force resistance (adventurer level + life force bonus) and the target score is 7 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- This check is made using a life force resistance roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- If the character's life force is negative , the negative amount will be used as a penalty . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- life force resistance + 2D -(damage applied beyond life force) < 7 → death. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- His life force resistance is 5 (adventurer level 2, life force bonus +3). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- Since Ducard II has a life force of -4 , that will also be the penalty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- His result after rolling 2D is 7 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- If the result of this death check had been 6 or lower, Ducard II would have met an untimely end . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
- 5 (life force resistance) + 7 ( roll ) - 4 (penalty) = 8 , which is greater or equal than 7. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.50)
