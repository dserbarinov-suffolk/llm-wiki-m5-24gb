# Required Strength

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Even swords of the same shape are sold in a variety of sizes to match the physical strength of the person using it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- For an adventurer, the first step to survival is to choose a weapon most suited to their strength. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- Basically, the most suitable weapon is one whose required strength equals the character's strength . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- Therefore, it's a good idea to choose one based on the image of your character. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- As long as you choose that, you are guaranteed the same attack capability, even if you use a different type of weapon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- Think of a sword, ax, or spear with a required strength of 14 as the same in terms of game value. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- However, most weapons have a limited range of required strength. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
