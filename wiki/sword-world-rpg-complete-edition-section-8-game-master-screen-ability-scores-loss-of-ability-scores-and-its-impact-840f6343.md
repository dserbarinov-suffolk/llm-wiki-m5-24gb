## Statements

- While adventuring, adventurers often suffer physical and mental injuries and fatigue. In Sword World RPG , this is represented by a loss of life force and mental power (point reduction). If either life force or mental power fall to 0 or less, the adventurer will fall unconscious and be unable to act. The four other ability scores of dexterity, agility, intelligence, and strength are rarely reduced, but if this does happen and these ability scores do fall to 0 or less, the adventurer will still become disabled. At this time, whether they're conscious or not depends on the situation. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00152))_
- If life force and mental power are reduced by wounds or exhaustion, they will recover once the wounds are treated or the exhaustion is wiped away. There are various conditions for these reductions and recoveries, which you will discover as you read through this book. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00153))_
- An ability score value is related to the success or failure of an action that an adventurer tries to perform, but in Sword World RPG , success or failure is determined by a bonus derived from the ability score, rather than the ability score itself. The result of each ability score divided by 6 (rounded down) will be the bonus for that ability score (Table 1-1: Ability Bonuses). Each of these numbers is referred to as a dexterity bonus , intelligence bonus , etc., using the name of the ability score. For example, it would say something like an adventurer with 14 dexterity has a dexterity bonus of +2 (of course, the bonus is always 0 or a positive number, but by nature it's usually written ±0, +1, +2...... etc.) When a character's ability score increases or decreases, even temporarily, the ability bonus increases or decreases accordingly. However, temporary reductions do not affect modifiers for only two scores: life force and mental power. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00155))_
- Humans are not the only adventurers in Forcelia. Next, we'll explain the races that are relatively common as adventurers in Forcelia, though they're not as common as humans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00164))_
- Players are free to choose from humans or any of these races to be their alter ego, an adventurer. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00165))_
- Dwarves were once members of the fae tribe who lived in the fairy world, but now they have fully immersed into the world inhabited by humans (which we call the material world) and no longer have the ability to travel to and from the fairy world. Their lives are no different from those of humans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00167))_
- They are about 120 cm tall, round and plump, and have bulbous noses. Men often have long beards on their chins, while women do not. Their hair ranges from brown to dark brown and their eyes are a similar color. Their skin color is dark, almost brown. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00168))_
- They can see things in the dark as well as they can in daylight. They are also very dexterous with their hands, and excel in such techniques as craftsmanship and blacksmithing. Many of their works are stunning and cannot be imitated by humans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00169))_
- Dwarves usually live in human cities and villages using their own skills, but there are several cities and villages where only dwarves live. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00170))_
- They have a very strong sense of tribalism, respecting the rules of their tribe and seldom violating them. They mainly worship the gods of the earth, with Braki as their main deity, who is also the god of smithing. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00171))_
- The life span of a dwarf is longer than that of a human, thought to be around 200 years. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00172))_
