# Magical Weapons and Armor

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In Forcelia, there are magical weapons and armor that were once created during the age of the magical kingdom (Kastuul). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- These magical weapons and armor are seldom found, and are usually rarely traded. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Characters may obtain these magical weapons and armor through their adventures. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- In Forcelia, the limit for this power is +3 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- There are only a scant few weapons and armor in the world with +3 power, and any with +4 or more have yet to be found. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- If +2, those bonuses are +2, and if +3, they're +3. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Magical weapons have exceptional attack power and bonus damage . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- If you use a +1 magical weapon, you receive a +1 bonus to attack power and bonus damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Magical shields increase evasion speed . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- A +1 magic shield grants +1 evasion speed , +2 if +2. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Magical armor increases evasion speed and damage reduction . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Magical weapons and armor are not usually traded, but if you are very lucky you may be able to buy them if they're +1. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- A guide to prices can be found in Chapter 14: Treasure (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Also, they offer basically no bonus to strike power or defense power, same as required strength. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- High-quality ones (made with lower required strength) seem to exist only rarely . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
