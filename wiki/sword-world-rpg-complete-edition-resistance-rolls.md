# Resistance Rolls

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- A success roll made using adventurer level + life force bonus as the baseline score is specifically referred to as a life force resistance roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Similarly, a success roll made using adventurer level + mental power bonus as the baseline score is specifically called a mental power resistance roll , and the two are collectively called simply resistance rolls . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- The reason for the name change and the distinction between the two in particular, is that they are frequently performed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Life force resistance rolls are made to avoid or reduce the effects of physical dangers such as poison, while mental power resistance rolls are made to avoid or reduce the effects of magic or curses. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Our 2D roll is 5, so with 5+5= 10 , he escapes paralysis, if barely. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Since Ducard II has an adventurer level of 2 and a +3 life force bonus, the baseline score of his life force resistance roll (life force resistance power) is 5 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Paralyzing poison is a special ability of ghouls. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- The toxicity score of ghoul poison is 10, which is the target score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- While touring an underground cave on an adventure, he wanders into a mysterious room where all four walls are painted in extreme colors. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Since Ducard II has an adventurer level of 2 and a +2 mental power bonus, the baseline score of his mental power resistance roll (mental power resistance power) is 4 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- The game master asks us to make a mental power resistance roll against target score 17. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- Normally, this would never succeed, but our 2D roll is 12 !. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- In fact, there is a magic trap set in this room that will put anyone who enters it into a deep sleep. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
- When a character with adventurer level 0 (a character with no adventurer skills) makes these checks, the baseline score is 0 regardless of their ability scores. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.33)
