# [ ⑥ Treasure Appraisal [Intelligence] ] (Not retriable)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- For standard ancient kingdom treasure-- many of which are magical items --the target score is around 15. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- However, if the difference from the target score is 6 or greater, there is a possibility of this kind of misunderstanding. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- The rarity of that treasure becomes the target score for a success roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- This ability is used to know the value and utility of treasure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- If the true value is 100 gamels, you will not think that it's worth 10,000 gamels just because you failed the check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- Basically, these connoisseurs and specialty shops will only exist in large towns. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
