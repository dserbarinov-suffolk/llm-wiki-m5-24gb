- This spell creates a magical barrier around the target's body, reducing the damage the target suffers by 1 point. This spell takes effect against anything that deals direct damage, whether it's a physical attack or a magical attack. However, it has no effect at all on spells that are offensive but do not deal damage to life force. It also cannot prevent damage dealt to mental power _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01920))_
