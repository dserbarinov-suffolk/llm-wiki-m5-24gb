# Action Canceling

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- If you fall down yourself early in the order, you will not be able to act. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- For example, if an enemy that you intended to attack falls down due to the attack of a fellow player, no further attack is necessary. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- A character may cancel and not perform their announced action. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- In such a case, a character may cancel their intended action. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- However, characters cannot switch their actions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- You also cannot switch from magic to a weapon attack because you intended to cast a spell but were blocked from chanting halfway through. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- The choice is basically this: either continue with the announced action or cancel it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
Just because a companion has knocked down an enemy in front of you does not mean that you can attack a different enemy.
```
