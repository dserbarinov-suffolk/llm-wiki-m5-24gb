# Moving Around Other Creatures

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- You can move through a nonhostile creature's space. In contrast, you can move through a hostile creature's space only if the creature is at least two sizes larger or smaller than you. Remember that another creature's space is difficult terrain for you. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00308))_
- Whether a creature is a friend or an enemy, you can't willingly end your move in its space. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00309))_
- If you leave a hostile creature's reach during your move, you provoke an opportunity attack, as explained later in the chapter. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00310))_
- In contrast, you can move through a hostile creature's space only if the creature is at least two sizes larger or smaller than you. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00308))_
