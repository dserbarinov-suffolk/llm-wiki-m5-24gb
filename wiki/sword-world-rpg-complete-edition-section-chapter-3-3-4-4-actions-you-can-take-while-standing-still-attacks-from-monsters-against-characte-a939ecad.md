- In Sword World , all those who use magic utilizing runes are called rune masters . Rune masters are divided into five types according to the type of magic (runes) they use. Namely, _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01493))_
- Sorcerer uses ancient magic Shaman uses spirit magic Priest uses holy magic Dark priest uses dark magic _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01494))_
- and dragon priest uses dragon roar magic . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01495))_
- Each corresponds to the skill for which it is named. Those with the sorcerer skill can use ancient magic and are called sorcerers . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01496))_
- Some rune masters are fluent in multiple runes. However, this also has some limitations. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01497))_
- One, is that some races may not be able to learn some or all of the runes. These are as explained earlier under 1.2: Races (see p. 13) _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01498))_
- The other, is that you cannot have more than one each of holy, dark, and dragon roar magic at the same time. These types of magic are obtained through belief in gods , wicked gods , and dragons , respectively.
