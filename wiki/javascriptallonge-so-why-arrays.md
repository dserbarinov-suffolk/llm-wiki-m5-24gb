## Key supported claims

- Arrays allow direct access to arbitrary items, unlike linked lists which require iteration. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.131-131)
- Pointer chasing in linked lists is slower due to extra fetches and cache misses. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.131-131)
- Modifying elements in linked lists affects both new and old lists, unlike arrays. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.131-131)
