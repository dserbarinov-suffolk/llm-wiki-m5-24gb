## Statements

- It's a white gas. Those who inhale this and fail to resist will have their entire body stiffen and become like a statue. Victims retain their will and senses, but are unable to move a single finger. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05642))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [White Marble (Inhaled/Magic)] / [Moonlight Drone (Dose/Magic)]

- This is a small granular poison, and if someone drinks it and fails to resist, they will become a woman if they're a man, and a man if they're a woman. There are no other penalties. Additionally, there are no changes to the magic that shamans can use. If you drink it again, you can return to normal. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05651))_
