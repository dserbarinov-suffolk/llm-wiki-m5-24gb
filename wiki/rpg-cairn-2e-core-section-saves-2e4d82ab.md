# Saves

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- - A save is a roll to avoid negative outcomes from risky choices. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00174))_
- Characters roll a d20 and compare the results to the appropriate attribute. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00174))_
- A 1 is always a success, and a 20 is always a failure. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00174))_
- If they roll equal to or under that attribute, they succeed. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00174))_
- - If two opponents are each trying to overcome the other, whoever is most at risk should save. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00175))_
- - If two characters need to take an action together, whoever is most at risk should save (usually the character with the lowest relevant Attribute). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00176))_
