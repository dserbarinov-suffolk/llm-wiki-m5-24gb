## Statements

- Magic that targets a weapon and enhances it. Two or more of this kind of magic cannot affect the same weapon at the same time. Please resolve them as counters (overpowers) magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01739))_
- Weapons that have been given power by ancient magic, silver weapons, or mithril silver weapons are assumed to already have non-removable (see below) weapon enhancement magic cast on them. Therefore, that power cannot be nullified, so even if you cast new weapon enhancement magic, it'll be useless . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01740))_
