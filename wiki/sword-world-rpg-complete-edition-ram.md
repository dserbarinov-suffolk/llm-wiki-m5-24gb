# Ram

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The attacker must make a success roll using fi ghter skill level + strength bonus as the baseline score, while the defender must make one using either adventurer level + strength bonus or adventurer level + life force bonus ( life point resistance -7 for monsters), whichever is greater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266-267)
- This can only be performed with the fi ghter skill , it cannot be used with the thief skill . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266-267)
- If your attack hits, make a strike power 0 and critical target 12 damage check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266-267)
- If the attacker's final score is greater, the opponent will fall prone . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266-267)
- If you use this optional rule, then if successfully hit by a monster whose attack method is ram , a character must determine whether he or she has fallen prone . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.267)
