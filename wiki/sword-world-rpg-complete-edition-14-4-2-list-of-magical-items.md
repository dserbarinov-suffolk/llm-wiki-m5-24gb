- For armor, items that exceed the required strength often see decreased demand, so higher required strength doesn't necessarily mean higher prices. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.246)
- This trend is particularly noticeable in non-metallic or ring mail armor preferred by thieves and rangers, where armor with high required strength tends to be avoided. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.246)
