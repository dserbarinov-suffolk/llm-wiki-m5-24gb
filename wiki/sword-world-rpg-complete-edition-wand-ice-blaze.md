# Wand Ice Blaze

What [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]] covers about wand ice blaze:

## Statements

### 《 Staves and Wands 》 [ Ice Blaze Wand ] / Ice Blaze Wand

- When you wave this wand while chanting a password, countless small blades of ice will fly in a straight line. These blades of ice deal damage to all targets in their path. Area of effect is 20m, strike power is 20, magic power is 6. This wand can only be used 1D+1 times. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09468))_
