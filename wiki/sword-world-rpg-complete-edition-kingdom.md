# Kingdoms

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Forcelia consists of one continent called Alecrast and several large islands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.283)
- The continent and islands are further divided into dozens of countries, each with its own system of governance. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.283)
- Each country has its own distinct character and form of government. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.283)
- However, most nations' typical governance structure consists of a king at the top, with nobles and knights forming the ruling class and common citizens below them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.283)
- While some countries have a serf class below this, such cases are rather rare. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.283)
