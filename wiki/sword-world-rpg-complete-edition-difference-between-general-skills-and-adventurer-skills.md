# The Difference Between General Skills and Adventurer Skills

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The details will be explained later, but the abilities of the two actually overlap quite a bit, and a good ranger can also become a good hunter. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
- Of course, the opposite is also true. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
- This is because the hunter skill and the ranger skill are completely different things. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
- The reason for this is that the hunter skill is a general skill, while the ranger skill is, as precisely called, an adventurer skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
- However, the biggest difference between the two is whether or not you can become a protagonist-- called a hero -who appears in fantasy novels, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
- General skills, so to speak, can be said to be the skills necessary for daily life. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
- Of course, adventurer skills are the necessary skills to live as an adventurer. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.125)
