From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- When you make a ranged attack, you fire a bow or a crossbow, hurl a handaxe, or otherwise send projectiles to strike a foe at a distance. A monster might shoot spines from its tail. Many spells also involve making a ranged attack. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00373))_
