# Guilds

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- First, there are professional associations called "guilds," and anyone considered an artisan must belong to one. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- Without membership, one cannot learn trade-specific skills, nor are they permitted to conduct business using such skills. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- While guilds are typically national organizations, they may sometimes be recognized in neighboring countries. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- While the relationship between a master and their apprentices is very close, interaction between different masters is not particularly active due to fears of technical knowledge being leaked. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
