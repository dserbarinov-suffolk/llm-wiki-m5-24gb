## Statements

- There are six variations of this spell, each of which increase or decrease a target's dexterity, agility, or strength by 6. However, even if you reduce them, they can never become 0. If the ability score was originally 6 or less, it'll instead become 1. In most cases, this will result in an increase or decrease in your ability bonus by 1 point. If you use it on a youma, etc. which is not represented by ability scores, all you have to do is increase or decrease its attack points (dexterity), evasion points (agility), or strike points (strength) by 1 point. Also, in the case of agility, action order will also increase (decrease) by 6. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02158))_
- The target is free to attempt to resist this spell or not. If it's used for a decreasing effect, they'll probably want to resist it. Targets who successfully resist will not receive any effect. Physical Enchant cannot expand its effect (that is, increase or decrease ability scores by 12 points, 18 points, etc.). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02159))_
- Physical Enchant is subdivided into six spells by their effects. They are called Sharpness, Dullness, Quickness, Slowness, Strength, and Weakness, respectively. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02160))_
- Increases the target's agility by 6. Affects evasion speed (points) and action order. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02166))_
- Also increases the baseline score of related abilities. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02167))_
- Reduces the target's agility by 6. Affects evasion speed (points) and action order. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02169))_
- Also decreases the baseline score of related abilities. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02170))_
- Reduces the target's strength by 6. Affects bonus damage (strike points). If, due to this spell being cast, the required strength of your weapon or armor exceeds your own strength, you will be disabled. However, if you're using the optional rules under 16.8: Weapons and Armor That Are Too Heavy (see p. 268), you can still act, but you'll receive a penalty to attack power, evasion speed, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02174))_
