# 5th Level Basic Dark Magic List

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- The curse will be lifted when you obtain the item or complete the action. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- An example of a curse is making a beautiful face ugly or changing the color of one's skin. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- However, the higher the caster's level, or if some kind of price is paid at the same time as the spell, the more terrifying the effect will be. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- Game masters should limit the effects of curses so that they do not immediately kill the individual. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- This spell is used on an individual to impose penalties that are not directly life-threatening. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- This key is usually unknown to the target of the curse. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- Base Mental Power Cost=25 Distance=10 meters Area=One corpse Duration=Permanent Effect=Resurrect the deceased as a zombie Type=Non-removable, ritual Expansion=Distance, targets, target volume Resist=None. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- The corpse can be resurrected as a zombie and used as a slave. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
- To cast this spell, you need a corpse that is within a few days of death. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.144)
