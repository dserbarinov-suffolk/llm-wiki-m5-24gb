- This spell has the effect of instilling fear in the target. An opponent who suffers the effect of this spell will feel an immeasurable fear towards the caster. For those affected, please determine the specific effect according to Table 5-5: Fears. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02988))_
- This spell's effect can be expanded. If expanded, make an equivalent number of checks on the Fears table, then apply which result the caster finds most desirable. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02989))_
