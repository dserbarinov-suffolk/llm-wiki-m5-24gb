## Key supported claims

- Maximus and Maximianus were one and the same person, or no such person as Maximianus ever reigned in Britain. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- An ancient scholiast adds that Guasmoric was built near Lugubalia [Carlisle], now called Palmecaster. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- The Bass is not a river but an isolated rock in the Frith of Forth, near North Berwick. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- See also Nennius, sec.47. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- According to Gunn, a small island in the duchy of Sleswick in Denmark, now called Angel, of which Flensburg is the metropolis. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
