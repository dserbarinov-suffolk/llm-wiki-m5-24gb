# [ Sink ] (Undine/Water Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Effect=Eliminates the buoyancy of water, causing floating people and objects to sink Type=Counters (Negates): Water Walking Countered (Negated) By: Water Walking Countered (Overpowered) By: Create Ghost Ship. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.88)
- Those who've had this spell cast on them lose their liquid buoyancy and sink completely to the bottom. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.88)
- If you cast this spell on something under the influence of Water Walking , their effects will negate each other, removing the effects of both spells. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.88)
