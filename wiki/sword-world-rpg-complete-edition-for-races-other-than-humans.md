# For Races Other Than Humans

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- For races other than human (except for half-elves raised by humans), the starting skills and experience points are determined by race (Table 1-6: Starting Experience by Race). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- Since the character we're creating is a human, we'll use the backgrounds table. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- The character ended up coming from the mercenary background. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- Therefore, we'll have 1 level in the fighter skill as our starting skill, and 2500 starting experience points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- In addition, money is listed as 2Dx200. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- The result of our next 2D roll is 6 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- This means we roll 2D and have money (in gamels ) equal to the result times 200. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
- The character has 1200 gamels. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.19)
