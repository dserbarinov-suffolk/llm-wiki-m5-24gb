# how this book is organized

Source: [[javascriptallonge]]

## Statements

- Code examples within each chapter are small and emphasize exposition rather than serving as patterns for everyday use. (javascriptallonge.pdf p.11)
- Following some of the chapters are a series of recipes designed to show the application of the chapter's ideas in practical form. (javascriptallonge.pdf p.12)
- While the content of each chapter builds naturally on what was discussed in the previous chapter, the recipes may draw upon any aspect of the JavaScript programming language. (javascriptallonge.pdf p.12)
