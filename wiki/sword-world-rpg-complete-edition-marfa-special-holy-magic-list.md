# Marfa Special Holy Magic List

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- This spell has no effect on those without a mind, or when it is natural for them to attack another due to hunger, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- Those who fail to resist will lose their hostility and attempt to stop the fight. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- Therefore, priests of Marfa can use this spell to take away the feelings of hatred that have arisen in each other's hearts and prevent them from fighting. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
