# Should You Use a Target Score Check or a Difficulty Check?

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- There are two types of success rolls: those that use a target score for the check and those that use a difficulty for the check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- The game master must decide which of these should be used to make the success roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- For example, if you want to jump over a cliff, success or failure is clear. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- If you succeed , you can jump over it, and if you fail, you will fall. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- Checks for actions with clear successes or failures are made using target scores . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- An example of something where success or failure cannot be determined is finding a trap. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- Even though it looks like it's a success, the monster may actually be pretending to be asleep. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- When a trap is not found, a character has no way of knowing whether there really are no traps or if it's because they couldn't find it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- If you can fail on a success roll but still have it appear to be a success, then the success roll should be made as a difficulty check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.135)
- However, the difficulty should be made as clear as possible. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.136)
- When a player makes a success roll, they use the baseline score (skill level + ability bonus) and the final score attained from the dice roll to infer the success or failure of the action. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.136)
- During a difficulty check, it is up to the game master to decide whether or not to reveal the difficulty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.136)
- At this time, if you don't know the difficulty, it'll be difficult to guess. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.136)
- In this case, the final score of his success roll is 13. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.136)
- However, this is not possible without knowing the difficulty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.136)
