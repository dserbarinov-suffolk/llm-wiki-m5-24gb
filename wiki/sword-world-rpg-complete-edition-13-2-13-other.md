# 13.2.13 Other

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Magic and skills cannot be used. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- A Lycanthrope is a monster with the ability to change from human to beast form. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- When in beast form , they cannot act intelligently, but rather follow their instincts and go on killing sprees. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- When the number of days is 16 or more, compare it with 30 - the number of days (the waxing and waning of the moon in Forcelia is one cycle every 30 days). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- Lycanthropes have the ability to summon and control other beasts of their kind in their surroundings (wolves for werewolves, bears for werebears, etc.). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- There may be some hiding their true identity and living among humans in towns and villages, but when their time comes, they're likely to take odd actions to hide their transformations. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- Lycanthropy is a type of infectious illness, and those who are wounded by a lycanthrope in beast form are at risk of becoming a lycanthrope of the same species. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- Monster Level=4 Rarity=8 Agility=16 Movement Speed =24 Number=One to several Frequency=Rare Intellect=Animal/Human Reaction=Adversarial Attack Points=Fangs:11 (4) Strike Points=12 Evasion Points=12 (5) Defense Points=8 Life Points/Resistance=20/13 (6) Mental Points/Resistance=14/12 (5) Special Abilities=Normal weapon immunity after shifting into wolf Infect (Intensity 7). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.232)
- A werewolf is a lycanthrope with the power to shapeshift into a wolf. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
