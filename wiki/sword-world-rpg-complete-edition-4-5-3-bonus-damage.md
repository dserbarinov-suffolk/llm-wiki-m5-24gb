# 4.5.3 Bonus Damage

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This will be the total amount of damage the character deals the monster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- Ducard II's bonus damage is 4 (fighter skill level 2, strength bonus 2). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- If base damage is 14, the total damage is 14+4= 18 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)
- No matter how much bonus damage you have , it means nothing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.45)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
Once you've determined base damage, you must then add your character's bonus damage.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When determining base damage (strike roll), if you suddenly roll double ones, the damage will always be 0 .
```
