## Key supported claims

- Functions capture the value of variables at the time they are created, not when they are called. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.155-157)
- The use of 'var' for loop variables can lead to unexpected behavior when used inside closures. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.155-157)
- The 'let' keyword provides a cleaner and more predictable alternative for loop variables. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.155-157)
