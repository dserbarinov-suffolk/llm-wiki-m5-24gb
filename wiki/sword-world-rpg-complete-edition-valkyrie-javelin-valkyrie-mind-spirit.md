# [ Valkyrie Javelin ] (Valkyrie/Mind Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Its strike power is 30. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.90)
- This javelin will always hit the target as long as they're within the caster's view, even if they're not in the line of fire. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.90)
- By casting this spell, the caster can strike the target with a javelin of light. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.90)
