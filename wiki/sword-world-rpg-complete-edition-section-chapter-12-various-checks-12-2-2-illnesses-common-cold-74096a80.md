## Statements

- This is what they call a cold. It's accompanied by a slight fever, cough, sneezing, runny nose, etc. Some people may experience diarrhea symptoms or joint pain. You suffer a -1 penalty on all success rolls while you have a cold. As the cold progresses, you may develop a high fever and also have difficulty breathing. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05576))_
- This briefly describes the conditions that appear in the illness. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05578))_
- This score is used when making a check to see if you know the illness. If an owner of the sage skill or healer skill succeeds on a success roll using skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score and rarity as the target score, they can look at a sick patient and identify which illness they have. This means not only knowing the name of the disease, but also its characteristics and how to deal with it. If a disease can be identified, owners of the healer skill can provide appropriate treatment. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05580))_
- After a certain period of time has elapsed after becoming ill, the illness either progresses further or gets better. The degree of progression of an illness is indicated by a score called depth . Immediately after contracting an illness, the depth is 1. After the time indicated by progression speed has elapsed since you contracted the illness, roll two dice and look at the result. If the roll is less than the progression intensity , the disease gets worse and the depth increases by 1. If the roll is greater than or equal to the progression intensity but less than the healing score , the illness neither worsens nor improves, and the depth remains the same. If the result is greater than or equal to the healing score , the illness will be cured and the depth will be reduced by 1. When the depth becomes 0, you are completely recovered. The patient's adventurer level and life force bonus have no influence on this check. Adventurer level may also be hero level, but even if you're a hero, you cannot win against illness . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05582))_
- Even if a shaman cannot use the spell Restore Health , they can still work with one type of spirit to alleviate symptoms. For example, by working with salamander, fever, which is the main symptom of a cold, can be alleviated. When a shaman performs such a treatment, they gain a +1 bonus on progression/healing checks. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05583))_
- In addition to these, depending on the illness, there are herbs and special medicines that can provide bonuses to progression/healing checks. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05584))_
- When the holy magic Cure Disease or Refresh , or the spirit magic Restore Health are cast, the caster must make a success roll using their magic power as the baseline score. The target score is double the healing score (added in parentheses). If you succeed, the depth of the illness is reduced by 1 and you can make an additional success roll. You can continue to make this check until you fail or until the illness is completely cured. If this does not completely cure the illness, it'll be useless to cast these spells on the same patient until the caster's magic power increases. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05585))_
- If a healer who knows the true nature of an illness cares for the patient for more than one hour per day, they can make a similar check using that healer's healer skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score. However, this must be at each progression speed of the illness (i.e. immediately after the illness's progression check), and you can only make one success roll. However, even if they fail, the same healer can continue to care for the patient. If after some time has passed, an illness progression check occurs, the healer can also make a healing check. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05586))_
- If, as an illness progresses, its depth reaches the fatal depth , the character will die from the illness. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05588))_
- Indicates the infectiousness of the illness. If it' s very strong , it has the infection power of a plague. Strong is about the same level as influenza. As in the example, moderate means it's just a cold, and weak means it's not very infectious. When it's very weak , it's not infectious and is unlikely to spread to other people. When an illness is very strong or strong , there is a high possibility that the infection will spread to the healer, etc. who is caring for the patient. If the game master determines that there is a possibility of contracting the illness, have the character make a progression check for the illness (compare a 2D roll with progression intensity). If they fail, the character has contracted the illness at depth 1. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05590))_
- A detailed description and data on the illness is included at the end. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05592))_
- Here are some examples of illnesses seen in Sword World . Most of the illnesses listed here are incurable diseases that are difficult to heal and have a high mortality rate. The game master may also create any other illnesses they desire. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05594))_
