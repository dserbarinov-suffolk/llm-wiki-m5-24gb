- No matter how far away the object is from the caster, the spell will still take effect. Also, the closer you get, the more you can feel its presence, so you can get a rough estimate of the distance. The caster must know the actual object being targeted by the spell well enough to specify it. ### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Location ] / 5th Level Ancient Magic List [ Apport ]

- This is a spell that allows the caster to teleport their possessions into the palm of their hand. However, even though it's their possession, the caster must have a complete memory of it, and moreover must be able to identify specifically where it's kept. Also, the size of the target is limited to something you can hold in your hand (for example, you can summon a sword, but not armor). If the duration expires or if the spell is canceled, the target immediately returns to its original location. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02285))_
