### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Decrease Weight ] Base Mental Power Cost=3 Distance=10 meters Area=One object Duration=18 rounds Effect=Reduce the weight of an object Type=Lost Counters (Negates): Increase Weight Countered (Negated) By: Increase Weight Expansion=Duration, distance, targets, effect (see description)

- This spell is used on a single object and has the effect of reducing the target's weight. If cast on a weapon or armor, it has the effect of reducing the target's required strength by -1 without changing its strike power or defense power. When used on other objects, it has the ability to reduce their weight by about 90%. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01923))_
- This spell's effect can be expanded. If you double or triple the amount of mental power you consume...... the required strength can become -2 or -3 ...... for weapons and armor. For other objects, it'll be reduced by 10% each time. When reducing the weight of an object, it's reduced by 90% each, the rate of double expansion would be 0.9 x 0.9 = 0.81 times . For triple , it' d be 0.9 x 0.9 x 0.9 = 0.721 times . To reduce the weight by half , you must expand it 7 times , and to reduce it to 1/10th , you must expand it 22 times . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01924))_
- Even if you reduce the required strength, the strike power or defense power of weapons and armor will not change. Also, in this case, required strength can only be reduced to 1. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01925))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Increase Weight ] Base Mental Power Cost=9 Distance=10 meters Area=One object Duration=18 rounds Effect=Increase the weight of an object Type=Lost Counters (Negates): Decrease Weight Countered (Negated) By: Decrease Weight

- This spell is used on a single object and has the ability to increase the target's weight. If cast on a weapon or armor, it has the effect of increasing required strength by +1 (strike power does not change). When used on other objects, consider it to have the effect of increasing their weight by 10%. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02034))_
- This spell's effect can be expanded. If you double or triple the amount of mental power you consume...... the required strength can become +2 or +3 ...... for weapons and armor. For other objects, it'll be increased by 10% each time. When increasing the weight of an object, it's increased by 10% each, the rate of double expansion would be 1.1 x 1.1 = 1.21 times . For triple , it' d be 1.1 x 1.1 x 1.1 = 1.331 times. To double the weight, you must expand it 8 times , and to increase it tenfold , you must expand it 25 times . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02035))_
- If this spell would cause a weapon or armor's required strength to exceed its owner's strength, that character cannot act. However, if you're using the optional rules under 16.8: Weapons and Armor That Are Too Heavy (see p. 268), they can still act, but they'll receive a penalty to attack power, evasion speed, etc. Even if you increase the required strength, the strike power or defense power of weapons and armor will not change. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02036))_

### Technical frame 1: 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Slow ]

**Context:** _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02409))_

> This is a spell that reduces the target's agility to half its original score. Targets who fail to resist will have their action order and movement speed reduced by half due to changes in their agility, and their evasion speed (points) will also take a penalty.
