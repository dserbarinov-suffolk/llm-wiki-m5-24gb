# [ ⑦ Medicines [Intelligence]] ((Appraisal only) Not retriable)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This ability represents knowledge of poisons and medicines. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- Sages can appraise unknown medicines and poisons, and can mix magical potions and poisons with various effects. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- Regardless of the result or dice roll , you will need materials and at least one week to attempt to create a medicine. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- You can make up to 10 doses at most. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- The target score for a poison which is able to kill people even in small amounts and be tasteless and odorless, is around 20. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- Therefore, game masters should not allow PCs to create or use poisons. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- In Alecrast, the creation and use of poison is considered shameful and inhumane . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- For detailed rules regarding poison, please also refer to 12.2.1: Poisons (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- Bards can earn bard skill level x bard skill level x 2D silver coins by singing and playing an instrument for about an hour in a suitable location (such as a tavern, etc.). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- Unlike the sage skill, there is no restriction on the languages that can be learned. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- This ability is almost the same as the sage skill's ability of the same name, but it only applies to speaking, not reading. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- In other words, bards can converse in languages other than their native language, but they cannot read (or write, of course) text. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- However, of course, you cannot learn runes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.119)
- For detailed rules regarding languages, please refer to 12.1: Rules Regarding Language (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- Musical instruments are always required to sing spellsongs. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- A bard can learn one spellsong per skill level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- For detailed rules regarding spellsongs, please refer to 5.3: Spellsongs (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When appraising medicines or poisons, a success roll must be made using their rarity as the target score.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
To create medicines or poisons, the game master should set a target score depending on the type, and ask for a success roll check.
```
