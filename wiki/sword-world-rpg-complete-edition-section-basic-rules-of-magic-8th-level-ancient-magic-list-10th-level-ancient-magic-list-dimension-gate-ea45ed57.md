- This spell creates a gate between two distant places, allowing you to instantly move to a destination. The gate is a silver disc 5 meters in diameter, which appears on the floor. A disc of the same shape will also appear above the destination. The disc can be penetrated like water, and those who jump into it will jump out of the other disc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02628))_
- There is no restriction on the travel distance, but the destination must be a place the caster knows. If the caster knows about the underworld, it's also possible to open a gate to the underworld. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02629))_
- A normal Dimension Gate lasts for only 3 minutes, but the sorcerers of the ancient kingdom knew the technique for fixing gates semi-permanently, and so used them as a way of moving between distant places or within labyrinths. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02630))_
- Effect=Makes the air within the area of effect into a lethal substance, killing living things instantly _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02636))_
- Death Cloud turns the air within its area of effect into a lethal poison gas. All creatures within range must attempt to resist this spell, and if they fail, they will die instantly. If successful, they will suffer no effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02640))_
- This spell is a powerful version of Dispel Magic , which removes the effects of all spells. Even spells of the nonremovable or curse type, which cannot be removed by Dispel Magic , will be nullified. However, to remove a spell with a target score, you'll need a final score accordingly. In addition, only the dragon roar magic Reborn Dragon cannot be removed by this magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02653))_
- The fact that there are countless unseen masses of rock floating in the sky is considered a matter of course among sages. These rocks fall to the ground at a rate of about once every hundred years. The meteorite will fall the moment you finish the spell, causing a large explosion. Targets within the area of effect will be hit by shockwaves and fragments of the meteorite, suffering strike power 50 _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02664))_
