## Interlude: The Carpenter Interviews for a Job

This source describes an interlude from *JavaScript Allongé* where The Carpenter, a meticulous JavaScript programmer, is interviewed by a recruiter for a job at a startup. The interview involves solving a problem related to determining whether a game on a checkerboard with randomly placed arrows will eventually halt. The Carpenter provides a solution that involves transforming the game into an iterable and using generator notation for clarity.

### Key Points:
- **The Carpenter** is known for his attention to detail and hand-crafted code.
- **The Interview Problem**: Determine if a game on a hidden checkerboard will halt based on a stream of directions.
- **Solution Approach**: The Carpenter uses an iterable to generate arrows and refactors the board for easier testing.
- **Code Example**: A `Board` and `Game` function are provided, demonstrating how to initialize the board and simulate the game.
- **Context**: The problem is similar to detecting loops in a linked list, a common interview question.
