## Statements

- This sword has knowledge about enemies and speaks to the user's mind, telling him or her the enemy's name and strength in concrete scores. The contents of this knowledge are limited to only what concerns the enemy. Also, the sword's language is low ancient, so a character who cannot speak low ancient cannot obtain this knowledge. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09185))_
