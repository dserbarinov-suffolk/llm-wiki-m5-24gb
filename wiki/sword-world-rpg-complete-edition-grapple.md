# Grapple

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- To attempt a grapple , you must first not be holding any large weapons or shields in your hands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.265)
- Then, any attempt to grapple will automatically occur at the end of the round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.265)
- If you succeed (and your opponent fails to evade), you'll enter a grapple. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.265-266)
- Large weapons must be kept out of hand. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- When grappling, both sides automatically become prone . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- Actions that characters in a grapple can take are limited to the following. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- If the one trying to escape's final score is greater, or if there's a tie, the grapple will be broken. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- Both sides will fall prone , with a short distance between them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- An opponent who is trying to maintain the grapple may counter this by making one using adventurer level + agility bonus or adventure level + strength bonus as the baseline score, whichever is greater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- Weapons you dropped when entering the grapple will probably be lying around nearby. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- To escape from a grapple, make a success roll check using adventurer level + agility bonus as the baseline score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- If the strangler or pinner attempts to escape, he or she will automatically succeed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- For adventurers who rely on weapons, this may pose a threat. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- Of course, a monster that does not have hands cannot strangle . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- It' s fine for the game master to have certain monsters attempt to grapple. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- If you roll 1~3, you can aim at your enemy. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- At this time, your enemy suffers a penalty (-2) for being prone . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- Even if this attack misses, it will not hit your allies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- This is because both your enemies and allies are fervently moving around. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- However, if your enemy is being strangled or pinned down , you can attack them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)
- Please make a damage check immediately. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.266)

Common  runes require you to not only be able to speak, but to also be able to use a catalyst. It's usually a ring, so you can use it.
