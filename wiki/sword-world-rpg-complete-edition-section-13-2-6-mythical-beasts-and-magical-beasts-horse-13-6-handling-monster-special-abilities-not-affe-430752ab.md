## Statements

- Certain mythical beasts, magical beasts, undead powered by negative life, and pseudo-life forms created by magic, are free from poison and illness. These monsters are not affected by poison and illness. Ancient magic that alters the atmosphere ( XX Cloud ) is also ineffective. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08868))_
