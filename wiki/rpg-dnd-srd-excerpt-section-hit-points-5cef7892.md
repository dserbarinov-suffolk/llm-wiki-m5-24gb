# Hit Points

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Typically, you add your Constitution modifier to each Hit Die you roll for your hit points. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00185))_
- So you add 3 hit points for your first three levels, and then roll your hit points for 4th level using your new modifier. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00186))_
- Or if you're 7th level and some effect lowers your Constitution score so as to reduce your Constitution modifier by 1, your hit point maximum is reduced by 7. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00186))_
- If your Constitution modifier changes, your hit point maximum changes as well, as though you had the new modifier from 1st level. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00186))_
- So you add 3 hit points for your first three levels, and then roll your hit points for 4th level using your new modifier. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00186))_

## Technical atoms

> For example, if you raise your Constitution score when you reach 4th level and your Constitution modifier increases from +1 to +2, you adjust your hit point maximum as though the modifier had always been +2.
