# Sap (Blackjack)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This weapon has the potential to cause fae, youma, or animals of human size or smaller to lose consciousness when you attack them unnoticed from behind. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
- The target score is the final score from when the attack hit . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
- If he or she fails, they'll fall unconscious and will not wake up for 2D x 10 minutes . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
- Normal damage is dealt regardless of success or failure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
- The attacked opponent gains a +4 bonus to his or her resistance roll if he or she is wearing metal armor. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
