# Monster Resistance

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The baseline score for a monster's mental power resistance roll is resistance points -7 (written in parentheses) . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- This is an exception to the rule in order to maintain numerical balance. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
- However, a monster's resistance roll fails if there's a tie . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.273)
