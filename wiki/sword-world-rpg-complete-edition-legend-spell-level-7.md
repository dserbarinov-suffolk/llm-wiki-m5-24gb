# [ Legend ] (Spell Level=7)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Base Mental Power Cost=28 Distance=Touch Area=One Duration=Permanent Effect= Make the target memorize text Type=Non-removable Expansion=Text amount (see explanation). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- This is a spell for passing down oral instruction. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- The caster can make the target of the spell memorize specific text without error. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- This spell allows you to increase the amount of text your target memorizes by expanding its effect (for example, they can memorize up to 20 sentences of text if you consume double the mental power). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- The text must be limited to 10 sentences. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- Also, it must be pronounced and heard directly. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- For reference, this spell's description, including this last part, is 8 sentences long. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
- In other words, text of this amount can be easily memorized. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.107)
