# 3.2.1 Delaying an Action

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Characters (including NPCs and monsters) do not have to act based on their agility and can instead delay their actions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- In such a case, a character may lower their action order to 0 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- In other words, they will act only at the very end of the round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- In this case, an intended attack on an enemy that is about to withdraw can be changed to another target that you're engaged with. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- At this time, an attacker can choose any new target to switch to (from among those they're engaged with). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- As we'll see later, if all the enemies you're engaged with attempt to withdraw (flee), even if they have high agility, you can still. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36-37)
- attack, and you will have a higher chance of hitting than usual. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** example

```
For example, suppose you know that a fellow wizard can use magic to increase the attack power of your sword. But what if your agility is higher than theirs? The support magic will be applied after you swing your sword, and the magic will be wasted.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If you want to delay an action, you must mention it in the action announcement phase, along with the action you intend to take.
```

<a id="atom-3"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If the enemy that's trying to withdraw has a higher agility than you, you must switch to a different target, because according to your action order, the enemy will have escaped.
```

<a id="atom-4"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If your agility is higher than that of the enemy who is trying to withdraw, you can attack them before they escape.
```
