# Loss of Ability Scores and Its Impact

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The four other ability scores of dexterity, agility, intelligence, and strength are rarely reduced, but if this does happen and these ability scores do fall to 0 or less, the adventurer will still become disabled. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- If either life force or mental power fall to 0 or less, the adventurer will fall unconscious and be unable to act. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- If life force and mental power are reduced by wounds or exhaustion, they will recover once the wounds are treated or the exhaustion is wiped away. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- There are various conditions for these reductions and recoveries, which you will discover as you read through this book. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- Each of these numbers is referred to as a dexterity bonus , intelligence bonus , etc., using the name of the ability score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- However, temporary reductions do not affect modifiers for only two scores: life force and mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
- An ability score value is related to the success or failure of an action that an adventurer tries to perform, but in Sword World RPG , success or failure is determined by a bonus derived from the ability score, rather than the ability score itself. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.12)
