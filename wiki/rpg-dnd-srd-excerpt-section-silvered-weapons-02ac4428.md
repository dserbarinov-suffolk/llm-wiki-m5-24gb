# Silvered Weapons

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Some monsters that have immunity or resistance to nonmagical weapons are susceptible to silver weapons, so cautious adventurers invest extra coin to plate their weapons with silver. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00527))_
- This cost represents not only the price of the silver, but the time and expertise needed to add silver to the weapon without making it less effective. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00527))_
- You can silver a single weapon or ten pieces of ammunition for 100 gp. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00527))_
