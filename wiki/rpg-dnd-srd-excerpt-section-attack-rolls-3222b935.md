# Attack Rolls

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- When you make an attack, your attack roll determines whether the attack hits or misses. To make an attack roll, roll a d20 and add the appropriate modifiers. If the total of the roll plus modifiers equals or exceeds the target's Armor Class (AC), the attack hits. The AC of a character is determined at character creation, whereas the AC of a monster is in its stat block. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00358))_
