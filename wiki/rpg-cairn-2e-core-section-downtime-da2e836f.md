# Downtime

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- Between game sessions, players can engage in a variety of activities such as research, following up on leads, improving skills, or building relationships. A PC is limited to one Downtime Action at a time. These actions cannot be undertaken in unsafe conditions or while a character is in recovery. A character cannot perform an action if it would put their safety at risk. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00371))_
