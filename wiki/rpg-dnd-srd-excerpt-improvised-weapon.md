# Improvised Weapons

What [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]] covers about improvised weapons:

## Statements

- Sometimes characters don't have their weapons and have to attack with whatever is at hand. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00523))_
- An improvised weapon includes any object you can wield in one or two hands, such as broken glass, a table leg, a frying pan, a wagon wheel, or a dead goblin. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00523))_
- An improvised thrown weapon has a normal range of 20 feet and a long range of 60 feet. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00525))_
- If a character uses a ranged weapon to make a melee attack, or throws a melee weapon that does not have the thrown property, it also deals 1d4 damage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00525))_
