## Statements

- This is a spell that allows the caster to teleport their possessions into the palm of their hand. However, even though it's their possession, the caster must have a complete memory of it, and moreover must be able to identify specifically where it's kept. Also, the size of the target is limited to something you can hold in your hand (for example, you can summon a sword, but not armor). If the duration expires or if the spell is canceled, the target immediately returns to its original location. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02195))_
- This spell has the effect of negating any one of the four major spirit powers of earth, water, fire, or wind. However, only one kind of spirit power can be erased at a time. This spell cannot be expanded to block two or more kinds of spirit power from working at the same time. As a result of this, spirit magic that depends on that spirit power will no longer be usable. Also, if a spell effect is present due to the negated spirit power (either gnome, undine, salamander, or sylph) within the spell's area of effect, it will be negated. If that spirit is present, it will also disappear from existence and return to the spirit world. However, this has no effect on greater spirits (behemoth, kraken, efreet, djinn). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02215))_
- Resist=None A skeleton warrior, also known as a dragontooth warrior, is a kind of golem created from a dragon's tooth, and resembles a human skeleton with a sword and shield. This spell is what creates that formidable warrior. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02225))_
- The caster can give commands to the skeleton warrior in high ancient. Only the caster can give commands. They cannot understand overly complex commands, but they handle simple commands accurately. For example, commands such as kill anyone who enters this room or kill that man are very simple and can be carried out, but a command such as go to the next village and kidnap the chief's daughter cannot be carried out. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02226))_
- The dragon's tooth, which is the source of the skeleton warrior, is a very valuable item as one of the treasures of the ancient kingdom. You'd have to pay a whopping 5000 silver coins to purchase it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02227))_
- By casting this spell, the caster can discover parts that are untrue, exaggerated, and dramatized in conversations they overhear. During the duration, all words that the caster hears are subject to a true-or-false check. However, while you can discover lies, you cannot know truths. You can only know that if it's a lie, it's a lie, and if it's an exaggeration, it's an exaggeration. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02238))_
- This is a spell that increases the caster's vision by several times, allowing them to see things far away. Just think of it as having the same effect as using binoculars to see far away. Therefore, anything that's not in view, such as behind a wall, will not become visible even if you use this spell. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02256))_
- Casting this spell allows the caster to fly freely. The speed of flying in the sky is what would be movement speed 50, and during full movement, 50 km per hour is the maximum speed. However, even if movement speed changes, the caster's agility itself does not change. If the spell's effect is removed while you're flying, you'll naturally fall. In this case, apply the rules for falling damage (see p. 122). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02266))_
