# 14.2.1 Negotiations With Adventurers

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Please consider the estimated reward for asking an adventurer to resolve a case to be around 50~200 times the (total of) each adventurer level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- For a party consisting of three 2nd level characters and two 3rd level characters, the estimated reward is around (2 x 2 x 3 + 3 x 3 x 2) x 50~200 = 1500~6000 gamels total. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- Rune masters tend to be lower level, but that doesn't mean they're any less useful than other characters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- Even if there's variation in level between the party, please make sure the rewards presented are equal per person. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- In the above example, the reward presented would be 300~1200 gamels per person. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- A 3rd level character would not be presented 450~1800 gamels nor would a 2nd level character be presented 200~800 gamels. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- Of course, this does not mean that the reward will increase if the adventurer intentionally delays the resolution. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- If it's shorter, there'll be less of a reward, and if it's longer, there'll be more. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- This reward is basically for a case that's expected to be resolved in one day, or 3~4 days at most. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- Such behavior would result in lost trust and likely void the agreement. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- In these contracts, 10~30% is given upfront as an advance payment. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
- If you fail to resolve the case, you will not be paid the remaining money. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.241)
