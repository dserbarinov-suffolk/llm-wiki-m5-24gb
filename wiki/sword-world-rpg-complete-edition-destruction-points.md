Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In the case of paper, the score is 1~10, so the minimum destruction points are 1 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)
- The lowest of this score is called the minimum destruction points . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)
- Defense points and destruction points are used in the basic system for destroying structures. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)
- However, the structure itself is not destroyed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)
- damage is calculated in the same way as when an attack hits a monster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)

- If the result is 0 or less (when damage is less than or equal to defense points), that means there was no effect at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)
- Then, you subtract the defense points from the determined damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)

- If a character attacks with a weapon and deals 22 or greater points of damage in one blow, this wooden wall will be destroyed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.277)
