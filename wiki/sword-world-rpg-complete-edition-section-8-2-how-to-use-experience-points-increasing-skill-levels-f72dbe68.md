## Statements

- At the end of an adventure, characters gain experience points. By spending these experience points, a character can increase their skill level-you pay experience points to buy skill levels. A character can advance in any number of skills and by any number of levels at once. However, in any case, a character's skill level can only reach 10th level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04382))_
- The adventurer Ducard II gains 5000 experience points as a result of his adventure. He would like to increase his fighter skill level, which is currently 2, to 3rd level. At this time, the required experience points are 2000 points. He still has 3000 experience points left. He pursues his abilities as a ranger and raises his ranger skill from 1st level to 3rd level in one go. The experience points required for this are 1000 + 1500 = 2500 points. There are 500 experience points left. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04385))_
- Any remaining experience points can be carried over and used in addition to the experience points earned on the next adventure. It may seem strange to carry over experience , but think of it as cutting off the adventurer's growth by the amount of experience points carried over for now. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04386))_
- Ducard II will keep 500 experience points for future use. If he gains 5000 experience points again on his next adventure, he can now use his 5500 experience points to advance. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04388))_
- Experience points cannot be negative. This is because you can't grow from experiences you haven't had. You also cannot sell your skill levels for experience points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04389))_
- However, in any case, a character's skill level can only reach 10th level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04382))_
