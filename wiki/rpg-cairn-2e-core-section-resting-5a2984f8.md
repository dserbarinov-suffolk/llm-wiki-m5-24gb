# Resting

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- - A character can spend a turn resting to restore all HP . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00284))_
- Present or oncoming danger makes rest impossible. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00285))_
- - A light source and a safe location are required to rest . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00285))_
- - Resting does not restore Fatigue , as it is impossible to safely Make Camp in a dungeon. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00286))_
