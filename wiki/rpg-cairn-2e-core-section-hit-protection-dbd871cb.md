# Hit Protection

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- Roll 1d6 to determine your PC's starting Hit Protection (HP), which reflects their ability to avoid damage in combat. It does not indicate a character's health or fortitude, nor do they lose it for very long. See Healing & Recovery. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00105))_
