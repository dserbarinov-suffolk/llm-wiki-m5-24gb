# Movement

What [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]] covers about movement:

## Statements

### 3.3.1 When It's Possible to / Movement and Actions

- Characters have three options for movement: full movement , normal movement , or stand still . Each has different distances that can be traveled and different restrictions on actions that can be taken. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01114))_

- Below are some guidelines on how movement works and which actions can be taken. For any other actions, please use common sense based on the time frame of 1 round = 10 seconds. Final decisions are to be made by the game master. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01115))_
