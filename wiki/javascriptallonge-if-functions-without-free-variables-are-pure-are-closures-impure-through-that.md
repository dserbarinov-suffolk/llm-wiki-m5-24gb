## Key supported claims

- Functions can be classified based on whether they contain free variables. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.45-45)
- Functions without free variables are called pure functions. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.45-45)
- Functions with one or more free variables are called closures. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.45-45)
- Closures cannot be fully understood without knowing the value of the free variable x. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.45-45)
- Composing a closure that contains a pure function requires understanding how free variables are evaluated. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.45-45)
