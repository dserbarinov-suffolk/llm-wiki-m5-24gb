## Statements

- Think of the adventurers of Alecrast as basically on an adventure to search for treasure lying untouched in the ruins of the ancient kingdom. Enormous amounts of riches lie in the ruins that make a profession like theirs viable. The thing to remember here is that, in reality, neither silver nor gold coins existed in the ancient kingdom. Therefore, unless they were brought in after coins were invented, no such coins are buried in the ruins of the ancient kingdom.
