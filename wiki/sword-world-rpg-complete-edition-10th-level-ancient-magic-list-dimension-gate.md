# 10th Level Ancient Magic List [ Dimension Gate ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- The gate is a silver disc 5 meters in diameter, which appears on the floor. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- The disc can be penetrated like water, and those who jump into it will jump out of the other disc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- A disc of the same shape will also appear above the destination. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- There is no restriction on the travel distance, but the destination must be a place the caster knows. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- A normal Dimension Gate lasts for only 3 minutes, but the sorcerers of the ancient kingdom knew the technique for fixing gates semi-permanently, and so used them as a way of moving between distant places or within labyrinths. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- Effect= Makes the air within the area of effect into a lethal substance, killing living things instantly. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- If successful, they will suffer no effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- This spell is a powerful version of Dispel Magic , which removes the effects of all spells. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- However, to remove a spell with a target score, you'll need a final score accordingly. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- The fact that there are countless unseen masses of rock floating in the sky is considered a matter of course among sages. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- These rocks floating in the sky are called meteorites. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- These rocks fall to the ground at a rate of about once every hundred years. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- The meteorite will fall the moment you finish the spell, causing a large explosion. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- The spell Meteor Strike is a spell that allows the caster to control a meteorite (a very small one) at will, and make it fall to the ground. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.82)
- Of course, its destructive power would be enough to destroy buildings and ramparts. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.83)
