# Number of Projectile Shots

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- on the ground and use them as bullets, it must be every other round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- A crossbow can only shoot every other round due to the time it takes to wind them up. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- However, if you leave it set for a long time, the string will lose its elasticity, and the quarrel will easily come off if you tip the crossbow. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- But if not , they'll take one round to prepare and can only be thrown every other round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- A bow can be fired every round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- However, this is only if you have arrows ready in your quiver. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
