# Dungeon Exploration

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about dungeon exploration:

## Statements

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / The Basics

- The dungeon exploration cycle (see below) is divided into a series of Turns , Actions , and their consequences. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00258))_

- On their turn , a character can move a distance equal to their torchlight's perimeter (about 40ft), and perform one action . Players can use their action to move up to three times that distance though that will increase the chance of triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00259))_

- The Warden should present obvious information about an area and its dangers freely and at no cost. Moving quickly or without caution may increase the chance of encountering a wandering monster, springing a trap, or triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00260))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Dungeon Exploration Cycle

- The Warden describes the party's surroundings and any immediate dangers (combat, traps, surprises, etc.). The players then declare their character's intended movements and actions . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00263))_

- The Warden resolves the actions of each character simultaneously, along with any actions that are already in progress. Remember, the Die of Fate can be a useful tool whenever the Warden is in doubt! _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00264))_

- The players record any loss of resources and any new conditions (i.e. item use, deprivation , etc). The cycle then begins again. If appropriate, the Warden should roll on the Dungeon Events table. Keep common sense in mind when interpreting the results! _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00265))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Actions

- Actions are any non-passive activities, such as searching for traps , forcing open a door , listening for danger , disarming a trap , engaging an enemy in combat , casting a spell , dodging a trap , running away , resting , etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00275))_

- Some actions have special rules (see below), while others may take multiple turns to complete. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00276))_

- Loud or noticeable actions may also trigger an encounter with the dungeon's denizens. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00277))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Panic

- A character that is surrounded by enemies, enveloped by darkness, or facing their greatest fears may experience panic . A WIL save is typically required to avoid losing control and becoming panicked . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00287))_

- A panicked character must make a WIL save to overcome their condition as an action on their turn . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00288))_

- A panicked character has 0 HP , does not act in the first round of combat, and all of their attacks are impaired . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00289))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Dungeon Elements

- A torch can be lit 3 times before permanently degrading. A lantern can be relit 6 times per oil can, but requires more inventory slots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00293))_

- Characters without a light source may suffer from panic until their situation is remedied. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00294))_

- Doors and entryways may be locked, stuck, or blocked entirely. Characters can try to force a door open (or wedge it shut) using available resources (spikes, glue) or through raw ability. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00296))_

### Dungeon Exploration / Principles

- Always clarify what is about to happen. Does that flaming barrel look stable or like it might explode at any minute? _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00600))_

- Regularly restate a character's surroundings, where they are in physical space, and what their senses are detecting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00601))_

- Do not tell characters how they should feel, and only tell them what's happening right now, before their eyes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00603))_
