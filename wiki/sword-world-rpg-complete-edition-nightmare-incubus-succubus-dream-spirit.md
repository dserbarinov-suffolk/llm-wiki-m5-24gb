# [ Nightmare ] (Incubus, Succubus/Dream Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This spell has no distance restrictions, but the target must be someone the caster knows personally. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- This is a spell that uses a dream spirit to send nightmares into the target's sleep, cursing your opponent to death. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- If the ritual is interrupted, even for one night, the spell's effect is completely lost. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- The spell begins when the target goes to sleep, while the caster remains awake and focuses on controlling the dream spirit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- This ritual must be performed every night until the target becomes weak and dies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- Also, the ritual must be continued overnight with undivided attention from sunset until dawn. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- Thus, the dream spirit controlled in this way enters the opponent's dreams and causes them to have nightmares. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- The target of the spell must attempt to resist daily. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- Also, regardless of whether the resist succeeded or failed, they will no longer be able to recover their mental power (points) by any means (it will only recover once the spell's effect is removed). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- If their mental power (points) reaches 0 due to the spell's reduction, the person will fall into a coma, and die if the ritual continues for one more week. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
- The spell's effect will never disappear until the ritual is interrupted for some reason or it is negated with Remove Curse , etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.95)
