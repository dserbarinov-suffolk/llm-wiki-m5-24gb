5.2 Common Runes
Common  runes were  invented  by Mana Rai, the great sage of Oran, and it involves  putting  the  power  of  ancient magic  into  items  (mainly  rings)  and activating it using a password in common .
Theoretically, any ancient magic can be  made  into  common  runes,  but  the sorcerers' guild (academy of sages) limits what gets put on the market, to prevent the  reputation  of  magic  and  sorcerers
Common  runes,  like  other  magic, consume mental power when used. The amount  of  mental  power  consumed  is determined by each common rune.
Common  runes  can  be  used by anyone who knows the password. Also, even if you can't use your hands or are wearing metal armor, you can use them as long as you can say the word. However, no  matter  how  high  your  rune  master skill is, you cannot reduce the amount of mental  power  consumed.  When  using common runes, if magic power is required for a game procedure, treat it as 0 regardless of the user. Common runes can  also  be  expanded  by  consuming extra mental power. However, increasing the final score is the one thing you cannot do.
