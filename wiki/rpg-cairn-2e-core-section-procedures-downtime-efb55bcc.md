## Statements

- Between game sessions, players can engage in a variety of activities such as research, following up on leads, improving skills, or building relationships. A PC is limited to one Downtime Action at a time. These actions cannot be undertaken in unsafe conditions or while a character is in recovery. A character cannot perform an action if it would put their safety at risk. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00388))_

## Statements by subsection

### Procedures / Downtime / Milestones

- For activities requiring multiple steps, the Warden assigns 1-5 Milestones for players to track progress. Each Milestone represents a comprehensive, non-interactive task. The Warden may present different strategies to achieve these goals, each with distinct Milestones . Depending on the unfolding events in the game, the Warden is also empowered to introduce new Milestones or discard existing ones. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00390))_

> Some Costs can be reduced or disregarded through character skills, connections, or force of will. For example, a PC may have already acquired the necessary reputation to gain access to a renowned institution, and thus the cost is abated. On the other hand, another character may not be so lucky and must rely on their force of personality instead. In this case, the Warden should state the risk (a permanent ban on entry, a loss of reputation, etc.). The PC then makes a WIL save; on a success, the cost is either reduced or avoided entirely.
