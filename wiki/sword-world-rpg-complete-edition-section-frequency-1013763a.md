- This is the frequency at which the monster appears. The types in descending order of frequency are frequent , moderate , rare , and very rare . It's a bit unnatural to have rare or very rare monsters repeatedly appear in a scenario. In such cases, it would be best to provide a reason that the players can understand. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05916))_
