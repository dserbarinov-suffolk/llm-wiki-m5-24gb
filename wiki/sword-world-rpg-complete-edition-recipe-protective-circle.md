## Applicability And Rationale

- There are two types of this spell: Heat Protective Circle and Cold Protective Circle . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03554))_
- This spell has the effect of protecting the caster and anyone within 5 meters from abnormally high or low temperatures. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03554))_
- The effect of this spell is almost the same as Heat Protective Circle , but it protects against damage from ice and cold. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03559))_
- If they are inside when the spell is cast, they will immediately try to escape, and if for some reason that is not possible, they will return to their own spirit world. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03559))_
- Also, frau, an ice spirit, will not try to enter this range. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03559))_

_Source: Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03556)_

```
Type=Counters (Overpowers): Cold Protective Circle, Spirit Wall: Salamander Countered (Overpowered) By: Cold Protective Circle, Spirit Wall: Salamander Expansion=Duration, area Resist=None This spell protects those within its area of effect from fire and heat. The specific effect is that you can subtract 2 points from damage from those attacks. The same goes for attacks from weapons that had Fire Weapon cast on them. Salamanders, which are fire spirits, will not attempt to enter within this range. If a fire spirit enters the area of effect, that spirit will immediately try to leave. If this is not possible (for example, if it's being controlled by another person using a spell such as Control Spirit , if it's sealed in an item, etc.), the spirit will return to its own spirit world.
```
