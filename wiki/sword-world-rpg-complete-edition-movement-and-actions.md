# Movement and Actions

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Characters have three options for movement: full movement , normal movement , or stand still . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- Each has different distances that can be traveled and different restrictions on actions that can be taken. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- For any other actions, please use common sense based on the time frame of 1 round = 10 seconds. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- Below are some guidelines on how movement works and which actions can be taken. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
