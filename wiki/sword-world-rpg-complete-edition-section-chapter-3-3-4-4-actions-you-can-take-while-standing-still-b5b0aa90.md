## Statements by subsection

### Chapter 3: / 3.4.4 Actions You Can Take While Standing Still / Stand Up and Ready Yourself

- From a state of falling down, stand up and ready yourself. You can only ready one weapon, etc. that you're wearing. You cannot pick up any items on the ground. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01142))_
- You can only ready one weapon, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01142))_

### Chapter 3: / 3.4.4 Actions You Can Take While Standing Still / Weapon and Armor Restrictions

- A character's arms are largely divided into weapons, shields, and armor. Required strength should not be added up, but rather considered separately for each of these. In other words, a character with 14 strength can wield a weapon with a required strength of 14, wield a shield with a required strength of 14, and wear armor with a required strength of 14. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01150))_

Combined Expansion and Contraction
Although  it's not  often  done,  it's possible  to  perform  magic  expansion and contraction  at  the  same  time.  For example,  you  could extend the  area  of effect  while reducing magic  power.  The mental  power  required  at  this  time  is decided by expansion . It cannot be reduced by contraction.
