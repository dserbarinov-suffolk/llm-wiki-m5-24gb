# 4.7.2 Damage Checks

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- This procedure is exactly the same for characters as it was during combat with monsters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- The difference is the damage dealt to the defender. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- Bucky is wielding a battle-ax (required strength 15) with both hands, so he has a strike power of 20 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- The result of his strike roll is 11 (base damage 9 ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- His following 2D roll is 9 (base damage 7 ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- His bonus damage is 5 (fighter skill level 3, strength bonus +2), which brings the total amount of damage to 9+7+5= 21 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- After adding his damage reduction of 2 , 3+2= 5 points is the final amount of damage Ducard II is able to reduce. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- As a result, the damage that Ducard II suffers is 21-5= 16 points . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
