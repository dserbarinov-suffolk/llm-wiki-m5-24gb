- This is a power that's at work in places where the ground is exposed or underground. Particularly strong spirit power is at work in the mountains. Since the power of water spirits is strong on the ocean floor and the bottoms of lakes, the spirit power of earth does not work there. Likewise, this spirit power does not work on the floors of buildings (whether they're stone or wood). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02770))_
- The spirit power of water is at work wherever there is water, such as the ocean or a lake. Even if you carry water in a water bottle, etc., if you open the lid, the spirit power will work. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02772))_
- This spirit power is at work wherever there's fire burning. You can also use fire from a torch you're carrying with you. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02774))_
- This spirit power is at work wherever there is snow or ice. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02776))_
- This spirit power is at work wherever there are living things with functioning minds. Of course if the caster him or herself is like that, there's no problem. Some of these types of spirits include those that control the nature of the mind, and those that control fluctuation of emotions. It's said that if one emotion has too much of an outburst, it can cause phenomena such as alienation or complete loss of functions of other spirits, which can lead to mental disorders. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02778))_

5.1.13 Spirit Magic  and the Shaman Skil
