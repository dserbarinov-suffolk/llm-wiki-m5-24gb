### Chapter 6: / 6.1 Precautions When Using Skills / Thief Skill / [ ⑫ Long Jump/High Jump [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction) / [ ⑬ Follow [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

- This is the ability to follow an opponent without them noticing. The side that's following must use thief skill level + agility bonus as their baseline score, while the side being followed must use adventurer level + intelligence bonus , and success or failure is determined by comparing the final scores of their success rolls. A tie is a failure to follow. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04043))_
- If you fail, your opponent will realize that they are being followed. What action the person takes after that is up to them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04044))_
- What action the person takes after that is up to them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04044))_

### Chapter 6: / 6.1 Precautions When Using Skills / Thief Skill / [ ⑫ Long Jump/High Jump [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction) / [ ⑭ Disguise [Dexterity] ] (No equipment restriction)

- This is the ability to change one's face and appearance using special makeup. It's easy to just act like a completely different person, but it's difficult to disguise yourself as someone specific. Things like height and physique can be changed to a certain extent, but there are still limits. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04046))_
- If the thief is disguised as a specific person and they encounter an acquaintance of that person, they must make a success roll to determine success or failure. The game master should add modifiers to success rolls to account for the unnaturalness of the disguise (such as disguising as the opposite sex, etc.). Especially when disguised as someone whose characteristics the thief doesn't know in detail, it's best to add a difficult modifier. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04047))_
