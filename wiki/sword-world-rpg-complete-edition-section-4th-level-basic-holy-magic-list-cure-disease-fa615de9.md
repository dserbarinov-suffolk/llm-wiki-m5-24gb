## Statements

- This spell can be used on an individual and has the effect of healing them of any illness they may be suffering from. If the final score when casting this spell is greater than or equal to double the healing score of each disease, you can heal that disease. For details, see 12.2.2: Illnesses (see p. 164). This spell can heal multiple diseases at once and restore health. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03546))_

### 4th Level Basic Holy Magic List [ Cure Disease ] / [ Protective Circle ]

- This spell has the effect of protecting the caster and anyone within 5 meters from abnormally high or low temperatures. There are two types of this spell: Heat Protective Circle and Cold Protective Circle . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03554))_
- The effect of this spell is almost the same as Heat Protective Circle , but it protects against damage from ice and cold. Those within the area of effect can subtract 2 points from this kind of damage. Also, frau, an ice spirit, will not try to enter this range. If they are inside when the spell is cast, they will immediately try to escape, and if for some reason that is not possible, they will return to their own spirit world. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03559))_
