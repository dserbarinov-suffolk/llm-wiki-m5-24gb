## Statements

- Even an adventurer who goes on an adventure for the first time is not a baby. As long as they've lived a certain number of years, they must have had some experience before entering the life of an adventurer. Once ability scores have been determined, the next step is to determine the adventurer's preadventure experience. This is represented by starting skills and starting experience points . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00373))_
