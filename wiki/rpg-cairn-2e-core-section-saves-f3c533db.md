## Statements

- A save is a roll to avoid negative outcomes from risky choices. Characters roll a d20 and compare the results to the appropriate attribute. If they roll equal to or under that attribute, they succeed. Otherwise, they fail. A 1 is always a success, and a 20 is always a failure. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00174))_
- If two opponents are each trying to overcome the other, whoever is most at risk should save. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00175))_
- If two characters need to take an action together, whoever is most at risk should save (usually the character with the lowest relevant Attribute). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00176))_
