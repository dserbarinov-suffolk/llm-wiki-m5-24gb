# Rules for Poison, Illness and Infection

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In Sword World RPG , illness is defined as a state in which the balance of spirit power within the body is disrupted. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- There are two types of symptoms that can appear. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- The concept of bacterial or viral contagion does not exist. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- One is due to poison and the other is due to illness . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- In addition, a wound may become infected , which is treated as an illness. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- However, if the poison itself is cleared away, the spirit power within the body will naturally return to a balanced state and restore health. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- As long as the poison remains in the body, even if the symptoms disappear, the balance of spirit power will be disrupted and the symptoms will appear again. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- Think of it as the spirits inside your body that are under the illusion that a false equilibrium is the correct one. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- Illness infection occurs when spirits transmit information about the incorrect equilibrium state to spirits in other human bodies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- On the other hand, illness is a state where the spirit power within the body remains unbalanced and in a fixed state. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
- In Sword World RPG , the phenomenon of a wound becoming infected means that the container was broken and deformed, causing the contents to become unbalanced . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.159)
