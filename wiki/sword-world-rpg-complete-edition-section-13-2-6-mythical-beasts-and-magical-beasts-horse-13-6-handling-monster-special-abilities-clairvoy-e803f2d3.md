## Statements

- The mythical beast lynx has a sharp clairvoyance ability. For this reason, even if a character lurks in a place that's hidden from view, etc. he or she will be discovered instantly. Please note that this ability does not mean it'll be able to see behind it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08874))_
