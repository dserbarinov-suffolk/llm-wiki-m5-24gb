# Acquiring Rune Master Skills and Increasing Levels

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Here is a summary of how to acquire and increase the level of each rune master skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- The other is to become an apprentice to a sorcerer with a sorcerer skill of 6th level or greater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- One is to join the sorcerers' guild. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- There are two ways to acquire the sorcerer skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Please interpret this as because the sorcerer's spellbook has spells that they cannot handle written down in advance. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Then, you'll be given basic knowledge as a sorcerer, such as speaking and reading in high and low ancient, thus a sorcerer who can use 1st level spells is born. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Also, although there are no specific restrictions in the rules, a training period of about 3 to 5 years is required. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- As your sorcerer skill increases, your magic power increases and at the same time you also immediately gain access to higher level spells. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- In this case, the rules require 2000 experience points (1500 points if you've acquired the sage skill). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Although there are no specific restrictions in the rules, training for one year or more is usually required. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- This is because your relationship with the spirits becomes stronger. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- As your shaman skill increases, your magic power increases and at the same time you also immediately gain access to higher level spells. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Therefore, there may be no particular need for a master, teacher, or period of training. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- To acquire the priest skill, it is necessary to awaken to faith . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- It is possible to suddenly feel Myrii's power while fighting and become a priest, or to feel heartbroken by the chaotic mayhem and, worried about what to do, become an apostle of Pharis. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- This is because it is the best way to experience the nature of faith firsthand. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- However, the surest way to awaken to faith is to work in a temple, constantly see other priests, and receive their teachings. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- As your priest skill increases, your magic power increases and at the same time you also immediately gain access to higher level spells. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- This is because your relationship with your god becomes stronger. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- From there, you will have to increase your priest skill again. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153-154)
- If the game master wishes, on the basis that you rebelled greatly against your old faith, they may require only half the normal experience points to increase your level until you reach your previous priest skill level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153-154)
- The method of acquiring and increasing the dark priest skill is almost the same as the priest skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.154)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When you awaken to faith and recognize the existence of the gods, you can acquire the 1st level priest skill.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
In rare cases, if a character who had the priest skill believes that their previous teachings are wrong and awakens to the teachings of another god, they may lose any previous priest skill levels they had and acquire 1st level in a new god's priest skill level instead.
```
