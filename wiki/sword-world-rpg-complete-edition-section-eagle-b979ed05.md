## Statements

- Eagles are large, carnivorous birds. They attack their opponents from the air with their claws and beak. When an adventurer attacks an eagle, they will suffer a -2 penalty to their attack because it' s airborne. An eagle can grab and carry away a medium-sized animal, such as a dog _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-06811))_
