# Sing (Mind)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Anyone who hears this spellsong will start singing the same lyrics along with the bard's song. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.110)
- There's no need to remember the lyrics. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.110)
- However, it has no effect on creatures that do not sing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.110)
- Those affected will not be restricted in their actions, but as long as the spellsong continues, they will also be singing and will not be able to cast magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.110)
