- For races other than human (except for half-elves raised by humans), the starting skills and experience points are determined by race (Table 1-6: Starting Experience by Race). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00415))_
- Since the character we're creating is a human, we'll use the backgrounds table. Our roll is "9". The character ended up coming from the mercenary background. Therefore, we'll have 1 level in the fighter skill as our starting skill, and 2500 starting experience points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00417))_
- In addition, money is listed as 2Dx200. This means we roll 2D and have money (in gamels ) equal to the result times 200. The result of our next 2D roll is 6 . The character has 1200 gamels. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00418))_
