## Technical atoms

> - In these situations, roll 1d6. A roll of 4 or more generally favors the players.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00087))_

> - Roll 3d6 for each of your character's Attributes , in order. You may then swap any two of the results.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00102))_

> - If an attack would take a PC's HP exactly to 0, the player must roll on the Scars table.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00106))_
