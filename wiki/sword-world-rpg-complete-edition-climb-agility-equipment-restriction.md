# [ ⑩ Climb [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Please refer to that section for details on determining target scores, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.114)
- This ability is used to climb walls and cliffs. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.114)
- Of particular note here is that An owner of the thief skill (as long as they follow equipment restrictions) succeeds when climbing using a rope unless they roll double ones on a success roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.114)
- Sailors have the ability to climb in order to perform actions such as ascending masts or stretched nets. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.128)
- This is handled in the same way as that of the thief skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.128)
- At this time, the game master should decide the difficulty or target score, taking into consideration the tightness of the knot and the time it'll take. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.128)
- Sailors have the ability to tie ropes at will so that they do not unravel or, conversely, quickly unravel. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.128)
- A rope that a sailor tied to keep it from coming undone should have a difficulty equal to the sailor's skill level if someone attempts to untie it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.128)
