# Critical Damage

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about critical damage:

## Statements

### Critical Damage

- Damage that reduces a target's HP below zero is subtracted from their STR by the amount of damage remaining. The target must then immediately make a STR save to avoid taking Critical Damage , using their new STR score . On a success, the target is still in the fight (albeit with a lower STR score) and must continue to make critical damage saves when incurring damage. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00220))_

- weakly, grasping for life. If given aid (such as bandages), they will stabilize. If left untreated, they die within the hour. NPCs and monsters that fail a Critical Damage save are considered dead, per the Warden's discretion. Additionally, some enemies will have special abilities or effects that are triggered when their target fails a critical damage save. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00222))_
