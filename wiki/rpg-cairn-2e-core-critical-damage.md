# Critical Damage

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about critical damage:

## Statements

- NPCs and monsters that fail a Critical Damage save are considered dead, per the Warden's discretion. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00222))_
- The target must then immediately make a STR save to avoid taking Critical Damage , using their new STR score . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00220))_
- Additionally, some enemies will have special abilities or effects that are triggered when their target fails a critical damage save. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00222))_
- If given aid (such as bandages), they will stabilize. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00222))_
- If left untreated, they die within the hour. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00222))_
