## Statements

- This spell is a spell that transfers the caster's mental power (points) to another person as it is. For example, if the caster spends 10 points of mental power with this spell, 9 points will be added to the target's mental power, excluding the base mental power cost of 1 point. Even those who have lost their consciousness with 0 mental power can be recovered with this spell. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03407))_
- Please note that this spell cannot transfer more mental power (points) than the target's starting mental power (points). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03409))_
