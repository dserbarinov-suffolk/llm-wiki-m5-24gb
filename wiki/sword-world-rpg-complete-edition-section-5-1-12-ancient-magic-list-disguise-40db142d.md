## Statements

- This spell is a type of illusion magic that works on the caster, changing their appearance into something else. However, this spell cannot significantly change the size or shape of your body. So a thin person cannot become an obese person, and a human cannot look like a grassrunner or a dwarf. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02060))_
