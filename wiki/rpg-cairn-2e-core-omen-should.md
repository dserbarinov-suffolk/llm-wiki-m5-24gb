# Omens Should...

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about omens should...:

## Statements

- The Warden should roll twice on the Wilderness Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00423))_
- The Warden should provide any Milestones and associated Costs . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00484))_
- If an Omen is ignored, it should come to pass and wreak whatever havoc it may. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00724))_
- The Warden should therefore communicate exactly what the PCs can see, hear, and smell. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00646))_
- And of course, the character's inspiration to improve should come from an experience in play. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00484))_
- Bonds and Omens provide new player characters with adventure hooks, starting gear, and abilities. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00715))_
- An Omen is resolved when the characters reveal the truth behind its origin or resolve its dangers. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00724))_
- In this case, the character should experience Growth of some kind, and the Warden can optionally create a new Bond for the PC. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00717))_
- Instead, the Warden should provide unlimited relevant information about every detail a character can perceive with their basic senses. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00626))_
- Once a question is posed and an appropriate Source has been identified, the Warden should provide any Milestones and associated Costs . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00468))_
- If the PCs ask questions about the sword or try to interact with it, the Warden should tell them about the shoe prints in the grass right away. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00647))_
- Omens are the seeds of an adventure yet to come, premonitions of a future disaster, or the cause of a problem the characters are currently facing. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00724))_
