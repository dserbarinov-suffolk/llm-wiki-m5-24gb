## Statements

- A roll of 4 or more generally favors the players. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00087))_
- Telegraph serious danger to players when it is present. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00072))_
- Player characters are not required to resolve their Bonds. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00717))_
- Players do not need to roll dice to learn about their circumstances. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00053))_
- Give players a solid choice to force outcomes when the situation lulls. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00081))_
- Give players opportunities to solve problems and interact with the world. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00074))_
- The players then declare their character's intended movements and actions . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00352))_
- If something the players want to do is sincerely impossible, no roll will allow them to do it. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00058))_
- Bonds and Omens provide new player characters with adventure hooks, starting gear, and abilities. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00715))_
- Players trust one another to engage with the shared setting, character goals, and party challenges. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00023))_
- Player Characters have just three Attributes: ( Strength (STR) , Dexterity (DEX) , and Willpower (WIL) ). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00101))_
