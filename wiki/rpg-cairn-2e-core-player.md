# Player

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about player:

## Statements

- Players should always understand the reasons behind the choices they've made, and information about potential risks should be provided freely and frequently. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00019))_
- Players trust one another to engage with the shared setting, character goals, and party challenges. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00023))_
- Players do not need to roll dice to learn about their circumstances. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00053))_
- Telegraph serious danger to players when it is present. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00072))_
- Player Characters have just three Attributes: ( Strength (STR) , Dexterity (DEX) , and Willpower (WIL) ). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00101))_
- Players can use their action to move up to three times that distance though that will increase the chance of triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00261))_
- The players then declare their character's intended movements and actions . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00265))_
- Between game sessions, players can engage in a variety of activities such as research, following up on leads, improving skills, or building relationships. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00371))_
- To take this action, the player must have a clearly formulated question they'd like to answer and a Source of knowledge in the game world that their character can interact with. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00384))_
- The player must describe precisely what they'd like to improve and a Master whom they might train with. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00400))_
- True agency comes from providing the players with everything they need to know to make an interesting decision, even if that is simply asking a question about what their character might perceive. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00557))_
- Players should always be made aware of the cost. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00580))_
- Player characters are not required to resolve their Bonds. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00640))_
