# [ Windstorm ] (Djinn/Greater Wind Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- in this state, all will suffer a -4 penalty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- Anyone except the caster within the spell's area of effect, or who attempts to enter the tornado during the spell's duration must attempt to resist. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- Those who successfully resist will move at half their normal movement speed (there is no change in their action order) and can take actions with a -2 penalty on success rolls such as attack, evasion, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- However, those who fail to resist are slammed to the ground and can only move 3 meters per round while crawling on the ground for the duration of the spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- Objects weighing less than 10 kilograms or equipment held in the hand or not securely attached to the body will be blown out of the area of effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- The cutting damage continues to have an effect until the target leaves the area of effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
