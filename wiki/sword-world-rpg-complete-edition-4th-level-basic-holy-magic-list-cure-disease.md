# 4th Level Basic Holy Magic List [ Cure Disease ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This spell can be used on an individual and has the effect of healing them of any illness they may be suffering from. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- This spell can heal multiple diseases at once and restore health. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- This spell has the effect of protecting the caster and anyone within 5 meters from abnormally high or low temperatures. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- There are two types of this spell: Heat Protective Circle and Cold Protective Circle . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- Type=Counters (Overpowers): Heat Protective Circle, Spirit Wall: Frau Countered (Overpowered) By: Heat Protective Circle, Spirit Wall: Frau Expansion=Duration, Area Resist=None. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- The effect of this spell is almost the same as Heat Protective Circle , but it protects against damage from ice and cold. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- Also, frau, an ice spirit, will not try to enter this range. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- If they are inside when the spell is cast, they will immediately try to escape, and if for some reason that is not possible, they will return to their own spirit world. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
