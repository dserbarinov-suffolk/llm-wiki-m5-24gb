# When an Adventurer Casts Magic on Adventurers

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The following procedure is used to make a check when data created for a character casts magic on adventurers, and vice versa. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- First, the side casting the magic rolls 2D. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- The final score (magic power + 2D) must then be determined this one time only. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- Suppose that the baseline score of all three of their mental power resistance rolls is 4 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- If their rolls are each 2 (double ones!), 6 , and 9 , then only the last one is able to resist the Lightning . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- First, Gorman rolls 2D and adds his own magic power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- If we assume his 2D roll is 8 , each of the three adventurers must make a mental power resistance roll, with a target score of 5 (magic power) + 8 (roll) = 13 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- If the side casting the magic rolls double ones, the magic has no effect on any targets. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
- Handling double ones and double sixes is no different from when there's only one target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.60)
