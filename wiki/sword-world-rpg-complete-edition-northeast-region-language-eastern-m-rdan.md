# Northeast Region (Language = Eastern/Mārdan)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In the northeastern part of the Alecrast continent, there are three countries: Lodorille, Baikal, and Plysis. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.285)
- However, when Queen June V, known as the "Iron Queen," ascended to the throne, the country underwent a rapid transformation, quickly becoming a military state with a powerful army while annexing neighboring countries. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.285-286)
- Queen June V has banned the use of magic within the country. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.285-286)
- Lodorille was once a poor country that barely survived on agriculture. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.285-286)
- Moreover, this beautiful and charismatic queen is also a cruel human who considers people from other nations as mere slaves, making it far from an ideal country for travelers to visit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.285-286)
- The population is 1.5 million, with 500,000 people concentrated in and around the royal capital of Chase. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.285-286)
- Among the tribes, the maritime tribes scattered along the northern Azul Bay hold more power than the tribes of the southern plains. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- This is because the maritime tribes generate profit through trade and piracy using galley ships called free merchant vessels, wielding incomparable power to the plains people who engage in farming. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Baikal is the northernmost country in Alecrast. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- The total population is slightly over 1 million. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- While the king resides in the royal capital of Boris, he is merely a figurehead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- The men of Baikal are fierce and warlike, but tribal bonds are extremely strong. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Plysis is the largest fortified city in Alecrast and has become a city-state in its own right. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- Plysis has been under invasion from Lodorille, but has continued to repel these attacks through its sturdy fortifications, large weaponry such as catapults, and the strategic brilliance of the talented military advisor Lukial, known as "The Chess Player." However, there was an incident where military advisor Lukial accepted an invitation from the great western nation of Lomar and departed, leading to rumors that the city's survival is now in jeopardy. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
- South of Lodorille and east of Plysis lies the ruins of an ancient kingdom known as "Ancient Ruined City." This city, which still contains the ancient kingdom's treasures, is a forbidden place haunted by the undead and ghosts, and it is said that almost none who venture in ever return. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.286)
