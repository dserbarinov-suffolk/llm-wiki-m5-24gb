## Statements by subsection

### 《 Rings 》 / [ Unpoison Ring ]

- Whoever wears this ring can place their hands on a person affected by poison and attempt to detox. Make a success roll with a baseline score of 8. If the final score is greater than or equal to the poison's toxicity score, it can be detoxed. However, if this fails, the poison's effect is doubled. First, damage is doubled. If it's not a damage poison, the effect time will be doubled. Both cannot be doubled. If neither applies, the game master should make his or her own decision. If this ring increases the effect of the poison, further detox attempts will have +2 to the toxicity score. This ring can only be used 2D times. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09661))_
- This ring can only be used 2D times. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09661))_

### 《 Rings 》 / [ Quickness Ring ]

- By wearing this ring and chanting a password, the user's agility bonus becomes +2. This effect lasts for 18 rounds from the round in which you chanted the password. There's no limit to the number of times it can be used, but if the same person uses it more than once a day , he or she will suffer a -1 penalty to their agility bonus thereafter. This penalty is cumulative . If you use it 3 times, it becomes -2 , and if you use it 4 times, it becomes -3 . If you don't use the ring for one full day, the penalty will disappear. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09669))_

### 《 Rings 》 / [ Thermal Ring ]

- This ring has the effect of keeping the wearer's body temperature constant. The wearer of the ring can feel comfortable even when exposed to the fierce heat of a desert or the cold weather of a snowy mountain. However, it's not effective against too high or low temperatures or sudden changes in temperature, so it has absolutely no effect against fire or cold produced by magic . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09676))_

### 《 Rings 》 / [ Linkage Ring ]

- When characters of the same race and opposite gender wear these rings, they can freely use each other's mental points. However, if one person has a spell, etc., that affects the mind cast on him or her, it'll affect both people. These rings will not work unless both people are within 1 km. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09698))_

### 《 Rings 》 / [ Rune Ring ]

- Those who wear this will gain a +1 bonus to magic power of ancient magic. This ring also has the ability to act as a normal magical catalyst. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09705))_
