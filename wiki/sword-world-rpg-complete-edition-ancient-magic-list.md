# Ancient Magic List

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- the strike roll can only be made with the strike power set to 5, regardless of the weapon's own strike power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- This spell can also be cast directly on a living creature. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- This bonus is effective on any mental power resistance roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- Anything within the area is affected, friend or foe, but targets that make a successful resistance roll will be completely unaffected by the spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- Resist= Negated effect This spell alters the air in a space within a 5 meter radius from the target, creating a gas with the effect of instantly putting any creatures inside to sleep. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- Those that fail to resist fall asleep, but since it's a natural sleep, they will wake up immediately if there's any noise, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- Also, if they're dealt physical or mental damage, if they receive a shock such as being hit or kicked even if it does not deal damage, or if an extremely loud noise is made, they will automatically wake up without the need for a resistance roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- You also cannot find things whose form you can't see directly, such as things inside a box. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- However, it's not possible to discern exactly what magical powers are at work. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- By casting this spell, a normally invisible magical aura becomes visible to the caster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- This lets you know if there are magical items, magical traps, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68)
- Expansion=Duration, Distance, Target volume Resist=None This can be used on any single point in space or on any object, canceling out the light in a space within a 10 meter radius from that point, completely closing it in darkness. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.68-69)
- [ Decrease Weight ] Base Mental Power Cost=3 Distance=10 meters Area=One object Duration=18 rounds Effect=Reduce the weight of an object Type=Lost Counters (Negates): Increase Weight Countered (Negated) By: Increase Weight Expansion=Duration, distance, targets, effect (see description). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- This spell is used on a single object and has the effect of reducing the target's weight. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- the required strength can become -2 or -3 .... (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- This spell's effect can be expanded. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- For triple , it' d be 0.9 x 0.9 x 0.9 = 0.721 times . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- Also, in this case, required strength can only be reduced to 1. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- Any spells cast on the targeted person, object, or space will be removed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- Effect=Removes the effects of other spells cast on the target Type=Ritual Counters (Negates): All magic except curses and non-removable Expansion=Final score, distance, targets or area Resist=None This spell acts as a negative force against all other spells and can remove their effects. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- This is a spell used on flammable objects several centimeters in size, causing them to ignite. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- It has almost no effect on monsters either. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- The effect of the spell will disappear in an instant, but the object should burn on its own afterwards. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- This spell's effect only works on highly flammable objects such as oil, dead grass, and sawdust, so it's completely useless if used on live trees or non-flammable materials. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- However, it has no effect at all on spells that are offensive but do not deal damage to life force. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- This spell has the effect of giving magic resistance to objects that do not have it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- If the targeted object is exposed to another spell that's not detection type, it will resist that spell unless the opponent succeeds on a success roll against a target score equal to the final score from when Magic Protection was cast. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- If the resist fails, the spell will take its full effect, and furthermore the Magic Protection spell itself will be removed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- For example, if you cast Kindle on a bundle of straw that has Magic Protection cast on it, if Kindle cannot break through the protection of Magic Protection (the final score is insufficient), it won't be able to ignite. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- In this case, the spell will take no effect at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- This spell is powerless against spells that belong to detection . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- Therefore, objects with this spell cast on them will respond to Sense Magic . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- Also, if you cast it on something that naturally has resistance (humans, fae, youma, etc.), it will have no effect at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.69)
- It has no effect at all when used on living things. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
- Using a single point as a light source, a space with a 10 meter radius is filled with white magical light. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
- The further away you are from the light source, the more the effect of the light will fade. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
- This spell is used on an object or a point in space. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
- Type=Target score Countered (Overpowered) By: Unlock Expansion=Final score, duration. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
- This password does not have to be in high ancient. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
- This spell can be used on objects that open and close, such as doors, treasure chest lids, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.70)
