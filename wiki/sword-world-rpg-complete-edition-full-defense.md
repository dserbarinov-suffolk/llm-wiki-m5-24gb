# Full Defense

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- When focusing on defense, an adventurer cannot attack or use any magic during that round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.264)
- In return, you'll gain a +2 bonus to your evasion speed . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.264)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When an opponent is too strong, and fighting normally is of no use, an adventurer can focus wholly on defense .
```
