# 5.1.1 Types of Magic

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In Sword World , there is a power called magic that can produce various mysterious effects by chanting words called runes and performing certain procedures at the same time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- The five runes are high ancient , silent spirit , holy prayer , daemon scream , and dragon roar . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- There are five types of runes in the world of Sword World . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- This difference in runes is at the same time a difference in the type of magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- Each has a different source of magical power and a completely different procedure (although holy prayer and daemon scream are essentially the same power, the only difference being the nature of the magic). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- In addition to these runes, there are two others that, although weak, can still manifest magical power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- They are called common runes and spellsongs , respectively. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
