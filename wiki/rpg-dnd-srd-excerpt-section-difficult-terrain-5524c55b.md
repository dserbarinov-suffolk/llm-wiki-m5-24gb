# Difficult Terrain

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Combat rarely takes place in bare rooms or on featureless plains. Boulder-strewn caverns, briar-choked forests, treacherous staircasesthe setting of a typical fight contains difficult terrain. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00276))_
- Every foot of movement in difficult terrain costs 1 extra foot. This rule is true even if multiple things in a space count as difficult terrain. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00277))_
- Low furniture, rubble, undergrowth, steep stairs, snow, and shallow bogs are examples of difficult terrain. The space of another creature, whether hostile or not, also counts as difficult terrain. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00278))_
