# Attributes

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- - Player Characters have just three Attributes: ( Strength (STR) , Dexterity (DEX) , and Willpower (WIL) ). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00101))_
- Their risk is simply higher. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00103))_
- A character with a low STR is not necessarily hopelessly weak; they can still attempt to lift a heavy door or survive a deadly fight! _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00103))_
- - Attributes are not universal descriptors. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00103))_
