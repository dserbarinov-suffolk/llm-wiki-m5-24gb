# Surprise

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- In these situations, one side of the battle gains surprise over the other. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00248))_
- Any character or monster that doesn't notice a threat is surprised at the start of the encounter. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00249))_
- The GM determines who might be surprised. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00249))_
- If neither side tries to be stealthy, they automatically notice each other. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00249))_
- A member of a group can be surprised even if the other members aren't. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00250))_

## Technical atoms

> If you're surprised, you can't move or take an action on your first turn of the combat, and you can't take a reaction until that turn ends.
_(source: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00250))_
