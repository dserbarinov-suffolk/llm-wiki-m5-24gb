Source: [[javascriptallonge]]

## Statements

- Many objects in JavaScript can model collections of things. (javascriptallonge.pdf p.206)
- But sometimes you want to open it up and do things with its contents. (javascriptallonge.pdf p.206)
- Acting on the elements of a collection one at a time is called iterating over the contents , and JavaScript has a standard way to iterate over the contents of collections. (javascriptallonge.pdf p.206)
