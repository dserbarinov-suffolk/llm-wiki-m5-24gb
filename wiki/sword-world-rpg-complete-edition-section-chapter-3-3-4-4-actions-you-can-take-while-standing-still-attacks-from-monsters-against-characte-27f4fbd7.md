- Effect=You can negate the power of detection spells and items, and know the type (effect) of spell or item, the one who cast it, their whereabouts, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02117))_
- If you cast this spell, you can know if you or something you're wearing is being detected by a magical item or spell. At the same time, it will also reveal the type (effect) of spell or item used on you, as well as the clear identity of the person who used it (not just knowing their name, but what they look like). After that the effect of the Counter Sense spell vanishes (although you can recast it, of course). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02121))_
- This spell targets spells of the detection type, and items such as crystal orb of clairvoyance that have the same effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02122))_
- Also, the effects of detection items and spells, no matter how long their duration, will vanish after 1 round due to Counter Sense activating. In other words, for 1 round, the opponent's detection spell or item will work normally, and information of what happened during that time will be told to the opponent. They'll also discover that you have used the spell Counter Sense . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02123))_
- Distance=Caster Area=Caster Duration=Until focus ends Effect=Makes the caster invisible to others Type=Focus, illusion Expansion=Final score Resist=Negated effect This spell has the effect of completely concealing the caster's presence from others. In other words, not only will they become invisible, you will no longer be able to hear sounds made by the caster, and of course you won't be able to smell them. However, you can feel their presence if you touch them. This spell cannot be cast on others. The duration lasts as long as your focus continues. If you take any action that breaks your focus, the effect of this spell vanishes immediately. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02127))_
- This is a spell that allows the caster to change their form into another living creature at will. However, the creatures they can change into are limited to about 10 times or 1/10th of the caster's weight. You can think of the maximum as a 6meter tall titan and the minimum as a medium-sized cat. The caster can cancel the spell's effect at any time, but once it's canceled, you cannot take on that form again unless you cast the spell again. You can only change into humans, fae, youma, titans, and animals, and only those the caster knows in detail (you must succeed on a roll such as the sage skill's monster check ). You can never change into something whose origins are not clearly known, such as magical beasts, mythical beasts, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02137))_
- Only the caster's body will change shape, the clothes and accessories they're wearing do not change. They will fall off or break in the process of changing. - By casting this spell, the caster will be able to converse in a language previously unknown to them. This spell only allows you to speak one language each time you cast it, so in order to be able to speak two or three unknown languages at the same time, you must expand the effect of the spell. Also, this spell only allows you to use languages used by intelligent creatures listed on Table 12-1: Languages (see p. 159), so that means you will not be able to speak to animals, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02160))_
- Type=Focus, target score the target's Expansion=Final score, distance Resist=Negated effect This is a spell that completely seals your opponent's movements. Those who fail to resist will lose their freedom and immediately become rigid on the spot. A target caught by this magic in an unstable state will likely fall down. If it were a bird, it would fall out of the sky. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02167))_
- To maintain this spell, the caster must keep focus. Someone who's had this spell cast on them may only attempt to resist the first time the spell is cast. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02168))_
