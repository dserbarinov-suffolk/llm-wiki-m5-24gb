# Work Requests to NPCs

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- You found a beautiful gemstone and would like to have it forged into a ring. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.168)
- As you progress through your adventures, such things will happen. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.168)
- Also, if there's no one available to do this for you, there is of course no point in paying any amount of money. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.168)

- If the task becomes a little more complex, use numbers such as x 40 or x 50 instead of x 30. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.168)
