# [ Divine Armor ] (Spell Level=7)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Even if the caster is already wearing armor, their defense power does not stack (the defense power of the armor you were wearing will be nullified). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- There is no required strength, and it does not hinder the caster's movement at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- This armor appears to glow vaguely. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
