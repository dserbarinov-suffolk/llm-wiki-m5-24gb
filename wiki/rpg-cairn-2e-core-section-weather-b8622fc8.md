# Weather

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- Each day, the Warden should roll on the weather table for the appropriate season. If the " Extreme " weather result is rolled twice in a row, the weather turns to " Catastrophic ". A squall becomes a hurricane, a storm floods the valley, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00322))_
