# Remove

What [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]] covers about remove:

## Statements

### Chapter 6: / 6.1 Precautions When Using Skills / Thief Skill / [ ⑮ Treasure Appraisal [Intelligence] ] (No equipment restriction, not retriable) / [ ⑰ Remove/Set Trap [Dexterity] ] (Equipment restriction, not retriable)

- You can attempt to remove traps discovered through fi nd . Removal also involves attempting a success roll against the trap's difficulty. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03992))_

- Unless the game master has specifically set traps that are easy to find but difficult to remove , or conversely, difficult to find but easy to remove , the difficulty of finding and removing should be the same. If you fail, the trap will be activated if the dice roll was 6 or more beneath the target score. The effect of an activated trap depends on the type of trap. Traps that you fail to find cannot be removed. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03993))_

- For set , you can set traps up to a difficulty equal to your thief skill level without failing (you cannot set traps of a higher level). If a third party attempts to sense , find , or remove a trap set by a player, the game master must attempt a success roll on their behalf. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03994))_
