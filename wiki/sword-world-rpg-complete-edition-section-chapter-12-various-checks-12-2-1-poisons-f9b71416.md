## Statements

- Just like in our world, in Alecrast, using poison as a weapon is an immoral act. If a player character uses poison to defeat an enemy, they are sure to be treated as a coward and looked down upon by the public. Therefore, a proud warrior does not want to use poison. On the other hand, assassins, evil rune masters, dark priests, etc. do like to use poison. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05413))_
- To remove a poison with magic, the caster's final score (magic power + 2D roll) must be greater than or equal to the poison's toxicity score . Once a detox fails, the same caster cannot attempt detox magic until their magic power increases (and even if they try, they will always fail). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05415))_
- Even if you detoxify someone after they die from poison, they will not come back to life. Some poisons have symptoms similar to illnesses, but they are not natural illnesses and cannot be cured with Cure Disease . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05416))_
- The spirit magic Purification can make liquid poison harmless if the final score when casting the magic is greater than or equal to than the toxicity score. However, it's not possible to drive off a poison that's already entered the body. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05417))_
- Poison details are expressed as follows. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05420))_
- This is an alcoholic beverage made by fermenting barley. Drinking a little can help lighten your mood and relieve stress, but drinking too much can leave you drunk (in this state, you suffer a -4 penalty on all success rolls). Y ou may also experience headaches and nausea after a certain period of time. Some adventurers, other than dwarves, have extremely low tolerance for alcohol, and such characters cannot resist this poison (?) no matter how high their adventurer level. It is up to the player to decide whether or not to set their character this way. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05424))_
- The usage of the poison is written in parentheses after the name. These are divided into injected , inhaled , dose , and touch . Injected poisons are poisons that need to be delivered directly into the bloodstream, usually in liquid form. It's applied to small daggers and needles and used as traps on doors and treasure chests. It can be applied to knives and arrows to increase their killing power, but the poison usually wears off after about three uses and loses its effect, so it must be reapplied frequently. Many creatures, such as giant scorpions, poisonous snakes, etc. use injected poison as a weapon. This type of poison only takes effect if you lose 1 or more points of life force when you are hurt by a poisoned blade. Even if the blade hits, if it doesn't cause a wound, it has no effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05426))_
- Inhaled poisons are in gas or powder form and take effect when inhaled through the respiratory tract. This is also often used as a trap, but another method is to store it in a breakable bottle and use it by slamming it into your opponent. In rare cases, some creatures use poison gas as a weapon. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05427))_
- If you're trapped in a room filled with poison gas, you must make a resistance roll every round. If you hold your breath, you will not be affected by the gas, but of course you will not be able to speak or cast spells while holding your breath. An adventurer can only hold their breath for a number of rounds equal to their life force. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05428))_
- Dose poison is used by mixing it into food or drink and comes in powder or liquid form. It is mainly used for assassinations, so adventurers will barely use it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05429))_
- Touch poison comes in liquid or powder form and can penetrate through the skin. There are not many types of them, and as expected they are often used as traps. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05430))_
- Poisons are sometimes labeled as magic , mind , or damage , depending on how they affect you. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05432))_
- Those that say magic are poisons that have magical effects. These are basically magical items, so they respond to the ancient magic called Sense Magic . Magical poisons and their effects can be removed with Dispel Magic as well as Purification , Cure Poison , etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05433))_
- Those that say mind are poisons that affect the target's mind. The holy magic Sanity is effective. The poison's effect also vanishes when another effect appears due to magic that belongs to mind . Poisons that are both magic and mind are equivalent to magic that has a target score . The target score of a poison is the same as its toxicity score (described later). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05434))_
- Those that say damage are poisons that damage life force (points), but there are common rules for these types of poisons. Each poison has its own unique strike power and bonus damage. If this type of poison enters your body, you will suffer this damage. Make a strike roll as you would for any magic that deals damage. If you roll 10 or greater, it becomes a critical. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05435))_
- Those affected by the poison can reduce this damage. For characters, the score is adventurer level, and for monsters, it's monster level. After deducting this score, if 1 or more damage remains, your life force (points) will be subtracted by the amount of damage that remains. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05436))_
- Each poison has a toxicity score . When the poison enters the body, make a life force resistance roll (for monsters, a life point resistance roll) using the toxicity score as the target score. If you fail, the effect will appear, but if you succeed, the effect will only appear incomplete or won't appear at all. The toxicity scores listed here are just examples; by diluting the substance, the toxicity score can be made as low as desired, and by concentrating it, it can be made somewhat greater. When you cast the holy magic Cure Poison on someone who has been poisoned, compare the final score when casting the magic to this toxicity score. If the final score is equal to or greater than the toxicity score, the poison can be removed by the magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05441))_
- This represents how famous the poison is. The higher this score, the rarer the poison. An adventurer must make a success roll using sage skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score, and if they succeed, they will know about the poison. You can also guess the name of the poison when you see the symptoms of the poisoned person. If your success roll is 4 or more above the rarity , you can also know how to make the poison and its antidote (if it has one). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05443))_
- However, even if you know how to make it, whether or not you can make it is another matter. Some poisons require very rare ingredients. If a player declares that they want to make a poison (or antidote), the game master is responsible for deciding which materials are needed. The time it takes to mix a poison (or antidote) varies from one hour to one week. Also, poisons with magical effects can only be mixed by sorcerers. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05444))_
- This is the time it takes from when the poison enters the body until it begins to take effect. Some poisons have an immediate effect, while others take many hours to take effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05446))_
- It represents the time from when the poison's effect appears until it disappears. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05448))_
- After basic data is presented, a detailed description is provided. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05450))_
- Typical poisons include the following. There are many other types of poisons as well. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05453))_
- This is the most terrifying poison used for assassination. This is because this poison is completely transparent, tasteless, and odorless, so it'll never be noticed if it's mixed into alcohol or food. If you fail to resist, you will die, but if you succeed, there'll be no harm at all. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05457))_
- This is a sweet-smelling liquid that is mixed into sweets, etc. Those who fail to resist become young children. Their ability scores are all divided by 6 (rounded up). If you are detoxed, you will recover (grow) within a few days, but unless you are detoxed, you will have to act with the same physique and mentality as a child. This poison does not have the effect of reversing a character's age, and may cause them to die of old age while remaining a child. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05461))_
- This is a hallucinogenic poison. Those who fail to resist will be attacked by hallucinations starting on the next round. Enemies may appear to have multiple parts, allies may appear to be monsters, or the surrounding scenery may appear to have changed. The victim suffers a -4 penalty on all checks while this condition lasts. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05465))_
- This is so named because its effect is similar to the Forget magic used by gremlins. Those who fail to resist will have no memory of their past, and will be unable to use any abilities related to intelligence (those that use intelligence bonus to calculate a baseline score). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05469))_
- A poison said to have been invented by a wise centaur. There is a myth that it's made from the blood of centaurs, but it's actually made from the fruit of certain plants. It' s used in assassinations by soaking it into items that are close to the body, such as underwear, etc. It's naturally a red liquid, but if you soak it in a cloth and then wash it, it becomes colorless. Even if you wash it, only the color will come off, and the poisonous ingredients will remain on the cloth. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05474))_
- What's more, this poison is completely harmless at room temperature, and only becomes toxic when warmed by body heat. Those wearing cloth soaked with centauri blood will experience intense pain and heat after one hour. Once that happens, you can no longer strip it from your body or wash it off, and it will continue to deal damage every hour from then on. The damage is a terrifying strike power 30 plus bonus damage 12. A successful resistance roll will reduce the damage. This poison is said to have killed a famous hero. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05475))_
- It has this name because its effect is similar to the spirit magic Sleep , but it's also called black lotus because it's made from black lotus seeds. The strong flower-scented gas will put anyone who fails to resist into a state of sleep. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05479))_
- This is a black liquid poison and is a type of narcotic. Those who fail to resist will lose all will and emotion and become like a doll. If someone pulls their arm, they will stagger, and if someone calls them, they will respond in an emotionless voice, but they cannot do anything of their own will. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05490))_
- Around one additional hour of suggestions can transform the victim into a robot that follows commands. Victims turned into robots faithfully follow the commands of the person who gave them the suggestions, and will casually kill even their family and friends (they cannot use magic). However, if you suffer a very strong physical or mental shock, you may recover from the paralysis even before the duration expires. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05491))_
- Effect=Become disabled, and suffer strike power 20 damage plus bonus damage 6 every 10 minutes. Resist negates effect Toxicity Score=15 Rarity=10 Effect Start Time= 1 round later Effect Duration=Permanent _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05493))_
- A poison often used by assassins, it is a pitch-black viscous liquid. If you are wounded by a knife coated with this poison, you will suffer damage every 10 minutes thereafter. The victim continues to suffer damage until the poison wears off or they die. During this time, you will be in terrible pain and unable to take any action. If treatment is delayed, you may remain in a coma for several days after the poison wears off. If you succeed on a resistance roll, you will not suffer any damage. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05494))_
- This is a liquid poison. Those who fail to resist will stop breathing and their heart will stop, making them appear completely dead. However, they're actually in a state of suspended animation and will return to normal after 12 hours. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05498))_

- Effect=Gives happy dreams Toxicity Score=10 Rarity=10 Effect Start Time= 6 rounds later Effect Duration=1 hour _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05504))_

There are several types of Beast Maker , including ones that turn you into a pig, ones that turn you into a dog and ones that turn you into a horse. Particularly powerful ones can turn you into a mythical beast such as a scylla, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05515))_

- This is a viscous poison and is used by smearing it on doorknobs, etc. Those who touch this poison and fail to resist will be struck with intense fear. Determine its effect using Table 5-5: Fears (see p. 89). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05533))_
- This is a small granular poison, and if someone drinks it and fails to resist, they will become a woman if they're a man, and a man if they're a woman. There are no other penalties. Additionally, there are no changes to the magic that shamans can use. If you drink it again, you can return to normal. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05538))_

Those who fail to resist will fall passionately in love with the first thing they see (regardless of gender or race). The only people you can fall in love with are fae, youma, titans, and mythical beasts that have human body parts (centaurs, etc.); you cannot fall in love with undead, animals, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05552))_
