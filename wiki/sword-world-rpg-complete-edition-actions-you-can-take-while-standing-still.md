# Actions You Can Take While Standing Still

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Although stationary, you do not have to remain completely still; you can move up to 3 meters . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.38)
- In this state, all actions you can take during normal movement described in the previous section can be performed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.38)
