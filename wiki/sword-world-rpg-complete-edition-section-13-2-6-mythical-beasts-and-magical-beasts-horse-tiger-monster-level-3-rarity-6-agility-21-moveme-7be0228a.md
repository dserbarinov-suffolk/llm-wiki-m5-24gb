## Statements

- Tigers are fierce, carnivorous beasts that usually act alone. Despite their large size, they possess the graceful and flexible movements characteristic of the cat family, and are among the strongest of all ordinary animals. If they're hungry, they may also attack humans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-07028))_
