# 3.4.2 Actions You Can Take

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Full movement means that a character runs without looking aside. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- Naturally, you can advance farther, but the restrictions on your actions will also be greater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- At this time, attacks from the enemy are also much more likely to hit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- The distance you can go with full movement is character's agility x 3 meters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- For monsters, their movement speed is set separately from agility, so they can move up to movement speed x 3 meters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
