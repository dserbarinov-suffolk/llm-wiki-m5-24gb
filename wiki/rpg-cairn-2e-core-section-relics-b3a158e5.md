## Statements

- When a character first acquires a Relic they are not familiar with, they can either spend time learning more about it (using a Downtime action) or experiment with it. The latter carries some dangers and may even require a successful WIL save to avoid negative consequences. However, it is often more interesting to allow the player to ask as many questions as they like about it and (eventually) to simply tell them how it works . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00554))_
