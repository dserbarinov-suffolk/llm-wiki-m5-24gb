## Statements

- There are several cases in which a normal life form becomes undead. Those that have had their mental power (points) taken away by other undead (wights, lesser vampires, etc.), those created by magic (skeleton, zombies, etc.), those created by their own will (haunts, undead kings, etc.), and there are also things such as ghouls whose causes are still unknown. In either case, if you become undead, your soul will become abnormal and you will not be able to be resurrected even if Resurrection is used normally. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03626))_
- This spell saves the souls of those who have become undead. If the undead agrees, the spell is almost guaranteed to succeed (with the exception of double ones), but otherwise you must attain a final score greater than or equal to the undead's mental point resistance. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03627))_
- If the spell is successful, the undead will cease all activity at that point. If you then cast Resurrection , it is also possible to restore their life. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03628))_
- One priest can only attempt one Save Soul against a given undead. If you fail, you cannot try again until your magic power increases. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03629))_
- This spell is cast immediately after an individual's death. The soul of the target of this spell will be reborn as a baby in another location within one week. Reborn babies have their ability scores preserved (they have the same ability scores after becoming adults). As the baby grows up, at a certain moment it'll remember its former personality. At that stage they will be able to use all their previous memories and skills. When they remember their previous personality depends on the final score from when the spell was cast (see Table 5-8: Reincarnation Memory). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03635))_
- Even before a person regains their personality, if someone who knew them well before reincarnation can identify their body after reincarnation, they can try to restore the person's old memories. You can help them recover their memory through constant supervision, so you can roll 2D once every year, and if you roll a 10 or greater, they will be able to regain their personality. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03636))_
- Those that have had their mental power (points) taken away by other undead (wights, lesser vampires, etc.), those created by magic (skeleton, zombies, etc.), those created by their own will (haunts, undead kings, etc.), and there are also things such as ghouls whose causes are still unknown. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03626))_
- If you then cast Resurrection , it is also possible to restore their life. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03628))_
- One priest can only attempt one Save Soul against a given undead. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03629))_
- This spell is cast immediately after an individual's death. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03635))_
- Reborn babies have their ability scores preserved (they have the same ability scores after becoming adults). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03635))_
- The soul of the target of this spell will be reborn as a baby in another location within one week. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03635))_
