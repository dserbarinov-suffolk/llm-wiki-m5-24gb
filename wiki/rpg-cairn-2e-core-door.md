# Door

## Statements

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Dungeon Elements

- Doors and entryways may be locked, stuck, or blocked entirely. Characters can try to force a door open (or wedge it shut) using available resources (spikes, glue) or through raw ability. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00296))_

- Doorway : The door to the Storeroom has nearly rotted away, leaving cracks in the wood. The iron chain is clearly rusted. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00618))_
