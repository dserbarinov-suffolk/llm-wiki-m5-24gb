## Statements

- Strike power modifier is a modifier added to a weapon's strike power . In the basic rules, a weapon's required strength is its strike power, but with this rule, a weapon's strike power equals weapon's required strength plus this strike power modifier . In other words, even if the required strength is the same , one can say a weapon with a higher strike power modifier will deal greater damage to an opponent. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10042))_
