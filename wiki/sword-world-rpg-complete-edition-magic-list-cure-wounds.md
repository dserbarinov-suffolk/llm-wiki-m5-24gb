# Magic List [ Cure Wounds ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Only one check needs to be made. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- This spell has the effect of instantly healing any wound. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- When using the Rating Table , the critical process is not performed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- However, even in this case, no critical will occur, and if the undead successfully resist, it'll have no effect at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- This spell can also deal damage to undead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- This has no effect on magical creatures that do not have normal life, such as skeleton warriors, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- [ Sanity ] Base Mental Power Cost=3 Distance=Touch Area=One Duration=Instant Effect=Restore the target's peace of mind Type=Mind Expansion=Final score Resist= Negated effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- As a result, you can remove the effects of spells that affect the mind of a target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- If this fails, the spell's effect will not be removed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- This spell has the effect of returning the target's mind to its normal state. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- Emotional tensions caused by the target's personal reasons can also be removed, but unless the cause is eliminated, they will likely return to their original state. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- If you have multiple targets, make success rolls to determine the effects for each one. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- However, a success roll check is required, and if you fail, there will be no effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- This spell creates a space that acts as a negative force against undead, and has the effects as shown on Table 5-6: Turn Undead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- If expanded , make an equivalent number of checks on the Turn Undead table, then apply which result the caster finds most desirable. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- This spell's effect can be expanded. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.99)
- For example, if the caster spends 10 points of mental power with this spell, 9 points will be added to the target's mental power, excluding the base mental power cost of 1 point. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.100)
- This spell is a spell that transfers the caster's mental power (points) to another person as it is. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.100)
- Even those who have lost their consciousness with 0 mental power can be recovered with this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.100)
- Please note that this spell cannot transfer more mental power (points) than the target's starting mental power (points). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.100)
