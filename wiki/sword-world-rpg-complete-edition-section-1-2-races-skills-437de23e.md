## Statements

- In addition to ability scores, Sword World RPG also uses numeric values called skills to describe the characteristics of characters. Skills have a greater impact on a character's actions than ability scores. This chapter explains the basics of skills. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00202))_

## Statements by subsection

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 1.3.1 Skill Basics

- There are several types of skills, which are divided into several groups. For each skill, a character's proficiency is represented by a level from 0~10. 0 levels indicates that someone is completely unrelated to that skill, while 10 levels represents the highest proficiency that a normal human can attain. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00204))_
- Skills are referred to by names such as fighter , thief , merchant , etc. As you can see, they have a strong connotation of professional job functions rather than skills. It is also possible for one character to acquire (have levels in) more than one skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00205))_
- Those who have these skills are often referred to by their skill names. So instead of someone who has the fighter skill , they are simply called fi ghters . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00206))_
- Half-elves have a longer life span than humans due to their elven characteristics, with a life span of around 200 years. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00207))_

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 1.3.3 Adventurer Level

- In Sword World RPG, a character's capability as an adventurer is determined by their adventurer skill level. The highest level among the adventurer skills that a character has is called that character's adventurer level . For example, a character with priest skill level 1, fighter skill level 4 (these are adventurer skills), sailor skill level 5, and fisher skill level 2 (these are general skills) would be at adventurer level 4 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00219))_
