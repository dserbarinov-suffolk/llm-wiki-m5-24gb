### Saving Throws

- The result of a successful or failed saving throw is also detailed in the effect that allows the save. Usually, a successful save means that a creature suffers no harm, or reduced harm, from an effect. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-4449ec9f-00247))_
