## Statements

- Distance=Caster Area=Caster Duration=Until focus ends Effect=Makes the caster invisible to others Type=Focus, illusion Expansion=Final score Resist=Negated effect This spell has the effect of completely concealing the caster's presence from others. In other words, not only will they become invisible, you will no longer be able to hear sounds made by the caster, and of course you won't be able to smell them. However, you can feel their presence if you touch them. This spell cannot be cast on others. The duration lasts as long as your focus continues. If you take any action that breaks your focus, the effect of this spell vanishes immediately. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02217))_
