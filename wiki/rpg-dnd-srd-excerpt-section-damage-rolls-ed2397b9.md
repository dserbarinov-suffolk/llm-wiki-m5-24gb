# Damage Rolls

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- With a penalty, it is possible to deal 0 damage, but never negative damage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00414))_
- You roll the damage die or dice, add any modifiers, and apply the damage to your target. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00414))_
- Magic weapons, special abilities, and other factors can grant a bonus to damage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00414))_
- A spell tells you which dice to roll for damage and whether to add any modifiers. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00415))_
- For example, when a wizard casts fi reball or a cleric casts fl ame strike , the spell's damage is rolled once for all creatures caught in the blast. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00416))_
