# 2.5.2 How to Make a Difficulty Check

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This roll is then added to the difficulty and used as the target score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- To make a difficulty check , the game master also rolls the dice (2D). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- The game master does not reveal their roll nor the target score that results from their calculation, to the players. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- Whether the base difficulty is revealed or not depends on the situation. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- They will be told the baseline score, roll 2D, and modify it with any bonuses or penalties to find a final score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- The players' dice rolls are otherwise the same. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- The game master then takes the final score and compares it to the target score (difficulty + 2D) that they have secretly set. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- If there is a tie or if the final score is greater, the action is a success ; if it is less, the action is a failure . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- They must only answer as much as the character can tell from their successes and failures. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- The game master does not say whether the result is the action succeeded or the action failed , because a difficulty check is made when the success or failure of the action is not known to the characters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
- In the case of #2, if you assume there are no traps, you will have a terrible time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.32)
