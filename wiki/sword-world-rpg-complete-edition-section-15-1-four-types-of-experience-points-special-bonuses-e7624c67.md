## Statements

- The game master is free to award bonus experience for particularly outstanding play. However, this should be done as sparingly as possible. Giving away a large amount of experience points is harmful to the enjoyment of the game. It'd be most appropriate to keep it to about 1/10th of the experience points earned from accomplishing a mission. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10006))_
