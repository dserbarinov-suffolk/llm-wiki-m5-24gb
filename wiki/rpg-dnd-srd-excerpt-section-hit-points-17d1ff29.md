## Statements

- Hit points represent a combination of physical and mental durability, the will to live, and luck. Creatures with more hit points are more difficult to kill. Those with fewer hit points are more fragile. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00410))_
- A creature's current hit points (usually just called hit points) can be any number from the creature's hit point maximum down to 0. This number changes frequently as a creature takes damage or receives healing. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00411))_
- Whenever a creature takes damage, that damage is subtracted from its hit points. The loss of hit points has no effect on a creature's capabilities until the creature drops to 0 hit points. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00412))_
