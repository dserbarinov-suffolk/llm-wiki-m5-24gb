# Attack Power Modifier

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Attack power modifier indicates the modifier added to an adventurer's attack power when using that weapon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.262)
- Weapons with higher numbers are more likely to hit an opponent, while weapons with smaller numbers are less likely to hit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.262)
