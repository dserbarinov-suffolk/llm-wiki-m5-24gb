# Restrained

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- - A restrained creature's speed becomes 0, and it can't benefit from any bonus to its speed. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00588))_
- - Attack rolls against the creature have advantage, and the creature's attack rolls have disadvantage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00589))_
- - The creature has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00590))_
