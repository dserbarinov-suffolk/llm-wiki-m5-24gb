## Statements

- As a major premise, large ships (fishing ships, trading ships, battleships, etc.) cannot be operated by one person. For these ships, at least half of the crew must have the sailor skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04355))_
- In order to perform delicate maneuvers such as surviving a storm , avoiding a reef , and ramming an enemy ship , the person at the helm must succeed on a success roll using their sailor skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score. At this time, the captain can also give instructions to the helmsman. In this case, make a success roll using the captain's sailor skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score. Even if a large ship fails, it will not sink immediately, but it will likely suffer some damage. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04356))_
