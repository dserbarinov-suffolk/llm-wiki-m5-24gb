## Key supported claims

- Logical operators rely on truthiness and falsiness rather than strict true/false values. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.99-99)
- ! is a logical operator that always returns true or false. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.99-99)
- The ternary operator (?:), ||, and && are control flow operators that do not always return true or false and have short-cut semantics. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.99-99)
