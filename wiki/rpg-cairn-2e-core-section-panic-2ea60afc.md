# Panic

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- A WIL save is typically required to avoid losing control and becoming panicked . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00290))_
- - A panicked character must make a WIL save to overcome their condition as an action on their turn . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00291))_
- - A panicked character has 0 HP , does not act in the first round of combat, and all of their attacks are impaired . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00292))_
