# 1st Level Dragon Roar Magic List [ Iron Stomach ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Your opponent will no longer attack you unless they're enraged or extremely hungry. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.150)
- You will be able to communicate with reptiles such as snakes and lizards, as well as lizardmen. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.150)
- This spell cannot give any binding commands to the opponent. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.150)
