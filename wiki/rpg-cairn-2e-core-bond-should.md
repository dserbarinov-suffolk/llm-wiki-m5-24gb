# Bonds Should...

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about bonds should...:

## Statements

- Give a character an item (often petty ) or a unique ability. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00645))_
- Encourage character engagement with the implied setting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00646))_
- Flesh out a character's history without relying on events tied to specific Backgrounds. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00647))_
- Provide a character with a quest, opportunity, or plan of action. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00648))_
