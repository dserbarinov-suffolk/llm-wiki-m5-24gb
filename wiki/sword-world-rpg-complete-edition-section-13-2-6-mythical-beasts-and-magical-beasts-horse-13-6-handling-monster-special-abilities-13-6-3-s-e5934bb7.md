## Statements

- Some monsters bring about disease. The spirit power of a monster is balanced differently than that of a human, and can always cause illness. However, in most cases the infection power of this information imbalance is weak, so it doesn't pose much of a problem, but if a character is bitten by a bat or rat, it's highly contagious, so there's a high chance he or she will become ill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08788))_
- When you're dealt damage by a monster with this ability, roll 2D and compare it with the progression intensity of each illness. If your roll is less than the progression intensity, you will be infected with the disease. Illnesses can be treated using the holy magic Cure Disease and Refresh , and the spirit magic Restore Health . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08789))_
- For detailed rules regarding illnesses, please refer to 12.2.2: Illnesses (see p. 164). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08790))_
