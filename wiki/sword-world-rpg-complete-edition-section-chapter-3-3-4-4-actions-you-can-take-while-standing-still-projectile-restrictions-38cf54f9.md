## Statements

- There are some restrictions when handling projectiles such as bows and javelins. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01165))_
- One is their range . There is no bow that has infinite reach. The maximum of shooting weapons such as bows and arrows- those marked shooting on the Weapons table (see p. 21) --is 100m , and of throwing weapons such as spearsthose marked throwable or made for throwing on the Weapons table --is 20m . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01166))_
- If an enemy is beyond this distance, the projectile will not hit them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01167))_
- In addition, projectiles cannot be used in melee. When there's an enemy in front of you, you can never use a projectile marked shooting or made for throwing . Weapons marked as throwable can also be used as melee weapons, but not as projectiles. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01168))_
- Again, movement is also restricted when using projectiles. A character must choose stand still as their movement weapon + shield + armor or two-handed weapon + armor. Of course, one-handed weapon + armor, two-handed weapon without armor, etc. are also possible, and it is acceptable to carry a spare weapon on your waist or back, as long as it is within the bounds of common sense. However, you may not hold two weapons in both hands or hold a shield in each hand-- it is fine to do so, but a shield in your dominant hand or a weapon in your non-dominant hand is of no use. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01169))_
