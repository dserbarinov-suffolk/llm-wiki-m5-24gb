# 10th Level Basic Dark Magic List [ Call God ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- However, humans' frail bodies and minds cannot withstand the great power of a god, so the sacrifice to the spell is destroyed, and you will not be able to bring them back to life again. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- Even wicked gods who have lost contact with the material world will be able to use their full power if summoned with this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- This spell is almost the same as the holy magic of the same name, except for its duration and the fact that it summons a god (wicked god) into the body of a sacrifice rather than the caster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- This spell requires a final score of 30 or greater while also defeating the sacrifice's resist for it to be fully effective. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- Even if you defeat the sacrifice's resist, if you do not attain a final score of 30 or greater, the dark god's summoning will only last for 1 minute. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- Their priest skill level is a reference for how close the sacrifice is to a god, but there are some humans who are close to a god regardless of their skill level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- This spell has a bonus added to the final score depending on the person being sacrificed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- Each game master is responsible for determining the suitability of sacrifices, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
- For example, it is for this reason that innocent girls are preferred as sacrifices. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.147)
