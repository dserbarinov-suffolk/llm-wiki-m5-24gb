### Actions You Can Take While Standing Still

- Although stationary, you do not have to remain completely still; you can move up to 3 meters . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01163))_
- In this state, all actions you can take during normal movement described in the previous section can be performed. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01164))_

### Use Magic and Projectiles

- Above all, the advantage of standing still is the ability to use magic and attack with projectiles. Conversely, if you are not standing still , you cannot use magic or projectiles. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01166))_
- If you want to attack with a projectile, please follow the process of actions you can take during normal movement . If you chose stand still , then attack with that weapon can be read as attack with that projectile . However, when you're engaged (being attacked by a melee weapon), you cannot use projectiles. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01167))_
- If you chose stand still , then attack with that weapon can be read as attack with that projectile . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01167))_

### Stand Up and Ready Yourself

- From a state of falling down, stand up and ready yourself. You can only ready one weapon, etc. that you're wearing. You cannot pick up any items on the ground. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01170))_
- You can only ready one weapon, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01170))_

### Weapon and Armor Restrictions

- A character's arms are largely divided into weapons, shields, and armor. Required strength should not be added up, but rather considered separately for each of these. In other words, a character with 14 strength can wield a weapon with a required strength of 14, wield a shield with a required strength of 14, and wear armor with a required strength of 14. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01179))_

### Weapon Combat Skills / 【 Fighter Skill 】

- The fighter skill is the most basic skill for weapon combat. In the following explanation, we will focus on this skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01186))_

### Weapon Combat Skills / 【 Thief Skill 】

- A thief also has the ability to fight, but only with light equipment. Specifically, you can only fight if the weapon or armor you're using requires up to half (rounded up) of your character's strength. As for armor, it's limited not only by required strength but also by type . Metal armor other than ring mail may not be used, even if the required strength (half of strength) is met. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01189))_
- A thief also has the ability to fight, but only with light equipment. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01189))_

### Projectile Restrictions

- There are some restrictions when handling projectiles such as bows and javelins. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01192))_
- One is their range . There is no bow that has infinite reach. The maximum of shooting weapons such as bows and arrows- those marked shooting on the Weapons table (see p. 21) --is 100m , and of throwing weapons such as spearsthose marked throwable or made for throwing on the Weapons table --is 20m . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01193))_
- If an enemy is beyond this distance, the projectile will not hit them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01194))_
- In addition, projectiles cannot be used in melee. When there's an enemy in front of you, you can never use a projectile marked shooting or made for throwing . Weapons marked as throwable can also be used as melee weapons, but not as projectiles. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01195))_
- Again, movement is also restricted when using projectiles. A character must choose stand still as their movement weapon + shield + armor or two-handed weapon + armor. Of course, one-handed weapon + armor, two-handed weapon without armor, etc. are also possible, and it is acceptable to carry a spare weapon on your waist or back, as long as it is within the bounds of common sense. However, you may not hold two weapons in both hands or hold a shield in each hand-- it is fine to do so, but a shield in your dominant hand or a weapon in your non-dominant hand is of no use. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01196))_
