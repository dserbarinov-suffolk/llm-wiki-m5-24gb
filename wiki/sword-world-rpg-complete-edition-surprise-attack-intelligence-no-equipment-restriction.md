# [ ⑭ Surprise Attack [Intelligence]] (No equipment restriction)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The ranger skill is used for surprise attacks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- This is basically the same ability as that of the thief skill, but it only applies to traps that use outdoor nature. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- Indoor mechanical traps cannot be found/sensed with the ranger skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- Indoor mechanical traps cannot be removed or set . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
