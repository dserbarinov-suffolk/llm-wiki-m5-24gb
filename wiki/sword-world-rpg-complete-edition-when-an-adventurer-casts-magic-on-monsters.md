# When an Adventurer Casts Magic on Monsters

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Lightning is a spell that shoots a bolt of lightning, dealing damage to everything in its path. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.59)
- Suppose there are three goblins (each with a mental point resistance of 9) and one goblin lord (mental point resistance 12) in this space. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.59)

<a id="atom-2"></a>

```
If there are multiple targets in the path of the lightning bolt, you must make a check for each one's attempt to resist.
```

