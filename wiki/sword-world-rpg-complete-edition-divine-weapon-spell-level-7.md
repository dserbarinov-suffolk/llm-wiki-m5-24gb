# [ Divine Weapon ] (Spell Level=7)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Resist=None When you cast this spell, a vaguely glowing weapon-like force field appears in the caster's hand. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- with this spell, arrows are automatically generated as well. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- Also, it's not possible to give this weapon to someone else. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- This spell has no effect if the caster already has something else in their hand. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- There is no required strength, and its strike power is treated as 30 (if the caster uses it as a twohanded weapon, its strike power is 35 ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
