- You will be able to communicate with reptiles such as snakes and lizards, as well as lizardmen. This spell cannot give any binding commands to the opponent. If the opponent's intelligence is high enough, you can negotiate, but if the opponent's intelligence is not sufficient, you can only know about the opponent's emotions and degree of hunger. However, the risk of being attacked by the opponent is reduced. Your opponent will no longer attack you unless they're enraged or extremely hungry. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05018))_
