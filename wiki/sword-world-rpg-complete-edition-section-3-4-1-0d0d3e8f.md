## Statements

- The following actions can be taken at any time , regardless of the movement method. They do not prevent other actions at all. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01118))_

## Statements by subsection

### Drop a Weapon, Etc. On the Ground

- Weapons and shields can be dropped on the ground at any time , regardless of whether they're one-handed, twohanded, or both-handed. If you do drop a weapon, etc. consider it as if you never had it in the first place , then choose your action. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01120))_

### 3.4.2 Actions You Can Take

- Full movement means that a character runs without looking aside. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01123))_
- Naturally, you can advance farther, but the restrictions on your actions will also be greater. At this time, attacks from the enemy are also much more likely to hit. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01124))_
- The distance you can go with full movement is character's agility x 3 meters. For monsters, their movement speed is set separately from agility, so they can move up to movement speed x 3 meters. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01125))_
- Full movement means that a character runs without looking aside. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01123))_

### Ready a Sheathed Weapon

- You can wield up to two, one in each hand. If you want to switch your weapon from a projectile such as a bow, to a melee weapon, you can drop your previous weapon on the ground, and then you can ready a new weapon. This is because, from the beginning, it' s assumed your hands were empty. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01128))_
- However, this is limited to items that are already worn and ready to go, such as those kept in a sheath. You cannot pick up a weapon that was left on the ground. If you're carrying a shield on your back, you can also ready it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01129))_
- This is because, from the beginning, it' s assumed your hands were empty. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01128))_
- However, this is limited to items that are already worn and ready to go, such as those kept in a sheath. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01129))_

### Switch a Weapon From Hand to Hand

- You can move something in your right hand to your left hand, or hold something you had in one hand with both hands. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01131))_
- Some weapons can be held in either one or both hands. If you have such a weapon in one hand and a shield in the other, if you drop the shield, the weapon is now considered to be in only one hand, so you can then hold it with both hands. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01132))_

### Sheath a Weapon, Etc.

- You can sheath up to two, one in each hand. However, this is only possible if there is a sheath, etc. Y ou can also place a shield on your back. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01134))_
- However, this is only possible if there is a sheath, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01134))_

### Withdraw (Flee) From Engagement

- You can escape from an opponent within melee attack range. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01136))_
- If an attack is declared on a character who is withdrawing, another character is needed to suffer the enemy's attack in their place (defend against the attack). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01137))_
- If you do not have a companion to take on your opponent, you will escape with your back completely turned, and you will suffer a -4 penalty when evading the attack. Also, in that case, regardless of your character's agility, they will only escape at the end of the round (agility 0, based on action order). In other words, there is always a chance for the enemy to attack a character before they withdraw. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01138))_
- You can escape from an opponent within melee attack range. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01136))_
- In other words, there is always a chance for the enemy to attack a character before they withdraw. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01138))_
- Also, in that case, regardless of your character's agility, they will only escape at the end of the round (agility 0, based on action order). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01138))_
