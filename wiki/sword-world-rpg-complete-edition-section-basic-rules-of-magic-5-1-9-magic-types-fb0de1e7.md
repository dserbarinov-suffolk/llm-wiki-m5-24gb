## Statements

- Some magic can nullify the effects of other magic. For example, the ancient magic Dispel Magic nullifies the effects of most magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01689))_
- Most magic is automatically nullified if a nullifying spell is used, but there are some that are not. This is magic that has a target score . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01690))_
- When casting any magic, no matter what kind of magic it is, you must roll 2D to check whether an automatic failure (double ones) will occur or not. If you're trying to nullify magic that has a target score with another spell, you must use that other spell's 2D roll to determine your final score. This final score equals magic power + 2D roll (similar to a final score for other success rolls). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01691))_
- If this final score is greater than or equal to the target score originally set for that first magic that was cast, then that first magic can be nullified. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01692))_
- This target score equals the final score of the first magic that was cast (in other words, the magic you're about to nullify). Therefore, when an adventurer uses magic that has a target score , that player should note down that score and remember it. However, if you feel this is cumbersome, or if you forget to make a note, then when it becomes necessary (nullifying magic has been used), you can roll 2D on the spot to determine a final score = target score . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01693))_
- When magic is cast against other magic, their effects may completely cancel each other out. For example, there are two kinds of ancient magic, Decrease Weight (which makes an object lighter) _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01697))_
- and Increase Weight (which makes an object heavier), but when both of these are cast on the same object at the same time, they will cancel each other out and disappear without taking effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01698))_
- Such magic, that is, magic that negates the effect of other magic, but at the same time is negated by that other magic without taking its own effect, is called counters (negates): type. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01699))_
- When casting counters (negates): magic to cancel the effect of certain magic, if the magic that was cast prior has a target score , then according to the definition of magic with a target score , the magic that's cast after cannot cancel the prior magic unless its final score is greater than or equal to the determined target score . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01700))_
- When certain magic is cast against other magic, it can cancel the effect of the prior magic but still take its own effect afterwards. This is magic of the counters (overpowers): type. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01702))_
- For example, the ancient magic Darkness can nullify the effects of the spirit magic Will-O-Wisp and still close the surrounding area in darkness, which means Darkness itself is more effective. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01703))_
- However, this is often not automatic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01704))_
- One, is when the target resists . In the case of magic that does not take effect if resisted, if the target does resist it, the magic will not take its effect, and the effect of the prior magic will continue. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01705))_
- The other, is when the prior magic has a target score . In this case, according to the definition of magic with a target score , the magic cast after will not take effect unless its final score is greater than or equal to the determined target score . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01706))_
- If the magic cast prior has a target score and the magic cast after is resistible , please follow the procedure below to make a check. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01707))_
- ② Next, the one casting the magic must roll 2D to find their final score (by adding their magic power). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01709))_
- ③ Compare target score in ① with fi nal score in ② . If the final score does not reach the target score, the magic will not be cast. This ends here. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01710))_
- ④ If fi nal score in ② is greater than or equal to target score in ① , the target may attempt to resist . The resist target score equals the fi nal score in ② . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01711))_
- ⑤ If the target successfully resisted in ④ , the new magic cast will not take effect. The effect of the prior magic will continue. If the target failed to resist , the new magic cast will take effect and the effect of the prior magic will be negated . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01712))_
- Counters (overpowers): magic does not mean that certain magic is definitely stronger than other magic. It's possible for magic A to counter (overpower) magic B, and at the same time for magic B to counter (overpower) magic A. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01713))_
- Also, all magic counters (overpowers) itself. Therefore, if certain magic takes effect, and you cast that same magic on the same target multiple times, the effect will not be cumulative. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01714))_
- This is magic that affects the target's mental state. This kind of magic can only take effect on one target at a time. If magic of the mind type is cast on a target, and other magic also of the mind type is cast on that target, the new magic will take effect, and the prior magic will be nullified . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01716))_
- In other words, all mind magic counters (overpowers) other mind magic. Note that many are resistible magic, and some have target scores . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01717))_
- This is magic that affects the target's physical abilities (strength, etc.). Magic of this type can only take effect on humans , fae , and youma . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01719))_
- Also, with magic of the physical ability type, two or more cannot affect the same target at the same time. Please resolve them as counters (overpowers) magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01720))_
- Magic that targets a weapon and enhances it. Two or more of this kind of magic cannot affect the same weapon at the same time. Please resolve them as counters (overpowers) magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01723))_
- Weapons that have been given power by ancient magic, silver weapons, or mithril silver weapons are assumed to already have non-removable (see below) weapon enhancement magic cast on them. Therefore, that power cannot be nullified, so even if you cast new weapon enhancement magic, it'll be useless . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01724))_
- Some magic, such as Dispel Magic (ancient magic) and Remove Curse (holy magic), can be used as counter magic that removes other magic. However, magic of the non-removable type cannot be removed by other magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01726))_
- Although, the ancient magic Perfect Cancellation is able to remove nonremovable magic. Also, sometimes there is counter magic that counters certain magic, which if specified, will be removed by that counter magic. It's also possible to remove non-removable magic by directly using the power of a god (wicked god) with the holy/dark magic Call God . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01727))_
- Dispel Magic (ancient magic) can be used to remove most magic, but for magic of the curse type, this is not possible. However, Remove Curse (holy magic) can be used to remove it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01729))_
- Magic has a fixed duration . Magic of the focus type is that in which the magic's effect lasts as long as the caster (the one who casts the magic) is focused . While focusing, the caster cannot take the following actions: _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01734))_
- Even while focusing, if another attacks you with a weapon, etc. you can still attempt to evade it. If magic is cast on you, it's also possible to resist it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01739))_
- Also, when the target is no longer within the magic's area of effect (out of reach, out of sight, etc.), the magic will lose its effect, even if you continue to focus. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01740))_
- If the caster suffers damage to their life force or mental power, their focus will be interrupted and the magic's effect will be lost . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01741))_
- If the caster wishes to voluntarily release their focus (in order to take another action), they must declare it at the beginning of the round, when no one else has made their action announcements yet. If your focus is interrupted due to damage, etc. your focus will be interrupted at the timing in which the damage is dealt. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01742))_
- A spell that deceives the five senses of the target. These spells deceive the opponent's sensory organs and give them false sensations. There are three types of senses through which monsters perceive their surroundings: fi ve senses , pseudo senses, and magic senses. Magic of the illusion type is effective on those that perceive their surroundings with fi ve senses or pseudo senses. Humans and fae perceive their surroundings through fi ve senses , so they can all be targeted by illusions . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01744))_
- If the target does not have the senses to deceive, this spell will have no effect at all. It is completely ineffective against monsters who perceive their surroundings through magic senses, and illusions that deceive vision are useless against monsters who lack vision. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01745))_
- The effects of spells of the illusion type can be detected by a resistance roll. However, in order to do this, you must first be suspicious of the illusion. The game master should decide in which cases it seems suspicious. In the case of player characters (adventurers), this may be when the players themselves suspect an illusion. For NPCs and monsters, the game master should decide based on their intelligence. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01746))_
- For example, suppose a fake floor is created above a pit using a Create Image (ancient magic) spell. No matter how smart you are, you're not likely to notice this. But what if whoever was walking at the front of the line had the misfortune to step on top of it and fall? _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01747))_
- No matter how foolish you are, you should suspect that the floor is an illusion. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01748))_
- It might be unreasonable for a monster with low intelligence, such as a goblin, but a human of average intelligence would find it suspicious. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01750))_
- In the event of such a suspicion, the target of the illusion can make a mental power resistance roll. The target score is the final score from when the illusion spell was cast (if you forgot, roll the dice and determine it again). If you're successful, you'll be able to see through the illusion and know the correct situation. However, you can only make this resistance roll once . Once you fail, no matter how suspicious it may be, you won't be able to detect its true form (of course, you can walk around suspicious floors). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01751))_
- Magic of the ritual type can increase its effect (final score) due to carefully preparing and performing a ritual. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01753))_
- With these spells, you can add +1 to your final score for each day you perform the ritual. If additional casters who can use the same spell also participate in the ritual, you will gain +1 to your final score for each one. Even humans who cannot use spells can participate in the ritual. In this case, you can gain +1 to your final score for every 10 people (rounded down). However, the ritual procedures are very complicated, so those who have not been taught the procedure in advance will not be able to participate in the ritual. If it's ancient magic, the caster must have been taught at a school such as an academy of sages , etc. and if it's holy (dark) magic, they must be a believer. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01754))_
- These bonuses are stacked in the form of days x participants . For example, suppose a priest performs a Remove Curse ritual. If the ritual takes two days (+2), one extra priest who can also use Remove Curse participates (+1), and 30 believers participate (+3), the final score bonus will be 2 x (1 + 3) = +8 . Fractions of the number of days must be rounded down , so if you can't spend a full day, the final score bonus will be 0 , regardless of how many participants there are. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01755))_
- Unlike magic expansion, there is no limit to the increase in final score through rituals. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01756))_
- A ritual must be performed without sleep or rest, and is usually limited to 2 days. If someone drops out during the ritual, their number will not be considered at all. In the previous example, if 10 believers drop out, even if it's right after the ritual begins, just before the ritual ends, or at any timing in between, the final score bonus will be 2 x (1 + 2) = +6 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01757))_
- The person who makes the success roll is the one who takes the center role in performing the ritual. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01758))_
- Among ancient magic, there are some spells known only to a limited number of sorcerers, and spells that were lost with the fall of the ancient kingdom. These are called lost spells . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01761))_
- The game master gets to determine which spells are considered lost spells. It's acceptable to have all spells on the magic list be lost, or conversely, to have all magic be known and no spells be lost. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01762))_
- If a spell says lost under its magic details, it may be easier to maintain balance when enjoying the game if you consider it a lost spell. Unless the game master has a particular reason, please consider these to be lost spells. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01763))_
