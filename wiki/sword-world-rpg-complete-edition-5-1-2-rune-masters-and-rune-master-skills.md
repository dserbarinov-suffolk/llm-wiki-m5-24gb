# 5.1.2 Rune Masters and Rune Master Skills

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Rune masters are divided into five types according to the type of magic (runes) they use. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- In Sword World , all those who use magic utilizing runes are called rune masters . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- Sorcerer uses ancient magic Shaman uses spirit magic Priest uses holy magic Dark priest uses dark magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- and dragon priest uses dragon roar magic . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- Those with the sorcerer skill can use ancient magic and are called sorcerers . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- Each corresponds to the skill for which it is named. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- However, this also has some limitations. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- Some rune masters are fluent in multiple runes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- One, is that some races may not be able to learn some or all of the runes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- These are as explained earlier under 1.2: Races (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- The other, is that you cannot have more than one each of holy, dark, and dragon roar magic at the same time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- A character cannot hold more than one of these beliefs at the same time. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
- These types of magic are obtained through belief in gods , wicked gods , and dragons , respectively. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.56)
