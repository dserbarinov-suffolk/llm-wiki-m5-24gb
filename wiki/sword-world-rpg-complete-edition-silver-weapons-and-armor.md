# Silver Weapons and Armor

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Silver is a special metal, imbued with magical powers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- It is also one of only two types of metal that's known to exist in the spirit world . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Even silver weapons and armor have no differences in their strike power, defense power, attack power, or evasion speed at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Also, shamans are not normally able to wear metal armor (as metal armor prevents them from using spirit magic), but they can use spirit magic while wearing it, if it's silver armor . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- To buy silver weapons and armor in a store, you must be in a town about the size of the royal city level in a small country. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Silver weapons and armor will also cost 5 times the original price. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- Y ou can also ask for a high-quality item of silver, but the price will be even higher . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
- For example, the price of a silver item with a required strength 2 times lower than the original would be 5 x 2 = 10 times the price of the original. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.52)
