## Key supported claims

- Talhaiarn Cataguen 94 and contemporaries like Taliesin and Bluchbard were prominent in British poetry. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- Eadfered Flesaurs reigned for twelve years in Bernicia and Deira, and founded Bebbanburg. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- Penda, son of Pybba, reigned ten years and separated the kingdom of Mercia from the North-men. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- The tradition of Brutus may be older than Geoffrey or Tyssilio, but this is uncertain due to potential interpolations. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- A wall from Penguaul to the mouth of the river Cluth and Cairpentaloch was ineffective. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
