From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- - If a PC takes damage outside of combat, they should instead receive damage to an Attribute, typically STR. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00224))_
- If their DEX is reduced to 0, they are paralyzed. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00225))_

- If their WIL is reduced to 0, they are delirious. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00225))_
