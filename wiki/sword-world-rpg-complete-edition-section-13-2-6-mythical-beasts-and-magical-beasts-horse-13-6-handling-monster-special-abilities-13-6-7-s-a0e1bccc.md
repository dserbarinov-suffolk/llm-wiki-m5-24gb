### 13.2.6 Mythical Beasts and Magical Beasts / [ Horse ] / 13.6 Handling Monster Special Abilities / 13.6.7 Special Defense Abilities: Weaknesses / 《 Damage From Healing Magic 》

- Undead that act with negative life force will suffer damage from healing magic that grants positive life force. They will suffer damage from Cure Wounds , and if Healing or Refresh are cast on them and they fail to resist, their life points will immediately become 0. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08801))_
