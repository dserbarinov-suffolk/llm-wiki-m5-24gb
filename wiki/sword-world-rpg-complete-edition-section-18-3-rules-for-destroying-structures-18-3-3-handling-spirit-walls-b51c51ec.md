### 18.3 Rules for Destroying Structures / 18.3.3 Handling Spirit Walls / [ Spirit Wall: Gnome ]

- Treat it as a stone wall. Without expansion, defense points are 15 and destruction points are 20. Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's stone. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10594))_
- Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's stone. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10594))_

### 18.3 Rules for Destroying Structures / 18.3.3 Handling Spirit Walls / [ Spirit Wall: Frau ]

- Treat it as an ice wall. Without expansion, defense points are 15 and destruction points are 20. Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's ice. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10596))_
- Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's ice. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10596))_

### 18.3 Rules for Destroying Structures / 18.3.3 Handling Spirit Walls / [ Spirit Wall: Dryad ]

- Treat it as a vegetation wall. Without expansion, defense points and destruction points are both 15. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10598))_
- Spirit Wall: Dryad has the power to regenerate even if it's destroyed. Each round, Spirit Wall: Dryad regenerates 1 defense point and 1 destruction point. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10599))_
