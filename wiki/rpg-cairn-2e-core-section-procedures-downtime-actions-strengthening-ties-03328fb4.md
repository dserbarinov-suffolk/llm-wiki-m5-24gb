## Statements

- A character fosters a connection with an NPC or Faction in the game world. First, they must identify the entity with whom they wish to strengthen ties, as well as a specific intent (e.g., building trust, mending a friendship, seeking membership in a Faction, forming an alliance, and so on). The Warden then provides concrete measures (described as Milestones and Costs ) that the PC can undertake to advance the relationship. With each completed Milestone , the Warden describes how the PC's relationship has grown or changed. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00423))_
