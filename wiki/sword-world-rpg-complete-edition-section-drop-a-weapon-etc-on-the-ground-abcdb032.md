# Drop a Weapon, Etc. On the Ground

From [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]].

## Statements

- Weapons and shields can be dropped on the ground at any time , regardless of whether they're one-handed, twohanded, or both-handed. If you do drop a weapon, etc. consider it as if you never had it in the first place , then choose your action. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01092))_
