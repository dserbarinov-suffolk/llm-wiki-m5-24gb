## Statements by subsection

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Neutrality

- The Warden's role is to act as a neutral arbiter and portray the rules, situations, non-player characters (NPCs), and narrative clearly. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00009))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Classless

- A character's role or skills are not limited by a single class. Instead, the equipment they carry and their experiences define their specialty. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00011))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Death

- Characters may be powerful, but they are also vulnerable to harm in its many forms. Death is always around the corner, but it is never random or without warning. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00013))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Fiction First

- Dice do not always reflect an obstacle's difficulty or its outcome. Instead, success and failure are based on in-world elements and arbitrated by the Warden in dialogue with the players. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00015))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Player Choice

- Players should always understand the reasons behind the choices they've made, and information about potential risks should be provided freely and frequently. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00019))_

### Overview & Principles / Overview / Shared Objectives

- Players trust one another to engage with the shared setting, character goals, and party challenges. Therefore the party is typically working together towards a common goal, as a team. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00023))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Players / Teamwork

- - Stay on the same page about goals and limits, respecting each other and accomplishing more as a group than alone. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00031))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Information

- Provide useful information about the game world as the characters explore it. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00052))_
- Players do not need to roll dice to learn about their circumstances. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00053))_
- Be helpful and direct with your answers to their questions. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00054))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Preparation

- The game world is organic, malleable and random. It intuits and makes sharp turns. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00062))_
- Use random tables and generators to develop situations, not stories or plots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00063))_
- NPCs remember what the PCs say and do, and how they affect the world. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00064))_
- NPCs don't want to die. Infuse their own self-interest and will to live into every personality. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00065))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Narrative Focus

- Emergent experience of play is what matters, not math or character abilities. Give the players weapon trainers and personal quests to facilitate improvement and specialization. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00067))_
- A dagger to your throat will kill you, regardless of your expensive armor and impressive training. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00069))_
- - Pay attention to the needs and wants of the players, then put realistic opportunities in their path. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00068))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Danger

- Telegraph serious danger to players when it is present. The more dangerous, the more obvious. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00072))_
- Give players opportunities to solve problems and interact with the world. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00074))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Treasure

- A Treasure is specific to the environment from where it is recovered. It tells a story. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00076))_
- Treasure is highly valuable, almost always bulky, and rarely useful beyond its worth and prestige. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00077))_
- Relics are not Treasure, though they are useful and interesting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00078))_
- Use Treasure as a lure to exotic locations under the protection of intimidating foes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00079))_

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Choice

- Give players a solid choice to force outcomes when the situation lulls. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00081))_
- Use binary "so, A or B?" responses when their intentions are vague. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00082))_
