## Statements

- This is an ability that is used by undead monsters collectively referred to as haunts. The targeted character must make a mental power resistance roll against the indicated target score. If you fail, your body will be possessed. A haunt who has possessed a body can also use that character's abilities. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08764))_
