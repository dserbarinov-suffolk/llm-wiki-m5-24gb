## Statements

- Critical target variation indicates that the armor reduces the chance of being dealt a strong blow from some weapons while wearing it. If this field says yes , the opponent's critical target will be +1 when they attack you with a sword, spear, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10214))_
