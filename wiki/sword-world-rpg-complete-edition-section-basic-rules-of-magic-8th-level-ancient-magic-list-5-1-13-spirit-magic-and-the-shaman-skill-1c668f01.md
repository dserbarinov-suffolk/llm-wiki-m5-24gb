- The basic rules for casting magic are the same, but the following conditions are required in order to use the shaman skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02670))_
- It must be in a place where the wind blows in its natural state. The spirit power of wind does not work inside buildings, deep caves, or even underground passages. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02678))_
- This is a power that's at work in places where the ground is exposed or underground. Particularly strong spirit power is at work in the mountains. Since the power of water spirits is strong on the ocean floor and the bottoms of lakes, the spirit power of earth does not work there. Likewise, this spirit power does not work on the floors of buildings (whether they're stone or wood). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02680))_
- The spirit power of water is at work wherever there is water, such as the ocean or a lake. Even if you carry water in a water bottle, etc., if you open the lid, the spirit power will work. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02682))_
- This spirit power is at work wherever there's fire burning. You can also use fire from a torch you're carrying with you. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02684))_
- This spirit power is at work wherever there is snow or ice. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02686))_
- This spirit power is at work wherever there are living things with functioning minds. Of course if the caster him or herself is like that, there's no problem. Some of these types of spirits include those that control the nature of the mind, and those that control fluctuation of emotions. It's said that if one emotion has too much of an outburst, it can cause phenomena such as alienation or complete loss of functions of other spirits, which can lead to mental disorders. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02688))_
- Very strong power is at work in forests, etc. but in places where plants and trees grow, they're only at work in small amounts. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02690))_
- These are the spirits that control light, darkness, and life. Explanations of where these are at work can be found under each individual spell. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02692))_
- Shamans have the following basic abilities, which they can use without any restrictions. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02695))_
- Salamander and frau control fire and ice, as well as heat and cold. Infravision is the ability to see things dimly even in the dark and find places where the temperature is unusually different compared to the surrounding area, by sensing the workings of these spirits. However, you cannot see colors at all. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02697))_
- This ability is used to know what kind of spirit power is at work in your surroundings. You cannot use spells that belong to a specific spirit in places where that spirit power is not at work. When a player declares that they will use this ability, the game master must tell the player what spirit power is at work in that location. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02699))_
- Since there are spirits of life and spirits of undeath (which control negative life force), the sense aura ability can be used to distinguish between living things and undead. Ordinary corpses and monsters created by magic (magical creatures) such as gargoyles do not emit either aura. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02700))_
- Sense aura is a subtle sensation, more like what we can smell than see . Even if you know that spirit power is at work, you won't be able to tell where that spirit power is coming from unless you get very close. Also, if there's extremely strong spirit power in your surroundings, you may not be able to sense weak spirit power at work. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02701))_
- These are runes used to communicate with spirits. Those who can use these runes can communicate with any spirit. Most spirits speak directly to the mind without using words, but when a caster gives commands to a spirit, they must speak these runes out loud. Also, some fae are well versed in silent spirit. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02703))_

5.1.13 Spirit Magic  and the Shaman Skil
Silent spirit are runes used to communicate with spirits and draw out the  power  controlled  by  those  spirits. The skill for using spirit magic is called the shaman skill , and those who have this skill  are  called shamans .  The  shaman skill  allows you to use all basic abilities and spirit  magic  up  to  the  caster's  skill level.
