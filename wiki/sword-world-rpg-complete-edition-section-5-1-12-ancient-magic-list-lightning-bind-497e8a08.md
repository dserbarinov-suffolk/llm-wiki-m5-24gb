### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / [ Wraith Form ]

- A caster who casts this spell will be able to separate their spiritual body from their physical body, allowing only their ethereal body to move around freely. This ethereal body is called a wraith. Wraiths are a kind of undead, but they can resume their life activities once they return to their own bodies. If you do not (or cannot) return to your original body after the spell's duration has expired, you will lose complete contact with your body, and must remain adrift for eternity as a wraith. The same applies if your body is destroyed (your life force or life points become 0 or less) while you are a wraith. If the wraith itself is destroyed, the caster dies. In this case, the body becomes nothing more than an empty husk without a soul, so it cannot be brought back to life by any means. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02645))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 9th Level Ancient Magic List [ Command Golem ] / [ Petrification

- Those who fail to resist this spell, no matter what they're made of, will instantly be turned to stone. Only creatures with natural life (humans, plants, animals, mythical beasts, magical beasts, fae, youma, and daemons) can be targets of this spell, with the exception of magical creatures such as spirits, undead, and golems. ### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 10th Level Ancient Magic List [ Dimension Gate ]

- This spell creates a gate between two distant places, allowing you to instantly move to a destination. The gate is a silver disc 5 meters in diameter, which appears on the floor. A disc of the same shape will also appear above the destination. The disc can be penetrated like water, and those who jump into it will jump out of the other disc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02718))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 《 Infravision 》

- Salamander and frau control fire and ice, as well as heat and cold. Infravision is the ability to see things dimly even in the dark and find places where the temperature is unusually different compared to the surrounding area, by sensing the workings of these spirits. However, you cannot see colors at all. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02787))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Lightning Bind ] / 《 Sense Aura 》

- This ability is used to know what kind of spirit power is at work in your surroundings. You cannot use spells that belong to a specific spirit in places where that spirit power is not at work. When a player declares that they will use this ability, the game master must tell the player what spirit power is at work in that location. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02789))_
- Since there are spirits of life and spirits of undeath (which control negative life force), the sense aura ability can be used to distinguish between living things and undead. Ordinary corpses and monsters created by magic (magical creatures) such as gargoyles do not emit either aura. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02790))_
- Sense aura is a subtle sensation, more like what we can smell than see . Even if you know that spirit power is at work, you won't be able to tell where that spirit power is coming from unless you get very close. Also, if there's extremely strong spirit power in your surroundings, you may not be able to sense weak spirit power at work. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02791))_
- Even if you know that spirit power is at work, you won't be able to tell where that spirit power is coming from unless you get very close. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02791))_
