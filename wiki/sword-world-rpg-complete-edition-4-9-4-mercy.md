# 4.9.4 Mercy

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- On Table 1-8: Weapons, maces , clubs , and staves are classified as dull weapons. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.51)
- However, even if an item is classified as one of these, if you throw it, you cannot declare mercy. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.51)
- However, to do so, their weapon must be a dull weapon (a bludgeoning weapon). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.51)
