## Statements

- This is what's called a love potion . It tastes and smells like alcohol, and takes effect not only by drinking it but also by smelling it (if it's only inhaled, its toxicity score is 9). Those who fail to resist will fall passionately in love with the first thing they see (regardless of gender or race). The only people you can fall in love with are fae, youma, titans, and mythical beasts that have human body parts (centaurs, etc.); you cannot fall in love with undead, animals, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05590))_
- The recipe for love passion has now been completely lost, so it's no longer possible to make it. There are only a few left from the ancient kingdom period, and they are traded at high prices. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05591))_
