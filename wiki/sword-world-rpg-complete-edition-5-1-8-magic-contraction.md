# 5.1.8 Magic Contraction

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- However, this does not reduce the amount of mental power consumed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- Magic can also be contracted as opposed to expanded. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- The shape (usually a circle) cannot be changed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- You also cannot cast it in such a way that it hits the enemy directly in the eyes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- Again, the shape cannot be changed, and you cannot directly aim it at the enemy's eyes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- You may only set it to wear off at the end of a round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- You must also decide how long you want the magic to last before you cast it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- Magic with a duration can be optionally shortened. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- Magic that's already been cast cannot be canceled at will. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- You must declare how many points you'll reduce before you roll the dice. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- Also, if the side casting the magic rolls double sixes, the resisting side will also need double sixes, no matter how much magic power was declared to be decreased (if it's a monster, it cannot resist). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- Magic power can be optionally reduced. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- All magic that can expand their effects can also contract their effects. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.62)
- These characteristics are presented here as magic types . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- The key number is , of course, the indicated strike power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- Conversely, If you roll double ones, it does not deal any damage at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- Spells of the damage type always have a clearly defined strike power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- Also, at this time, no matter how high you roll during your strike roll, a critical will not occur. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- In this case, you must make the strike roll after subtracting 10 from strike power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- In either case, the caster's magic power can be added as bonus damage to the result of the strike roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- Also, the target's adventurer level (or the monster's monster level) is subtracted from the damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- However, there's an exception if you roll double ones during your strike roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)
- At this time, if the target successfully resists, you cannot make a strike roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.63)

5.1.8.2
Combined Expansion and Contraction
Although  it's not  often  done,  it's possible  to  perform  magic  expansion and contraction  at  the  same  time.  For example,  you  could extend the  area  of effect  while reducing magic  power.  The mental  power  required  at  this  time  is decided by expansion . It cannot be reduced by contraction. For example, if you double the  area and reduce  magic power by -4 , the mental power consumed will still be double .
