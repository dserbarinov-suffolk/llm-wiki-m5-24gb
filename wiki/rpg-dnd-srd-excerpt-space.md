# Space

What [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]] covers about space:

## Statements

- A typical Medium creature isn't 5 feet wide, for example, but it does control a space that wide. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00316))_
- A creature's space is the area in feet that it effectively controls in combat, not an expression of its physical dimensions. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00316))_
- For that reason, there's a limit to the number of creatures that can surround another creature in combat. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00317))_
- Assuming Medium combatants, eight creatures can fit in a 5-foot radius around another one. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00317))_
- Because larger creatures take up more space, fewer of them can surround a creature. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00318))_
- In contrast, as many as twenty Medium creatures can surround a Gargantuan one. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00318))_

## Technical atoms

> Context: Because larger creatures take up more space, fewer of them can surround a creature. If four Large creatures crowd around a Medium or smaller one, there's little room for anyone else. In contrast, as many as twenty Medium creatures can surround a Gargantuan one.
_(context: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00318))_

> If a Medium hobgoblin stands in a 5-foot wide doorway, other creatures can't get through unless the hobgoblin lets them.
_(source: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00316))_
