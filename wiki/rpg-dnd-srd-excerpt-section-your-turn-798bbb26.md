# Your Turn

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- On your turn, you can move a distance up to your speed and take one action . You decide whether to move first or take your action first. Your speed- sometimes called your walking speed-is noted on your character sheet. The most common actions you can take are described in the "Actions in Combat" section later in this chapter. Many class features and other abilities provide additional options for your action. The "Movement and Position" section later in this chapter gives the rules for your move.
