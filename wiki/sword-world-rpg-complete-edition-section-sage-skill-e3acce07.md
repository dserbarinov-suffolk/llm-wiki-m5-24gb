## Statements

- There is a close connection between the sorcerer skill and the sage skill. The sorcerer skill is the mastery of ancient through study and the improvement of magical ability, while the sage skill is knowledge gained primarily through the study of ancient books. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04534))_
- These two skills have something in common in that their basics are the study of ancient as well as ancient books. In fact, many sorcerers are excellent sages, and the sorcerers' guild is also called the academy of sages . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04535))_
- For this reason, a character can spend fewer experience points only when acquiring both the sorcerer and sage skills. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04536))_
- When a character who already has the sorcerer skill acquires the sage skill at the same level or lower, or when a character who already has the sage skill acquires the sorcerer skill at the same level or lower, the amount of experience points spent can be reduced depending on the skill level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04537))_
- Suppose a character has a 3rd level sorcerer skill and a 1st level sage skill and increases his sage skill to 2nd level. At this time, the newly acquired sage skill level 2 is lower than the already acquired sorcerer skill level 3 , so he can acquire it for fewer experience points.
