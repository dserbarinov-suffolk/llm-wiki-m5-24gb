# 9th Level Dragon Roar Magic List [ Summon Lesser Dragon ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Although greater than a wyvern, a lesser dragon's intellect is also not very high, so it can only be used to fight enemies or to carry you on its back. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Lesser dragons have super strength, so this restriction is not a matter of weight, but of space for riding. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Up to two adult humans can ride on the back of a lesser dragon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
