# Price Calculation

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Weapons and armor have different prices for the same shape, depending on the required strength. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.23)
- There you will find expressions like x20+40 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.23)
- The required strength is applied to this formula to find the actual price. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.23)
