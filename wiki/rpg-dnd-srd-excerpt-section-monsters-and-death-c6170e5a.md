From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Most GMs have a monster die the instant it drops to 0 hit points, rather than having it fall unconscious and make death saving throws. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00447))_
- Mighty villains and special nonplayer characters are common exceptions; the GM might have them fall unconscious and follow the same rules as player characters. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00448))_
