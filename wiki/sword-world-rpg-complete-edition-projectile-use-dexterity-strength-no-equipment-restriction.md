# [ ⑫ Projectile Use [Dexterity, Strength] ] (No equipment restriction)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The ranger skill is treated the same as the fighter skill (attack power, bonus damage modifiers) only when attacking with projectiles. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- The ranger skill is not relevant to the use of or defense from melee weapons. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- When using the thief skill for defense, you must be lightly armed (required strength of weapons and armor must be half your own strength or less). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
