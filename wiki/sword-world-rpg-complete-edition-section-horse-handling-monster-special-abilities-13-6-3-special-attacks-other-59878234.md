### [ Horse ] / Handling Monster Special Abilities / 13.6.3 Special Attacks: Other / 《 Disease 》

- Some monsters bring about disease. The spirit power of a monster is balanced differently than that of a human, and can always cause illness. However, in most cases the infection power of this information imbalance is weak, so it doesn't pose much of a problem, but if a character is bitten by a bat or rat, it's highly contagious, so there's a high chance he or she will become ill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08788))_
- When you're dealt damage by a monster with this ability, roll 2D and compare it with the progression intensity of each illness. If your roll is less than the progression intensity, you will be infected with the disease. Illnesses can be treated using the holy magic Cure Disease and Refresh , and the spirit magic Restore Health . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08789))_
- For detailed rules regarding illnesses, please refer to 12.2.2: Illnesses (see p. 164). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08790))_

### [ Horse ] / Handling Monster Special Abilities / 13.6.3 Special Attacks: Other / 《 Strangling 》

- This is an ability that many monsters have. If an attack with this ability is successful, then in subsequent rounds, the character will remain strangled, and the only thing he or she can do is try to escape. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08792))_
- To escape, make a success roll using adventurer level + strength bonus as the baseline score. The attack points of the attack is the target score (or the attack points of the hit , if using the optional rule to give monsters variance in attack points). If you succeed, you can break free, but if you fail, you'll automatically suffer damage at the end of the round. This is treated as normal damage, so it's possible to reduce it due to armor. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08793))_
- While being strangled, a character suffers a -4 penalty to evasion and is normally unable to make sound. Depending on the monster, the penalty may be smaller, or it might be possible to still make sound, but if this is the case it'll be specified in the description . Unless otherwise noted, the evasion penalty is 4 and magic is also considered not possible. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08794))_
- If a character is strangled and another is trying to rescue him or her by attacking, please refer to the optional rule for grappling (see p. 265) for how to handle it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08795))_
- If an attack with this ability is successful, then in subsequent rounds, the character will remain strangled, and the only thing he or she can do is try to escape. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08792))_

### [ Horse ] / Handling Monster Special Abilities / 13.6.3 Special Attacks: Other / 《 Entangling 》

- Sprays an adhesive, high-strength thread, etc. that binds a character. The character must make a success roll using adventurer level + strength bonus as the baseline score in order to escape. Each time you fail, you'll normally become more and more entangled, and the target score will increase each time. If the difference between the baseline score and the target score reaches 13 or greater, the character will be unable to escape on his or her own, and will eventually suffocate to death. Whether or not another can rescue them, by attacking or burning the thread, depends on the monster. In the case of the giant webspinner, which is a typical monster that performs this attack, it' s almost impossible to cut the thread. Otherwise, if possible, defense points, destruction points, etc. are set for cutting the thread. When a thread is cut, damage will also be applied to the entangled character at the same time. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08800))_

### [ Horse ] / Handling Monster Special Abilities / 13.6.3 Special Attacks: Other / 《 Swallow Whole 》

- Swallow a character whole, digesting them. A swallowed character cannot escape until the monster is dead. Thus, during this time he or she will continue to suffer the set amount of damage (which can only be reduced by adventurer level) each round. A swallowed character can also attack from inside, if he or she has a dagger, shortsword, or similar small bladed weapon. In this case, make a hit check with a penalty of attack power -4 , and if it hits, make a damage check as normal. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08802))_
- Thus, during this time he or she will continue to suffer the set amount of damage (which can only be reduced by adventurer level) each round. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08802))_
