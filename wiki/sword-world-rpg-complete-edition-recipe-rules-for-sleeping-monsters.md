## Applicability And Rationale

- If you succeed on a success roll using thief skill level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score, the monster will not wake up. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04730))_
- To get within 5 meters of a sleeping monster, you must successfully perform the thief skill stealthy step . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04730))_
- If you fail, the monster will sense your presence and wake up immediately. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04730))_
- If you are attacking with the thief skill, your critical target will be -2. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04732))_
- It will probably become angry and counterattack the adventurer starting on the next round. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04733))_
- If there is combat, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04734))_

## Technical Atoms

### Atom 1: `worked-example`

_Source: Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04731)_

```
The game master must set the monster's monster level as the difficulty and add a 2D roll to it to determine the target score. Depending on the situation (for example, there are pebbles on the floor and they make a lot of noise, or the monster happens to be wearing earplugs), you can apply positive or negative modifiers to the final score (at this time, the reason you must use a difficulty instead of a target score is because there is a possibility that the monster will continue to pretend to be asleep even though it's aware). If you use Silence or Mute magic, there will be no sound, so there is no need for a success roll.
```
