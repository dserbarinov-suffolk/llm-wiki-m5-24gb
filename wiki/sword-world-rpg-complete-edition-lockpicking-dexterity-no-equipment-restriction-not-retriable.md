# [ ② Lockpicking [Dexterity]] (No equipment restriction, not retriable)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- However, the probability of success will be lower (impose a -2 modifier on success rolls). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.113-114)
- The game master should set the lock's target score depending on the difficulty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.113-114)
- You can attempt to pick a lock normally even if you are equipped with armor that exceeds the restriction. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.113-114)
- However, this is almost impossible when wearing metal gauntlets, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.113-114)
