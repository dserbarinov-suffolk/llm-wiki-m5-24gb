# Long Distance Modifiers

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Hitting a distant target with a weapon is a nigh-impossible task. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.264)
- Throwing weapons have a -1 modifier to attack power for every meter beyond 20 meters , and shooting weapons have the same for every 5 meters beyond 100 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.264)
