- This spell gives the target a " do not __ " command and makes them obey it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- This spell's effect only works on those who have some degree of intelligence. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76)
- Depending on the state of decomposition of the corpse, it will determine whether it'll become a skeleton or a zombie. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- If it's still a corpse before that happens, then a zombie will be born. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- To use this spell, of course, you'll need a corpse (usually a human corpse is used). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- Even walking alongside you as guards is too difficult for them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- Create Undead is a spell that creates undead monsters such as skeletons and zombies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- If you use this spell on a mummified or severely decomposed corpse, the corpse will be completely skeletonized and a skeleton will be born. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- The caster can give commands to the skeletons and zombies created in this way. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)
- The commands must be extremely simple, at best they can be made to protect a specific location. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.76-77)

- On the other hand, turning small animals into undead does not mean that they consume less mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- The required mental power (points) increases in proportion with the size of the target ( double for an ogre, seven times for a chimera, and over 1000 times for a 20-meter long dragon!). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- Other scores may not need to be changed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- A rough guideline is to set life scores to 1/2 for skeletons, and 3/2 for zombies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)

- By casting this spell, the caster can see things on the other side of obstacles such as walls, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- This spell is used on objects imbued with magical power to hide the presence of that magical power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- When discerning magic power with Analyze Enchantment , you'll need a final score greater than or equal to the Seal Enchantment that was cast on it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)

- This spell cannot be cast on living things. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)

- Effect=Steals strike power 20 mental power from the target and gives it to the caster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- This spell allows you to steal mental power (points) from the target and make it your own. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- However, you cannot make your mental power (points) higher than your original score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- Also, it has no effect at all if your opponent successfully resists. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- First make a strike power 20 check to deal damage to the target, then the target's mental power (points) will be reduced while at the same time the caster's mental power (points) will be recovered by the same amount. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- This also has no effect on opponents that do not have mental power (points), such as golems and skeletons. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77)
- This is a spell that reduces the target's agility to half its original score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77-78)
- Please refer to Table 5-3: Slow: Agility and Evasion Penalties. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77-78)
- Regarding the evasion speed (points) penalty, the effect differs depending on the target's original agility. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77-78)
- Targets who fail to resist will have their action order and movement speed reduced by half due to changes in their agility, and their evasion speed (points) will also take a penalty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.77-78)
- Please refer to Table 5-4: Haste: Agility and Evasion Bonuses. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- Regarding the evasion speed (points) bonus, the effect differs depending on the target's original agility. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- The target's action order and movement speed will be doubled, and their evasion speed (points) will also receive a bonus. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- This is a spell that doubles the target's agility from its original score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- Area=One Duration=18 rounds Effect=Binds the target with invisible rope Type=Target score Expansion=Final score, duration, distance, targets Resist= Negated effect This spell creates an invisible magical rope, with the effect of binding the target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- The poor victim will be unable to take any actions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- However, since they can still move their mouth, they can speak and use holy magic. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- Not even an ogre can break this magical rope. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- Note that if the target succeeds on a resistance roll against this spell, it'll have no effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
- This spell works on targets slightly larger than a human (such as an ogre or a manticore), but has no effect on targets of a larger size than that (such as a dragon or a roc). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.78)
