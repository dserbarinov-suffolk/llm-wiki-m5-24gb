## Statements

- Monsters with this ability disguise themselves in a particular way, wait for a victim to approach, then strike when he or she is not careful. To discover this, you must succeed on a resistance roll against the indicated target score using ranger skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score. If you fail, you'll suffer a surprise attack , with a -4 penalty to your evasion speed for one round, and you cannot counterattack or use magic. From the second round onwards, it'll be normal combat. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08785))_
