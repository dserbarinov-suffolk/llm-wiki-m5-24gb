## Statements

- If you want to take advantage of your thief skill, your weapons and armor must be light . Specifically, weapons and armor that require up to half your strength (rounded up) . For armor, metal armor other than ring mail may not be used, regardless of the character's strength or required strength. Choose non-metal armor or ring mail. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00805))_
