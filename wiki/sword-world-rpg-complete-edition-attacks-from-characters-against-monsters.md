# Attacks From Characters Against Monsters

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- A score equal to a monster's evasion points - 7 is written next to it in parenthesis. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- This is the target score for a character to hit the monster with an attack. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- Please remember that the game master should be considered fi rst to roll the dice. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- As in the previous section, this is the process for a success roll with monster's evasion points -7 as the difficulty . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- Therefore, a tie would result in character's attack success = monster's evasion failure . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- A monster's defense points are fixed, and the difference is based on the character's strike roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
