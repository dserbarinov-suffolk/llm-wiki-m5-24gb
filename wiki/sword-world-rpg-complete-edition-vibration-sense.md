### Monster Catalog / How to Read the Monster Catalog

- ( Vibration sense ) means the monster can sense vibrations in the ground and perceive things moving around it. They cannot perceive things that are flying in the air or that are not moving. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05856))_
