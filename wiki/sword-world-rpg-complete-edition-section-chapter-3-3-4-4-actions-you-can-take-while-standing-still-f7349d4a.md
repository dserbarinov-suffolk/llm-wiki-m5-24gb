## Statements

- Although stationary, you do not have to remain completely still; you can move up to 3 meters . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01137))_
- In this state, all actions you can take during normal movement described in the previous section can be performed. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01138))_

## Statements by subsection

### Chapter 3: / 3.4.4 Actions You Can Take While Standing Still / Stand Up and Ready Yourself

- From a state of falling down, stand up and ready yourself. You can only ready one weapon, etc. that you're wearing. You cannot pick up any items on the ground. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01144))_
- You can only ready one weapon, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01144))_

### Technical frame 1: Chapter 3: / 3.4.4 Actions You Can Take While Standing Still / Weapon and Armor Restrictions

**Context:** _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01152))_

> A character's arms are largely divided into weapons, shields, and armor. Required strength should not be added up, but rather considered separately for each of these. In other words, a character with 14 strength can wield a weapon with a required strength of 14, wield a shield with a required strength of 14, and wear armor with a required strength of 14.

**Atom:** _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01151))_

<a id="atom-technical-atom-c8a5fd32a3a199d0"></a>
```text
Weapon and Armor Restrictions
4.1 Weapon and Armor Restrictions
A very frail man, all skin and bones, dressed in heavy plate armor and wielding a long greatsword...
All joking aside, however, if we want to be realistic, weapons and armor used should be limited by a character's strength. All weapons and armor have a required strength to use them. Characters  may  not  use  weapons  or armor  with  a  required  strength  greater than their own strength.
4.2
```
