# Thieves Guild/Sorcerers' Guild (Academy)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Both are global organizations that regularly exchange information between headquarters and branches. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- Though not official guilds, the Thieves Guild and Sorcerers' Guild (Academy) are essential organizations. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- In exchange, members receive substantial protection (though if you make a bad impression on the leadership, you might be quietly eliminated). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- The Thieves Guild's power is so vast that it's said to control the underworld of nations. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- The organization is tightly structured, with members having various obligations, including tribute payments, and once you join, it's extremely difficult to leave. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- While the advice is readily available, it might not be particularly reliable in times of crisis. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
- The Sorcerers' Guild, on the other hand, is less restrictive and operates more as a cooperative for mutual development. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.284)
