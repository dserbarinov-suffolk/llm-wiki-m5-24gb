## Statements

- There are several types of skills, which are divided into several groups. For each skill, a character's proficiency is represented by a level from 0~10. 0 levels indicates that someone is completely unrelated to that skill, while 10 levels represents the highest proficiency that a normal human can attain. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00222))_
- Skills are referred to by names such as fighter , thief , merchant , etc. As you can see, they have a strong connotation of professional job functions rather than skills. It is also possible for one character to acquire (have levels in) more than one skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00223))_
- Those who have these skills are often referred to by their skill names. So instead of someone who has the fighter skill , they are simply called fi ghters . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00224))_
- Half-elves have a longer life span than humans due to their elven characteristics, with a life span of around 200 years. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00225))_
