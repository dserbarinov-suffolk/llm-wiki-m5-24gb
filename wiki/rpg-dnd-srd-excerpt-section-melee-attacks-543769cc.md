# Melee Attacks

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- A typical monster makes a melee attack when it strikes with its claws, horns, teeth, tentacles, or other body part. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00381))_
- A melee attack typically uses a handheld weapon such as a sword, a warhammer, or an axe. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00381))_

- Certain creatures (typically those larger than Medium) have melee attacks with a greater reach than 5 feet, as noted in their descriptions. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00382))_
- You are proficient with your unarmed strikes. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00383))_
- Instead of using a weapon to make a melee weapon attack, you can use an unarmed strike : a punch, kick, head-butt, or similar forceful blow (none of which count as weapons). _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00383))_
- On a hit, an unarmed strike deals bludgeoning damage equal to 1 + your Strength modifier. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00383))_
