## Statements

- This staff can be pre-imbued with magic that activates either when a specific password is spoken or after a predetermined time has elapsed. The spells that can be stored in this staff are limited to ancient magic with a consumed mental power (not the base mental power cost) of 3 or less. In other words, as a sorcerer reaches higher levels, they become capable of storing more powerful spells. To store a spell in this staff, you must expend the same amount of mental power as casting the spell normally would require. Furthermore, the mental power used to store a spell in this staff cannot be recovered by any means until the stored magic is used. Anyone who knows the password can activate the magic stored in the staff. If set to activate after a certain time instead of with a password, the spell will be centered on the staff. In this case, if the activating magic is an area-effect damage magic like Fireball , the staff will be destroyed upon activation. While this staff can be used as a normal magical catalyst, it provides no bonus when used as a weapon. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09630))_
