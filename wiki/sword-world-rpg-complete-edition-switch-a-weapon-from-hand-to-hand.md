# Switch a Weapon From Hand to Hand

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- You can move something in your right hand to your left hand, or hold something you had in one hand with both hands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)
- Some weapons can be held in either one or both hands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.37)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If you have such a weapon in one hand and a shield in the other, if you drop the shield, the weapon is now considered to be in only one hand, so you can then hold it with both hands.
```
