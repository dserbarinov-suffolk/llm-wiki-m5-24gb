## Statements

- This spell has the effect of taking away the target's hearing. The target can attempt to resist, but a target who fails will no longer be able to hear any sounds. If they're successful, they won't be affected at all. The penalty that'll be imposed depends on the situation, so the game master should act on the assumption that the target cannot hear. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04810))_

## Statements by subsection

### 2nd Level Basic Dark Magic List / Blindness

- This spell has the effect of taking away the target's vision. If the target successfully resists, it'll have no effect. If a human or monster in this state (only those that rely on vision) attempts to attack, they suffer a -4 modifier to attack and evasion. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04821))_
