# Travel

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about travel:

## Statements

- Obvious locations, features, and terrain of nearby areas are revealed according to their distance. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00354))_
- This risk can increase or decrease, depending on path Difficulty , maps, party skills, and guides. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00355))_
- The party rolls 1d6 to see if they get lost along the way. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00355))_
- If lost, the party may need to spend a Wilderness Action to recover their way. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00356))_
