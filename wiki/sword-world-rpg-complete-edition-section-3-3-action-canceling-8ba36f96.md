## Statements

- A character may cancel and not perform their announced action. For example, if an enemy that you intended to attack falls down due to the attack of a fellow player, no further attack is necessary. If you fall down yourself early in the order, you will not be able to act. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01066))_
- In such a case, a character may cancel their intended action. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01068))_
- However, characters cannot switch their actions. Just because a companion has knocked down an enemy in front of you does not mean that you can attack a different enemy. You also cannot switch from magic to a weapon attack because you intended to cast a spell but were blocked from chanting halfway through. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01069))_
- The choice is basically this: either continue with the announced action or cancel it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01070))_
- For example, if an enemy that you intended to attack falls down due to the attack of a fellow player, no further attack is necessary. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01066))_
