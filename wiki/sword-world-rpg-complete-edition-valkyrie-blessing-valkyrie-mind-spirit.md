# [ Valkyrie Blessing ] (Valkyrie/Mind Spirit)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- When the protection runs out, any damage beyond that (such as suffering 20 damage when the protection has only 10 remaining) will not reach the target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- The dome serves as a tent that protects from the elements and as a temporary shelter when a suitable campsite cannot be found. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- The outer walls of the dome are tough and densely intertwined with living plants, protecting those inside from attacks by wild beasts and less powerful monsters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- When you chant this spell, plants will grow along the perimeter of a 5 meter radius circle centered on the place the caster touches, creating a 2 meter tall dome of natural plants on the next round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
- The inside of the dome is covered with a carpet of soft fresh grass, making it moderately warm and moderately cool. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.93)
