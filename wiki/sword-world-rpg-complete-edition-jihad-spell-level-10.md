# [ Jihad ] (Spell Level=10)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- As a result, anyone who agrees to the spell, including the person who cast it, will experience the following effects. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- This spell is used when a high priest of Pharis discovers a great evil that absolutely cannot be overlooked. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- Gather as many Pharis believers as you can to a temple, and cast this in front of them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- Those with fighter skill 0 will be temporarily given 2 levels) 2. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
- It has no impact at all on those who do not agree to the spell, but no devout follower of Pharis would not agree to the high priest's spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.106)
