- This plant can grow up to about 10 meters in height. The stem has 2 to 5 bud-shaped flowers about 1 meter in diameter. When a creature such as a human, etc. approaches, the folded flower stalks extend out and the buds open to envelop their prey. The flower stalks grow to about 10 meters long. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-07442))_
- This flower's attack strangles (see p. 236) the character and deals damage. When the prey dies, the flower reverts back to a bud and finishes absorbing nutrients in about one week. If the prey escapes with all their might, the flower becomes torn and unusable, and will eventually wither. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-07458))_
