# Attacks From Monsters Against Characters

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This is the target score for a character to evade the monster's attack. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- A score equal to a monster's attack points - 7 is written next to it in parenthesis. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- As you can see from the way the dice are rolled, this is the process for a success roll with monster's attack points -7 as the difficulty . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- Also, if the game master rolls double ones or double sixes, that does not automatically result in failure/success (but for a character , it still does ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- Therefore, a tie would result in character's evasion success = monster's attack failure . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- The monster's strike points are fixed, and the difference is based on protection from the character's armor. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
