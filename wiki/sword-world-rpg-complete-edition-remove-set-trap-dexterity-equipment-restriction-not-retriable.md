# [ ⑰ Remove/Set Trap [Dexterity] ] (Equipment restriction, not retriable)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- You can attempt to remove traps discovered through fi nd . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- Removal also involves attempting a success roll against the trap's difficulty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- Unless the game master has specifically set traps that are easy to find but difficult to remove , or conversely, difficult to find but easy to remove , the difficulty of finding and removing should be the same. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- The effect of an activated trap depends on the type of trap. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- Traps that you fail to find cannot be removed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- If a third party attempts to sense , find , or remove a trap set by a player, the game master must attempt a success roll on their behalf. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.116)
