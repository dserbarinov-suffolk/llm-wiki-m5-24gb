# 4.6.5 Final Damage

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- The final damage your character suffers from a monster equals the monster's strike points minus the result of your defense roll plus your character's damage reduction . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- Final damage = monster's strike points - (defense roll result + damage reduction). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- If the result of Ducard II's defense roll is 0 (roll 3) or 6 (roll 12), respectively, the final damage he will suffer is calculated to be:. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- If the defense roll came up double ones , no such calculation would be made, and the goblin's 7 strike points would be the damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- If final damage is 0 or negative , that means you took no damage at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
