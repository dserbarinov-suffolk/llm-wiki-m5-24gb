# 10th Level Dragon Roar Magic List [ Reborn Dragon ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Base Mental Power Cost=30 Distance=Caster Area=Caster Duration=Instant Effect= Be reborn as a dragon Type=Non-removable, ritual Expansion=Final score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- It is no exaggeration to say that dragon priests undergo rigorous training for this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- If this final score does not reach 20, the caster will die as they are. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Upon using this spell, death will come to the dragon priest. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- Even Perfect Cancellation is ineffective. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- When you're reborn, your memories of your previous life will be lost, but your potential as a dragon is high, and after several decades you will shed your skin and transform into an elder dragon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
- At this time, along with the memories from when you became a dragon, the memories and skills you had during your lifetime will be resurrected. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.153)
