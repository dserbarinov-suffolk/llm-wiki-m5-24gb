## Key supported claims

- Nennius established a large monastery on the river Renis, named after him, which still exists today. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- Nennius constructed Gloiuda, a significant city on the Severn, known as Cair Gloui in British and Gloucester in Saxon. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- Nennius's journey to Britain was hindered by divine signs, redirecting him from Ireland to Britain, where he eventually died in Pictland. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- The text records a timeline spanning 5,330 years from the world's beginning to the fifth year of king Logiore. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- A battle near Gurnion castle involved Arthur carrying the image of the Holy Virgin, leading to a Saxon defeat. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
