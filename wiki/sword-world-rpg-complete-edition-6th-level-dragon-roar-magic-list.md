# 6th Level Dragon Roar Magic List

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- However, a wyvern's intellect is not very high, so you can only have it do things like attack enemies, or carry you while you hold onto its legs. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- Specifically, the caster must consume their life force at the same time as their mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- That blood allows you to protect others from all attacks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- Losing 1 point of life force can give you 1 point of defense effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- While the spell is in effect, the caster cannot regain the life force they've consumed (think of it as their starting life force being lowered). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- There is no need to consume extra life force when expanding the final score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- If you wanted to give 2 points of defense effect to three people each, a 6th level caster's mental power consumed would be 24 points and their life force consumed would be 6 points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- The caster can expand the effect to provide a greater defense effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- However, in order to do this, you must consume an extra amount of your life force as well as your mental power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- For example, a 6th level caster can consume 8 points of mental power and 2 points of life force at the same time to provide 2 points of defense effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- If an 8th level caster were to do the same thing, the mental power consumed would only be 18 points, but the life force consumed would still be 6 points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
- Please note that this spell cannot be cast on the caster him or herself. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.152)
