# Making an Attack

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Whether you're striking with a melee weapon, firing a weapon at range, or making an attack roll as part of a spell, an attack has a simple structure. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00352))_
- Pick a target within your attack's Range: a creature, an object, or a location. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00353))_
- The GM determines whether the target has cover and whether you have advantage or disadvantage against the target. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00354))_
- In addition, spells, special abilities, and other effects can apply penalties or bonuses to your attack roll. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00354))_
- Some attacks cause special effects in addition to or instead of damage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00355))_
- On a hit, you roll damage, unless the particular attack has rules that specify otherwise. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00355))_
- You make the attack roll. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00355))_
- On a hit, you roll damage, unless the particular attack has rules that specify otherwise. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00355))_
