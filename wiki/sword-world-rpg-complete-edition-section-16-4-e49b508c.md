## Statements by subsection

### Projectile Range Extension

- In the basic rules, the range limit is 20 meters for throwing weapons and 100 meters for shooting weapons. If you follow these optional rules, you can extend these even further. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10300))_

### Projectile Range Extension / Long Distance Modifiers

- Hitting a distant target with a weapon is a nigh-impossible task. Throwing weapons have a -1 modifier to attack power for every meter beyond 20 meters , and shooting weapons have the same for every 5 meters beyond 100 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10303))_

### Projectile Range Extension / Maximum Range

- There's no such thing as a bow with infinite reach. Each throwing or shooting weapon has a maximum range , and they will never hit a target farther away than this ( Table 16-4: Maximum Ranges ). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10305))_
