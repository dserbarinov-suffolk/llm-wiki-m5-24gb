### Chapter 14: / 14.4 Magical Item List / 《 Amulets 》 / [ Telecall Amulet ]

- This is a magical amulet that allows you to converse with someone in a distant location. The amulet possesses magical power such that when you correctly recite the password of your intended recipient's charm and speak towards the jewel at its tip, your voice will emerge from their jewel. In other words, at least two amulets are required to make a call. While this useful item has been found relatively frequently in the ruins of ancient kingdom, its communication range is limited to approximately 10 kilometers. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09537))_

### Chapter 14: / 14.4 Magical Item List / 《 Amulets 》 / [ Crystal ]

- Crystals, represented by magic stones, are items that contain magic power sealed within crystals. They were produced in relatively large quantities during the Ancient Kingdom period. According to one theory, they were used as currency in the Kastuul Kingdom. Below, we will introduce their types. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09539))_
- When referring to a magic crystal, it means a stone that concentrates magic power. While it looks similar to topaz at first glance, upon closer inspection it emits a bluish-white light from within. Normally, this stone stores 2D+10 mental points, and a rune master can use magic by consuming these mental points instead of their own mental power. However, only one stone can be used at a time, and it cannot be used simultaneously with the caster's own mental power. It can also be used as a substitute for mental power when using common runes. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09545))_
- When the stored mental points are depleted, the magic crystal shatters. The method of creating magic crystals has been lost, and it is impossible to replenish mental points in magic crystals. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09546))_
- It is believed that these treasures were used as currency during the Ancient Kingdom period, and they are frequently discovered in the ruins of the Ancient Kingdom.
