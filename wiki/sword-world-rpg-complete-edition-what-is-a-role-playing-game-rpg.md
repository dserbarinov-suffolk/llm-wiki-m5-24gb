# What is a Role-Playing Game (RPG)?

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Role-Playing means to act out a role . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Participants in an RPG can be roughly divided into two groups: One is the game master ( GM ), while the other are the players . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- You may either create your own, or you may use an existing one. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- A number of scenario collections have already been produced for Sword World RPG , and will continue to be released in the future. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Before playing a game, the game master must prepare what's called a scenario . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- The game master is the one who facilitates the scenario, while the players play the role -- of the characters --in the scenario. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- The game proceeds according to a prepared scenario. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- However, there is something that's very different from film or theater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Which is : the players do not know the scenario. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- The game master's scenario does not provide any dialogue, etc., for the characters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- GM is the game master, and A , B , and C are different players. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- You're now resting at a tavern now that the sun is setting. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- GM ： You guys were traveling and you arrived at a small village. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- okay, I'll give them a little warning. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- That would soil the name of a hero of justice!. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- GM ： They have very dirty leather armor and shortswords. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- C ： Even I have the backing of the Thieves Guild (laughs). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- (laughs) One of the thugs, who looks particularly rough, snarls 'What, you have the gall to complain about us - the Brando family?!' . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- The conflict only arose because the players wanted it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- It only says thugs will misbehave in the tavern , then it's up to the players to decide what they'll do about it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- If the players were all like player B , there would've been no conflict. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Here is where another major feature of role-playing games comes into play. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Not knowing what the results will be. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Neither the game master nor the players know whether the characters will be able to kick the thugs out of the tavern and become heroes of justice , or get beaten by the thugs and instead become heroes of comedy . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Even in real life, if you start a fight, you won't know who will win without actually fighting. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
- Player ： ( rolls ) Hey, I got a good roll, my attack is a hit!. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)

- In this way, due to two types of randomness, player's choice and random numbers (dice) , the actual game can display a wide degree of fluctuation, sometimes the "story" will evolve beyond the expectations of the game master. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.5)
