## Statements

- Once you've acquired your final skills (starting skill and growth via starting experience points), you can determine your adventurer level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00916))_
- Adventurer level is equal to the highest adventurer skill level. Write this number down. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00917))_
