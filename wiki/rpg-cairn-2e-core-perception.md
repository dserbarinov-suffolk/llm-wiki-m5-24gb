# Perception

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about perception:

## Statements

- The answer to these questions is almost always an emphatic No . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00559))_
- True agency comes from providing the players with everything they need to know to make an interesting decision, even if that is simply asking a question about what their character might perceive. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00560))_
- A man observes in the shadows beneath the tree, and a rusted sword lies on the ground not far from the tree. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00562))_
- Bees : Their buzzing is nearly deafening, creating a wall of sound around the tree. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00563))_
- The hive is dripping with honey. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00564))_
- Any attempt to take honey from the hive is met with an aggressive response from the colony [3 HP , 3 STR, 12 DEX, 4 WIL, sting (d6), detachment ]. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00565))_
- Man : Leans against a long staff, his gaze fixed on the hive. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00566))_
- He will not react to any direct stimuli. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00567))_
- If the hive is damaged in any way, the man will scream very loudly, then disappear. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00568))_
- The man is an unknown entity and could be friend or foe, while the Bloodvines are hidden and act as a sort of trap. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00572))_
- The Warden should therefore communicate exactly what the PCs can see, hear, and smell. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00572))_
- The danger from bees is more obvious, as the PCs can clearly see the danger and are familiar with bees already. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00572))_
- In this example, there are three potential dangers: the bees, the man, and the Bloodvine plant. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00572))_
- If the PCs ask questions about the sword or try to interact with it, the Warden should tell them about the shoe prints in the grass right away. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00573))_
