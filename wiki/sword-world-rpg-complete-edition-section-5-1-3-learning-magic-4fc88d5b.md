## Statements

- Magic is not omnipotent. It is only possible to achieve a defined and limited set of effects by weaving certain words (in runes), called spells . There are several types of spells, each with different effects. Depending on the strength of their effects, spells are divided into levels from 1-10 . 1st level spells are very rudimentary, while 10th level spells are very powerful. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01519))_
- However, working with higher level (i.e., more advanced) spells requires a high rune master skill. A rune master can use all magic up to their skill level, but cannot use magic of a higher level than their current skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01520))_
- For those who have acquired multiple rune master skills, consider the level of spells you can use for each type of magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01521))_
- Suppose we have an adventurer with priest skill level 1 and shaman skill level 2. This adventurer can use 1st level holy magic and 1st-2nd level spirit magic. He cannot use any ancient magic, nor any holy magic of 2nd level or higher, nor any spirit magic of 3rd level or higher. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01523))_
