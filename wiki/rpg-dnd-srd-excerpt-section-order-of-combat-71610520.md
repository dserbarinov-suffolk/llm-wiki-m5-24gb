# ORDER OF COMBAT

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Once everyone has taken a turn, the fight continues to the next round if neither side has defeated the other. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00240))_
- A round represents about 6 seconds in the game world. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00240))_
- The order of turns is determined at the beginning of a combat encounter, when everyone rolls initiative. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00240))_
- A typical combat encounter is a clash between two sides, a flurry of weapon swings, feints, parries, footwork, and spellcasting. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00240))_
