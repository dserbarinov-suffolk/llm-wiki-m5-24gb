## Statements

- This is a liquid poison. Those who fail to resist will stop breathing and their heart will stop, making them appear completely dead. However, they're actually in a state of suspended animation and will return to normal after 12 hours. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05611))_

## Statements by subsection

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [ Dream Runner (Inhaled) ]

- This is smoke produced when the leaves of certain plants are burned. Those who fail to resist will fall asleep and have happy dreams. If you use it too many times, you may harm your health or go crazy. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05618))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [ Bad Joke (Inhaled) ]

- This is a gas that smells good. Those who fail to resist will end up laughing, unable to cast spells that round, and will suffer a -2 penalty on all checks. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05625))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [ Bad Joke (Inhaled) ] / [Beast Maker (Dose/Magic)]

- This is a liquid with a slight odor, used by mixing into cooking, etc. It has an effect similar to the ancient magic Shape Change , and those who drink it and fail to resist will be transformed into an animal. There are several types of Beast Maker , including ones that turn you into a pig, ones that turn you into a dog and ones that turn you into a horse. Particularly powerful ones can turn you into a mythical beast such as a scylla, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05628))_
- Particularly powerful ones can turn you into a mythical beast such as a scylla, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05628))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [ Bad Joke (Inhaled) ] / [Black Death (Touch, Injected, or Inhaled/Magic, Damage)]

- This is a special black viscous poison that enters the body when it comes in contact with the skin. It will continue to deal damage every hour thereafter. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05635))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [White Marble (Inhaled/Magic)]

- It's a white gas. Those who inhale this and fail to resist will have their entire body stiffen and become like a statue. Victims retain their will and senses, but are unable to move a single finger. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05642))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [White Marble (Inhaled/Magic)] / [Midnight Scream (Touch/Mind)]

- This is a viscous poison and is used by smearing it on doorknobs, etc. Those who touch this poison and fail to resist will be struck with intense fear. Determine its effect using Table 5-5: Fears (see p. 89). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05646))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [White Marble (Inhaled/Magic)] / [Moonlight Drone (Dose/Magic)]

- This is a small granular poison, and if someone drinks it and fails to resist, they will become a woman if they're a man, and a man if they're a woman. There are no other penalties. Additionally, there are no changes to the magic that shamans can use. If you drink it again, you can return to normal. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05651))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [Medusa Eyes (Dose/Magic)]

- This is a clear liquid poison that has a slight odor, so it's used by mixing with alcohol, etc. Those who fail to resist will have their bodies petrified and become stone statues. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05658))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [ Love Passion (Inhaled, Dose/Magic, Mind) ]

- This is what's called a love potion . It tastes and smells like alcohol, and takes effect not only by drinking it but also by smelling it (if it's only inhaled, its toxicity score is 9). Those who fail to resist will fall passionately in love with the first thing they see (regardless of gender or race). The only people you can fall in love with are fae, youma, titans, and mythical beasts that have human body parts (centaurs, etc.); you cannot fall in love with undead, animals, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05665))_
- The recipe for love passion has now been completely lost, so it's no longer possible to make it. There are only a few left from the ancient kingdom period, and they are traded at high prices. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05666))_

### [Deathlike Sleep (Dose)] / [ Love Passion (Inhaled, Dose/Magic, Mind) ] / [Living Doll (Injected/Magic)]

- This is a very rare type of poison, and only a few know the recipe. If a woman is wounded by a knife poisoned with this and fails to resist, her body will shrink and become a little person, about 30 cm tall, and will enter a state of suspended animation. The victims look like dolls, hence the name. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05674))_
- This poison has its normal effect only on women. When a man is affected by this poison and fails to resist, his body undergoes abnormal mutations as a side effect, turning him into a mutant monster. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05675))_
