- By casting this spell, a normally invisible magical aura becomes visible to the caster. This lets you know if there are magical items, magical traps, etc. within your field of view. However, it's not possible to discern exactly what magical powers are at work. You also cannot find things whose form you can't see directly, such as things inside a box. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01967))_
