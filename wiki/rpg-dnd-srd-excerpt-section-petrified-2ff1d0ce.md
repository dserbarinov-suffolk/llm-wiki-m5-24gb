# Petrified

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- - A petrified creature is transformed, along with any nonmagical object it is wearing or carrying, into a solid inanimate substance (usually stone). _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00574))_
- - The creature is incapacitated (see the condition), can't move or speak, and is unaware of its surroundings. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00575))_
- - Attack rolls against the creature have advantage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00576))_
- - The creature has resistance to all damage. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00578))_
- - The creature is immune to poison and disease, although a poison or disease already in its system is suspended, not neutralized. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00579))_
