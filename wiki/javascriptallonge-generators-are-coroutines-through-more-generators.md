## Key supported claims

- Generators in JavaScript are a form of coroutines, allowing for suspended and resumed execution through yield statements. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Generators can be used to create iterable objects, such as collections that yield values sequentially. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- The source provides examples of generators producing infinite streams and implementing sequences like Fibonacci. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- The source acknowledges uncertainty about the full scope of generators, noting that there is more to explore beyond the examples provided. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
