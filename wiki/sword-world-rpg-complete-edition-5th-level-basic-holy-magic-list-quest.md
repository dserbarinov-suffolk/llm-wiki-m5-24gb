# 5th Level Basic Holy Magic List [ Quest ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Duration=Until the mission is accomplished Effect=Force one mission on the target Type=Curse, target score, ritual Expansion=Final score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101)
- This is not a matter of action, but of the mind, and the spell does not cause pain unless the one on whom it was cast forgets their mission. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101-102)
- If the target of the spell takes any action that violates this mission, they will experience severe pain throughout their entire body. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101-102)
- However, even if you do remember, you will still experience pain if you do not actively try to fulfill it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101-102)
- The pain is so severe that if you continue to be exposed to it for more than 10 minutes, you will become mentally abnormal. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101-102)
- For example, you might be asked to fetch a certain item or defeat a certain monster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101-102)
- This spell has the effect of allowing the caster to set one mission for the target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.101-102)
- For example, asking someone to bring you the most beautiful woman in the world is an abstract command, and ruling the world is a mission that goes beyond the compulsion of this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.102)
- This spell is canceled when the caster dies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.102)
- The mission that can be forced on an opponent through a Quest must be specific and able to be identified, and a mission that is too great will nullify the spell's coercive power. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.102)
- However, the caster can tell whether the spell has taken effect or not, so it may be repeated two or three times. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.102)
- It's especially often used for spells with curse effects, such as Curse and Geas . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.102)
- However, you can only attempt to remove one spell's effect once . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.102)
