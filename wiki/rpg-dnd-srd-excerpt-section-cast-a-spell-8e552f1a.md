## Statements

- Each spell has a casting time, which specifies whether the caster must use an action, a reaction, minutes, or even hours to cast the spell. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00328))_
- Most spells do have a casting time of 1 action, so a spellcaster often uses his or her action in combat to cast such a spell. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00328))_
- Casting a spell is, therefore, not necessarily an action. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00328))_
