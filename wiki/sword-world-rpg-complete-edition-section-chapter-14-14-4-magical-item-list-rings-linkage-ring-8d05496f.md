## Statements

- When characters of the same race and opposite gender wear these rings, they can freely use each other's mental points. However, if one person has a spell, etc., that affects the mind cast on him or her, it'll affect both people. These rings will not work unless both people are within 1 km. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09698))_
