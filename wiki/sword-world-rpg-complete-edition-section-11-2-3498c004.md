## Statements by subsection

### Dark Magic

- As mentioned earlier, these runes, like holy magic, use the gods as its source of power. However, it is derived from the power of the gods of evil (wicked gods) such as Phalaris, who are believed to cause harm to us humans. For this reason, the rune masters of these runes are both hated and feared by the general public. A rune master who uses dark magic is called a dark priest . In a narrow sense, only the priests of Phalaris are sometimes called dark priests. This is because believers in wicked gods other than Phalaris are rarely seen. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04765))_
- Dark magic basically has the same origin as holy magic, and dark priests can use most of the basic holy magic. In addition, dark priests can use wicked magic that the gods of light do not allow (do not give power to). Furthermore, just as there is special holy magic that is unique to each god among holy magic, there is also special dark magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04766))_
