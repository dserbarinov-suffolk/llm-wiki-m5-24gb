## Procedure

- In such cases, the game master applies a bonus or penalty to the success roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.31)
- A player must also calculate these before making a success roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.31)
- When such bonuses or penalties are applied, the baseline score will go up or down. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.31)
