# Equipment

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- If you only have weapons and armor, you will not be able to go on an adventure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- It won't be an adventure if you forget your ropes and torches. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Many games set some kind of weight on weapons, armor and equipment, limiting the amount of goods a character can carry. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- However, Sword World RPG does not do this. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Instead, please use your common sense to make decisions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- This would be a matter of course even if the rules do not stipulate it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- A character cannot have an infinite number of things. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- It is impossible to hold 1000 torches or 1 kilometer of rope. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Ducard II has 580 gamels left. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Without it, it'll be difficult to carry luggage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- We should also have a belt pouch (15G) for small items and money. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Since we're going on an adventure, a water bag (20G) is a must. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- First, we'll need a rucksack (50G). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- If he has friends, though, it would be nice if we could pool our money together to buy it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- A tent is too expensive and we don't need something large enough for five people. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Tableware (12G) and cooking utensils (50G) will also be needed for camping. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- If we're planning to sleep outdoors, we'll need a mantle (40G) and a blanket (50G). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Without that, a fire cannot be started. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- For a lighting tool, we'll first need a tinderbox (20G). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- We'll also need torches (6 for 5 G). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- A lantern would be brighter, but money is tight and we can't afford that luxury. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- Ducard II's ranger skill includes the map creation ability. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- It may be useful at some point. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- The thieves' tools are for a thief and the musical instruments are for a bard, so they're not necessary for Ducard II. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- So far, 277 gamels have been spent on purchases, with 303 gamels remaining. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
- We won't buy preserved food yet. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.24)
