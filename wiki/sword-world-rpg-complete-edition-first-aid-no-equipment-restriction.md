# [ ② First-Aid [-] ] (No equipment restriction)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The armor you wear must have a required strength of up to half your own strength, and must be ring mail or non-metal. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.116)
- The ranger skill has some similar abilities to the thief skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.116)
- However, while the thief skill can be used both outdoors and indoors, the ranger skill is mostly useful only outdoors. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.116)
