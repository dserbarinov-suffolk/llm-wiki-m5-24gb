## Statements

- Actions are any non-passive activities, such as searching for traps , forcing open a door , listening for danger , disarming a trap , engaging an enemy in combat , casting a spell , dodging a trap , running away , resting , etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00275))_
- Some actions have special rules (see below), while others may take multiple turns to complete. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00276))_
- Loud or noticeable actions may also trigger an encounter with the dungeon's denizens. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00277))_
- A character can spend a turn performing an exhaustive search of one object or location in an area, revealing any relevant hidden treasure, traps, secret doors, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00279))_
- Larger rooms and difficult or complex dungeon terrain may take a few turns to properly search. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00280))_
- Searching a room fi rst is a safer way to explore the dungeon, but it has a steep cost: time. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00281))_
- A character can spend a turn resting to restore all HP . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00283))_
- A light source and a safe location are required to rest . Present or oncoming danger makes rest impossible. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00284))_
- Resting does not restore Fatigue , as it is impossible to safely Make Camp in a dungeon. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00285))_
