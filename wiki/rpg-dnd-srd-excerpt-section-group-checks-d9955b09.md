# Group Checks

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- When a number of individuals are trying to accomplish something as a group, the GM might ask for a group ability check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00117))_
- In such a situation, the characters who are skilled at a particular task help cover those who aren't. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00117))_
- To make a group ability check, everyone in the group makes the ability check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00118))_
- If at least half the group succeeds, the successful characters are able to guide their companions out of danger. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00119))_
- Group checks don't come up very often, and they're most useful when all the characters succeed or fail as a group. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00119))_
- For example, when adventurers are navigating a swamp, the GM might call for a group Wisdom (Survival) check to see if the characters can avoid the quicksand, sinkholes, and other natural hazards of the environment. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00119))_
