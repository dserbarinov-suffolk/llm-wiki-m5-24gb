## Key supported claims

- Functions are applied to zero or more arguments to produce values, similar to mathematical operations. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.31-31)
- Parentheses are used to apply functions to arguments, distinguishing them from operations like grouping in expressions. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.31-31)
- Putting a function in parentheses helps keep the parser happy, as demonstrated with (() => 0). (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf p.31-31)
