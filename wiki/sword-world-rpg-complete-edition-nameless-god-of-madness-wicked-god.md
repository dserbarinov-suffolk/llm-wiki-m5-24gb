# The Nameless God of Madness (Wicked God)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- He is the god of madness, and because of his madness, he is a deity whose name cannot be identified. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.98)
- His followers are few and he has no temples either. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.98)
- It is said that he gives sudden inspiration that is not based in logic, and for this reason he is also considered the god of art and invention, so there are sometimes believers in this god among artists and sages. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.98)
- It is said that he exists in the star world and causes people to go mad, and it is believed that those who come into contact with the light emitted by this mad god on a full moon's night will be afflicted with madness. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.98)
- The priests of his cult teach that madness frees its believers from bondage and gives them freedom. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.98)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When a priest wants to do something, they can make a vow to their god.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** example

```
This is a voluntary vow. When a priest wants to do something, they can make a vow to their god. When a priest's actions contradict or move away from their vows, their god will give them some form of attention or assistance. If a priest of Marfa and his wife make a vow to never divorce at the time of their marriage, but then the relationship between the two turns sour, the two of them may end up having a child together. No vow can be made that violates your faith in your god. For example, a priest of Myrii can make a vow to defeat the traitor XX with my own hands , but a priest of Marfa cannot .
```
