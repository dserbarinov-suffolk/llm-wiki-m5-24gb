- This spell is a powerful version of Dispel Magic , which removes the effects of all spells. Even spells of the nonremovable or curse type, which cannot be removed by Dispel Magic , will be nullified. However, to remove a spell with a target score, you'll need a final score accordingly. In addition, only the dragon roar magic Reborn Dragon cannot be removed by this magic. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02743))_
