## Statements

- This spell is used on an object or a point in space. It has no effect at all when used on living things. Using a single point as a light source, a space with a 10 meter radius is filled with white magical light. The further away you are from the light source, the more the effect of the light will fade. If you cast it on a portable object such as a shield, you can move the light source along with the object. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02037))_
