# Save

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about save:

## Statements

### Overview & Principles / Principles for Wardens / Difficulty

- Saves cover a great deal of uncertain situations and are often all that is necessary for risky actions. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00060))_

### Core Rules / Saves

- A save is a roll to avoid negative outcomes from risky choices. Characters roll a d20 and compare the results to the appropriate attribute. If they roll equal to or under that attribute, they succeed. Otherwise, they fail. A 1 is always a success, and a 20 is always a failure. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00163))_

### Core Rules / Combat / Rounds

- During the fi rst round of combat , each PC must make a DEX save in order to act. Special circumstances, abilities, items, or skills may negate this requirement. PCs that fail their save lose their turn for this round. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00205))_

### Core Rules / Combat / Critical Damage

- weakly, grasping for life. If given aid (such as bandages), they will stabilize. If left untreated, they die within the hour. NPCs and monsters that fail a Critical Damage save are considered dead, per the Warden's discretion. Additionally, some enemies will have special abilities or effects that are triggered when their target fails a critical damage save. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00221))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Panic

- A character that is surrounded by enemies, enveloped by darkness, or facing their greatest fears may experience panic . A WIL save is typically required to avoid losing control and becoming panicked . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00287))_

### Combat / Example

- Wolfram, Lucius, and Freya each save DEX to see whether they can take a turn in the first round. Wolfram and Freya succeed, but Lucius does not. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00467))_

- Wolfram : "My order takes mercy on all folk willing to forge a new path. If we save your sister with our skills and resources, will you vow to pledge yourself to a life of piety, poverty, and kindness?" _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00492))_

### Saves / Save Principles

- The outcome of a save need not be a simple binary. You can tailor the results based on the totality of input: the effort, forethought, resources, and skill that went into the roll. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00508))_
