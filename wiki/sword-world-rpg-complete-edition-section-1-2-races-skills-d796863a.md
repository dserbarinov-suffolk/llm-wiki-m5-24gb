## Statements

- In addition to ability scores, Sword World RPG also uses numeric values called skills to describe the characteristics of characters. Skills have a greater impact on a character's actions than ability scores. This chapter explains the basics of skills. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00220))_

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 1.3.1 Skill Basics

- There are several types of skills, which are divided into several groups. For each skill, a character's proficiency is represented by a level from 0~10. 0 levels indicates that someone is completely unrelated to that skill, while 10 levels represents the highest proficiency that a normal human can attain. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00222))_
- Skills are referred to by names such as fighter , thief , merchant , etc. As you can see, they have a strong connotation of professional job functions rather than skills. It is also possible for one character to acquire (have levels in) more than one skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00223))_
- Those who have these skills are often referred to by their skill names. So instead of someone who has the fighter skill , they are simply called fi ghters . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00224))_
- Half-elves have a longer life span than humans due to their elven characteristics, with a life span of around 200 years. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00225))_

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 1.3.2 Skill Types

- Skills are grouped into several types. Below is an explanation. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00228))_
- First, skills are divided into two types: adventurer skills and general skills . Adventurer skills, as the name implies, are acquired by player characters, the adventurers, or by equivalent characters. There are eight of these: fighter , thief , priest , sorcerer , shaman , sage , ranger , and bard . There are also two adventurer skills that cannot be acquired by a player character: dark priest and dragon priest . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00229))_
- General skills are skills of an ordinary person as opposed to an adventurer, such as merchant , farmer , etc. Some of these are useful during an adventure, and it is well worth it for an adventurer to acquire general skills. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00230))_
- Each skill consists of a number of abilities appropriate to the skill name, and having a skill level (1 skill level or higher) means that you can demonstrate those abilities. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00231))_
- General skills are skills of an ordinary person as opposed to an adventurer, such as merchant , farmer , etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00230))_

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 1.3.3 Adventurer Level

- In Sword World RPG, a character's capability as an adventurer is determined by their adventurer skill level. The highest level among the adventurer skills that a character has is called that character's adventurer level . For example, a character with priest skill level 1, fighter skill level 4 (these are adventurer skills), sailor skill level 5, and fisher skill level 2 (these are general skills) would be at adventurer level 4 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00237))_

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 【 Sorcerer 】

- The ancient kingdom of Kastuul, which fell more than 500 years ago, is sometimes referred to as the magical kingdom . As its nickname suggests, Kastuul is said to have accomplished various miracles through the power of magic. It is this magical power that is known as high ancient and is the magic that the sorcerers of today have inherited. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00265))_
- With the fall of Kastuul, much of high ancient was lost, but some has survived to the present day. The skill that can handle it is the sorcerer skill. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00267))_
- Many sorcerers are also sages. The rules do not require one character to acquire both of these skills, but if they are acquired at the same time, it gives them a significant advantage. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00268))_

The spirit world is the world of spirits who command various powers. - The powers controlled by spirits are diverse, ranging from the four major powers of earth , water , fi re , and wind to the spirit of humans , and there are many different types of spirit magic used by shamans. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00272))_

### 1.2 Races / Skills / 【 Priest 】

- Those who exercise their power as agents of the gods are priests. In the ancient days of myth, the gods who created the world were later divided into light and darkness and waged a great war. A great war that engulfed all the ancient races- including the legendary dragons of old --ended with all the gods losing their bodies. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00274))_
- Gods who have lost their physical bodies cannot engage with the material world , which is the world for those with bodies. Instead, they interact with the world through priests. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00275))_
- Priests, as agents of the gods, can use holy prayer and cast holy magic . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00276))_
