## Statements

- This is a magical crystal ball that shows the user every place around the world. The extent to which the effect reaches depends on the magical power stored in the crystal ball. Particularly powerful ones are said to be able to ignore magical barriers that would completely block a normal crystal ball, or show the state of another world. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09631))_
- The commonly found Crystal Orb of Clairvoyance can project any place you've been to before , but can only use this power once per day . The duration is 15 minutes. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09632))_
