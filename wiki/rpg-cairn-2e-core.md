## Overview & Principles

### Overview

#### Neutrality

- The Warden's role is to act as a neutral arbiter and portray the rules, situations, non-player characters (NPCs), and narrative clearly. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00009))_

#### Classless

- Instead, the equipment they carry and their experiences define their specialty. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00011))_

#### Death

- Characters may be powerful, but they are also vulnerable to harm in its many forms. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00013))_
- Death is always around the corner, but it is never random or without warning. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00013))_

#### Fiction First

- Dice do not always reflect an obstacle's difficulty or its outcome. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00015))_

#### Player Choice

### Principles for Wardens

#### Information

- - Provide useful information about the game world as the characters explore it. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00052))_
- - Players do not need to roll dice to learn about their circumstances. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00053))_
- - Be helpful and direct with your answers to their questions. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00054))_
- - Respond honestly, describe consistently, and always let them know they can keep asking questions. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00055))_

#### Difficulty

- - Saves cover a great deal of uncertain situations and are often all that is necessary for risky actions. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00060))_

**Atom:** _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00058))_

> - If something the players want to do is sincerely impossible, no roll will allow them to do it.

#### Preparation

- It intuits and makes sharp turns. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00062))_
- - The game world is organic, malleable and random. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00062))_
- - Use random tables and generators to develop situations, not stories or plots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00063))_
- - NPCs remember what the PCs say and do, and how they affect the world. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00064))_
- Infuse their own self-interest and will to live into every personality. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00065))_

#### Narrative Focus

- - Emergent experience of play is what matters, not math or character abilities. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00067))_
- - Pay attention to the needs and wants of the players, then put realistic opportunities in their path. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00068))_
- - A dagger to your throat will kill you, regardless of your expensive armor and impressive training. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00069))_

#### Danger

- - Telegraph serious danger to players when it is present. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00072))_
- - Give players opportunities to solve problems and interact with the world. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00074))_

#### Treasure

- - A Treasure is specific to the environment from where it is recovered. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00076))_
- - Treasure is highly valuable, almost always bulky, and rarely useful beyond its worth and prestige. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00077))_
- - Relics are not Treasure, though they are useful and interesting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00078))_
- - Use Treasure as a lure to exotic locations under the protection of intimidating foes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00079))_

#### Choice

- - Give players a solid choice to force outcomes when the situation lulls. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00081))_
- - Use binary "so, A or B?" responses when their intentions are vague. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00082))_

### Attributes

- - Player Characters have just three Attributes: ( Strength (STR) , Dexterity (DEX) , and Willpower (WIL) ). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00101))_
- A character with a low STR is not necessarily hopelessly weak; they can still attempt to lift a heavy door or survive a deadly fight! _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00103))_
- - Attributes are not universal descriptors. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00103))_
- Their risk is simply higher. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00103))_

- Bonds can often tie a player character to another PC, the setting, and its characters. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00666))_
- Player characters are not required to resolve their Bonds. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00666))_
- In this case, the character should experience Growth of some kind, and the Warden can optionally create a new Bond for the PC. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00666))_

#### Bonds Should...

- - Give a character an item (often petty ) or a unique ability. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00668))_
- - Encourage character engagement with the implied setting. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00669))_
- - Flesh out a character's history without relying on events tied to specific Backgrounds. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00670))_
- - Provide a character with a quest, opportunity, or plan of action. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00671))_

### Omens

- An Omen is resolved when the characters reveal the truth behind its origin or resolve its dangers. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00673))_
- Resolving an Omen should yield Growth of some kind, either for the party as a collective or to an entangled individual. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00673))_
- Omens are the seeds of an adventure yet to come, premonitions of a future disaster, or the cause of a problem the characters are currently facing. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00673))_

- - Alway tell the characters something about the setting they did not already know. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00676))_
