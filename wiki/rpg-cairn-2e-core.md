## Save Principles

- - When in doubt, ask whether the party has the time, resources, skills, or advantage to overcome a problem. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00555))_
- If the answer is "no," then a save is usually warranted. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00555))_
- If the answer is "no," then a save is usually warranted. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00555))_
- If the risk is so great that the outcome might be fatal, make sure the player understands what they're getting into. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00557))_
- The more choices, the more interesting the situation becomes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00558))_
- - The outcome of a save need not be a simple binary. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00559))_
- This doesn't mean revealing all of the world's secrets, but provide each player with the maximum information their character might have. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00560))_
- - Avoid requiring a dice roll whenever possible. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00561))_
- - Replace multiple dice rolls with a single dice throw. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00562))_

> If the answer is "I don't know," then a roll with the Die of Fate may be necessary.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00555))_

> If the party cannot proceed due to circumstances beyond their control, give them another option to consider.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00556))_

> - Feel free to say "no," but remember to offer an alternative. If the party cannot proceed due to circumstances beyond their control, give them another option to consider.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00556))_

> - Character death should never come as a surprise.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00557))_

> - Always provide multiple viable options for the PCs to choose from.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00558))_

> You can tailor the results based on the totality of input: the effort, forethought, resources, and skill that went into the roll.
