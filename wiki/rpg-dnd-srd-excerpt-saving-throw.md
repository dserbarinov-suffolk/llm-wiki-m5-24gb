# Saving Throws

What [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]] covers about saving throws:

## Statements

- As with skill proficiencies, proficiency in a saving throw lets a character add his or her proficiency bonus to saving throws made using a particular ability score. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00236))_
- A saving throw-also called a save-represents an attempt to resist a spell, a trap, a poison, a disease, or a similar threat. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00233))_
- You don't normally decide to make a saving throw; you are forced to make one because your character or monster is at risk of harm. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00233))_
- To make a saving throw, roll a d20 and add the appropriate ability modifier. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00234))_
- For example, you use your Dexterity modifier for a Dexterity saving throw. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00234))_
- Each class gives proficiency in at least two saving throws. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00236))_
- Some monsters have saving throw proficiencies as well. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00236))_
- The result of a successful or failed saving throw is also detailed in the effect that allows the save. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00238))_
- Usually, a successful save means that a creature suffers no harm, or reduced harm, from an effect. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00238))_
- The wizard, for example, is proficient in Intelligence saves. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00236))_
