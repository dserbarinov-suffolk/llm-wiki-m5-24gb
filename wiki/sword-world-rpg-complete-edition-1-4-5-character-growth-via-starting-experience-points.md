# 1.4.5 Character Growth via Starting Experience Points

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- A character has already acquired certain skills (starting skills) according to Table 1-5: Backgrounds or Table 1-6: Starting Experience by Race. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- In addition to this, starting experience points can also be used to acquire new skills or increase a character's skills. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- However, the skills that can be acquired or increased are limited to those of adventurer skills, and general skills cannot be acquired or increased. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- This should be understood as purchasing skills with experience points . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- You may even acquire 2 or more levels at a time, so long as there are enough experience points to spend. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- It is also possible to increase the level of skills that have already been acquired. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- Note, however, that experience points are spent at each level attained. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- Only adventurer skills can be acquired or increased using experience points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- General skills cannot be acquired or increased with experience points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- Leaving them in place will give you an advantage in the growth of your character after the game. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- In addition, starting experience points do not have to be used up. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- Ducard II has 2500 experience points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- In addition, we can even acquire 2 levels in thief at once (2500-1000-1500= 0 remaining). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- With these, for example, we could acquire 1 level in sorcerer (25002000= 500 remaining) or increase fighter to 2 levels (2500-1500= 1000 remaining). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- This leaves us 500 points, which we'll save for future use . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.20)
- When you have the sorcerer skill, there's a few things to be careful of. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- You may acquire sorcerer only, but it's better to avoid this, as it will put you at a disadvantage during the game. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- This skill is closely related to the sage skill and requires fewer experience points when acquiring both. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- The sage skill is useful enough on its own. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- Of course, having the sage skill does not mean that you're required to have the sorcerer skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- It is important to note that it is against the rules for a character who has neither the sorcerer nor sage skills to acquire only the sorcerer skill for 1500 points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- Table 1-7: Experience Points by Skill (at Character Creation) is almost identical to a segment of Table 8-1: Experience Points by Skill, which is used for character growth as explained in chapter 8. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
- As this shows , the use of starting experience points is exactly the same as the use of experience points during character growth, so it might be a good idea to read the explanation in chapter 8 first. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.21)
