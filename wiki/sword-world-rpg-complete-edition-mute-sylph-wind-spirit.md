- Those under the influence of this spell can act without making any sound at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.88)
- This spell differs from Silence in that it can be used on an individual to silence only the voices and sounds they make. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.88)
