## Key supported claims

- Quasi-literals are expressions that resemble their result, denoted with back quotes. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Name bindings in quasi-literals are evaluated at function invocation time, not at declaration. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Iterating over collections involves acting on elements one at a time, with a standard method in JavaScript. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Objects can return iterators for themselves, enabling functional iteration. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
