## Statements

- This score is the target score for a success roll when using the sage skill's treasure appraisal ability. If the success roll results in a final score greater than or equal to this rarity, you will discover the true identity of the treasure. However, some treasures may have more than one listed rarity. In this case, the treasure appraisal may be incomplete depending on the attained final score.
