## Statements

- This is the ability to handle small boats that are moved by poles or oars, and move them as desired. If you're just going straight through calm water, you don't need a success roll. In special cases such as avoiding collision with an approaching ship or surviving rapids , you must succeed on a success roll using sailor skill level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score. The difficulty is determined by the game master. If you fail, the boat will capsize. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04361))_
