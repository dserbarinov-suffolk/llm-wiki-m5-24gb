- Base Mental Power Cost=30 Distance=Caster Area=Caster Duration=Instant Effect=Be reborn as a dragon Type=Non-removable, ritual Expansion=Final score _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05200))_
- The ultimate dragon roar magic spell. It is no exaggeration to say that dragon priests undergo rigorous training for this spell. Upon using this spell, death will come to the dragon priest. At this time, the caster must make a success roll to determine a final score. If this final score does not reach 20, the caster will die as they are. They cannot be revived by any means. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05202))_
- If a final score of 20 or greater is attained, then after the caster's death, their corpse will be wrapped in a dragon egg, and a few years later, will be reborn as a lesser dragon. The egg cannot be destroyed by any means. Even Perfect Cancellation is ineffective. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05203))_
- When you're reborn, your memories of your previous life will be lost, but your potential as a dragon is high, and after several decades you will shed your skin and transform into an elder dragon. At this time, along with the memories from when you became a dragon, the memories and skills you had during your lifetime will be resurrected. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05204))_
