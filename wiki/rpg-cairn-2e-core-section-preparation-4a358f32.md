# Preparation

From [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]].

## Statements

- The game world is organic, malleable and random. It intuits and makes sharp turns. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00062))_
- Use random tables and generators to develop situations, not stories or plots. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00063))_
- NPCs remember what the PCs say and do, and how they affect the world. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00064))_
- NPCs don't want to die. Infuse their own self-interest and will to live into every personality. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00065))_
