## Key supported claims

- Copy-on-read strategy makes a copy when reading a child of the list to allow modifications without affecting the original. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Copy-on-write strategy delays copying until a write operation to prevent unnecessary duplication. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Copy-on-write ensures changes to shared information are isolated to the private copy, preventing interference with other tasks. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- mapWith function could be expensive if copies are made repeatedly during rest(node) calls. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
