## Statements

- Basically, automatic success and automatic failure are not definitive success or definitive failure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.31)
- It simply means that you succeeded or failed, not that you succeeded very well or failed very badly . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.31)
