# Combat in Which Monsters Roll Dice

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- In Sword World RPG , the monster side (game master) rarely rolls dice during combat. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- This reduces the burden on the game master and allows him or her to focus on other parts. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- By rolling the dice yourself, you can feel like you're playing the game. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- However, some game masters may also want to roll the dice. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- In an emergency, the game master can control the results of his or her own dice, making it easier to weave a story. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
- This is because these advantages exist. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.261)
