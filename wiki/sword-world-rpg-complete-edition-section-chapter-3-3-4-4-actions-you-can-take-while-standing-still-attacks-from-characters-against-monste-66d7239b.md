- If base damage is 14, the total damage is 14+4= 18 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01250))_
- When determining base damage (strike roll), if you suddenly roll double ones, the damage will always be 0 . No matter how much bonus damage you have, it means nothing. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01251))_
