# One-Handed and TwoHanded Weapons

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- A two- handed weapon will deal more damage to an opponent than a onehanded weapon of the same required strength. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- 2H stands for two- handed . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- 1H stands for one- handed . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- To determine whether a weapon is one-handed or two-handed, see the usage column on Table 1-8: Weapons. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
- 1~2H means the weapon can be wielded with one or both hands. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.22)
