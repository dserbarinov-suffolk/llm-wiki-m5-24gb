### Youma

- Kobolds are small youma with doglike faces and tails. They have no body hair at all. They live in forests, hills, and mountains near human villages, but they are timid and rarely cause harm to human homes. However, kobolds are also known to be sneaky and will attack opponents who are outnumbered, wounded, or unable to fight, such as women and children. They usually attack with their fangs, but they may also use a piece of wood as a club (in terms of data, they are the same). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-06042))_
