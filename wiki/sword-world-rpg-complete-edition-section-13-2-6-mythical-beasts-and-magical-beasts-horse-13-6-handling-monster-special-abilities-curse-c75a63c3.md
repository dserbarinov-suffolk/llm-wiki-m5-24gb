## Statements

- This is an attack method that brings disaster to those involved. There are several processes by which a curse can be brought about (such as normal physical attacks, by gaze, etc.), but in all cases you must ultimately make a mental power resistance roll against a set target score. If you fail, you will be affected by the curse. There are various types of curses. A typical mummy's curse prevents the recovery of all ability score reductions, including life force and mental power. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-08766))_
