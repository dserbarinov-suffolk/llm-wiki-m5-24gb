## Statements

- By casting this spell, the caster can turn any target that fails (or does not attempt) to resist into an animal of their choice. The targets of this spell can only be animals and plants created by the hands of the gods, including humans, fae, and youma, and do not include mythical beasts, magical beasts, titans, etc. Since they're not living things, undead and magical creatures are also impossible. Creatures that have already been transformed are also included in this limitation. The target will be completely transformed physically and will be able to use all of the creature's physical abilities after transformation (flying, breathing underwater, etc.). In addition, their memories, intelligence, and soul from before the transformation will be passed on to the new body. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02484))_
