## Statements

- This is a spell that reduces the target's agility to half its original score. Targets who fail to resist will have their action order and movement speed reduced by half due to changes in their agility, and their evasion speed (points) will also take a penalty. Regarding the evasion speed (points) penalty, the effect differs depending on the target's original agility. Please refer to Table 5-3: Slow: Agility and Evasion Penalties. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02466))_
