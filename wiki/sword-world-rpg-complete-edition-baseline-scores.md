Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- His fighter skill level is 2 and his dexterity bonus is +2. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.30)

- Fighter is only useful in combat. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.30)
