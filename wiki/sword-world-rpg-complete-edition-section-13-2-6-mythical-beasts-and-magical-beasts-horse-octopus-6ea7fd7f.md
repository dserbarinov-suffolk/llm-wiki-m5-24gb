## Statements

- An octopus is a sea-dwelling mollusk that uses its eight tentacles to entangle and strangle its prey to death. On rare occasions, they may come ashore, in which case they suffer a -4 penalty to hit and evasion. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07417))_
- There are many different types of octopus, but most of them are small and harmless. Here we will only explain the two dangerous types known as killer and giant . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07418))_
- Monster Level=3 Rarity=13 Agility=12 Movement Speed=3/15 (water) Number=One Frequency=Moderate Intellect=Animal Reaction=Hunger-based Attack Points=Strangle:10 (3) Strike Points=8 Evasion Points=11 (4) Defense Points=5 Life Points/Resistance=17/11 (4) Mental Points/Resistance=6/10 (3) Special Abilities=Strangling Water adaptation Habitat=Sunken ships, underwater ruins Languages=None _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07420))_
- Perception=Five senses The killer octopus is an aggressive octopus with tentacles up to 2 meters long. They kill and eat large fish, but they also attack human swimmers. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07421))_
- A killer octopus will use all of its tentacles to attack one target, constricting the opponent with its entire body. Please follow the rules for strangling (see p. 236). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07422))_
- The giant octopus is almost legendary, feared by fishermen and sailors as the devil of the sea. Its tentacles, which are up to 10 meters long, can entangle boats and sink them. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07441))_
- A giant octopus can use each of its eight tentacles to perform a strangling (see p. 236) attack on separate targets. If you deal 5 or more points of damage (14 or more points of damage if defense points are taken into account) to a giant octopus with a bladed weapon, you'll have cut off one of its tentacles, and the octopus' number of attacks will be reduced by one. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-07442))_
