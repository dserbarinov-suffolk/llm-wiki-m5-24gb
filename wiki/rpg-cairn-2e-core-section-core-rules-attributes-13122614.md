## Statements

- Each of the three Attributes are used in different circumstances. (See Saves , below.) _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00158))_
- Strength (STR) : Used for saves requiring physical power, like lifting gates, bending bars, resisting poison, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00159))_
- Dexterity (DEX) : Used for saves requiring poise, speed, reflexes, dodging, climbing, sneaking, balancing, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00160))_
- Willpower (WIL) : Used for saves to persuade, deceive, interrogate, intimidate, charm, provoke, manipulate spells, etc. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00161))_
