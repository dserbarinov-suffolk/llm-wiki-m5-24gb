## Statements

- This is a spell that inflicts a plague on the target. There are various types of this plague. Each game master should specify the symptoms. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04984))_
- Regardless of the symptoms specified by the game master, the plague's rarity is 16. Also, the progression speed/progression Intensity/ Healing score is 3 days/8/11 (22). It has a lethal depth of 3 and is very highly contagious. Note that the caster of the spell also becomes a target of the contagion immediately after the target contracts the illness. Also, if the target successfully resists the spell, they'll suffer no effect. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04985))_
- For detailed rules regarding illnesses, please refer to 12.2.2: Illnesses (see p. 164). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04986))_
- Note that the caster of the spell also becomes a target of the contagion immediately after the target contracts the illness. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04985))_

### Chapter 11: Notes on Magic / 11.2 Dark Magic / 9th Level Basic Dark Magic List [ Plague ] / [ Recall Spirit ]

- This spell is almost the same as the divine magic of the same name, except that it uses the body of a sacrifice instead of the caster to bring the dead back to life. Only those that the caster knows well (or at least is acquainted with) can be resurrected. Also, those who have lost their souls and are destroyed, or those who have become undead, cannot be resurrected. The person who was sacrificed loses their soul container and cannot be resurrected. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04997))_
- This spell requires a final score of 20 or greater while also defeating the sacrifice's resist for it to be fully effective. Even if you defeat the sacrifice's resist, if you do not attain a final score of 20 or greater, the dead who was resurrected will lose their memories. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04998))_
