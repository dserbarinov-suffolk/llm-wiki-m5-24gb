# 4.6.3 Defense Rolls

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- For example, if your defense power is 7, you would look at key number column 7 with on the Rating Table . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- This process is specifically called a defense roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- Since Ducard II has a defense power of 7 , we'll use key number column 7. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- This is the damage that Ducard II is able to prevent with his armor. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- The number on row 3 under key number column 7 is 0 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- In this case, your character cannot reduce the monster's damage (strike points) at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
