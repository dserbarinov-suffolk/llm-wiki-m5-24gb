# 4.6.3 Defense Rolls

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- For example, if your defense power is 7, you would look at key number column 7 with on the Rating Table . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- This process is specifically called a defense roll . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- Since Ducard II has a defense power of 7 , we'll use key number column 7. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- This is the damage that Ducard II is able to prevent with his armor. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- The number on row 3 under key number column 7 is 0 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)
- In this case, your character cannot reduce the monster's damage (strike points) at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.47)

**Atom:** rule

Characters can reduce the damage they receive from monsters by means of armor.

<a id="atom-3"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When making a defense roll, if your roll is double ones, that means that you've been hit extremely hard.
```

<a id="atom-4"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When making a defense roll, criticals do not occur like they do with a strike roll.
```

<a id="atom-5"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
You cannot roll the dice again to increase this number further.
```

<a id="atom-6"></a>
**Atom:** example

```
Suppose Ducard II's defense roll came up 12 (double sixes!). The number on row 12 under key number column 7 is 6 . Unlike a strike roll, a defense roll is made only once. You cannot roll the dice again to increase this number further.
```
