## Statements

- When buying weapons and armor, one must be aware of their required strength . Even swords of the same shape are sold in a variety of sizes to match the physical strength of the person using it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00612))_
- In real life, when a baseball player chooses a bat for himself, he chooses one that matches his physical strength. For an adventurer, the first step to survival is to choose a weapon most suited to their strength. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00613))_
- Basically, the most suitable weapon is one whose required strength equals the character's strength . As long as you choose that, you are guaranteed the same attack capability, even if you use a different type of weapon. Think of a sword, ax, or spear with a required strength of 14 as the same in terms of game value. Therefore, it's a good idea to choose one based on the image of your character. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00614))_
- However, most weapons have a limited range of required strength. It's a constraint that comes with the general size and nature of the weapon. Therefore, if the required strength of the weapon you're buying on Table 1-8: Weapons does not match your character's strength, you should not buy that weapon. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00615))_
