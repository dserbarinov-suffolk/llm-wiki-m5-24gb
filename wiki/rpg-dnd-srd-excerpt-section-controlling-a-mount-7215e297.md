# Controlling a Mount

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- You can either control the mount or allow it to act independently. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00463))_
- While you're mounted, you have two options. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00463))_
- The initiative of a controlled mount changes to match yours when you mount it. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00463))_
- It moves as you direct it, and it has only three action options: Dash, Disengage, and Dodge. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00463))_
- Intelligent creatures, such as dragons, act independently. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00463))_
- It moves as you direct it, and it has only three action options: Dash, Disengage, and Dodge. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00463))_
- mount can move and act even on the turn that you mount it. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00464))_
- Bearing a rider puts no restrictions on the actions the mount can take, and it moves and acts as it wishes. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00465))_
- It might flee from combat, rush to attack and devour a badly injured foe, or otherwise act against your wishes. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00465))_
