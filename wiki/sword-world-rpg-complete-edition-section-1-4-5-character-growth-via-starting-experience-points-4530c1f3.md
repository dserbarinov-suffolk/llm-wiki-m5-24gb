## Statements

- A character has already acquired certain skills (starting skills) according to Table 1-5: Backgrounds or Table 1-6: Starting Experience by Race. In addition to this, starting experience points can also be used to acquire new skills or increase a character's skills. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00448))_
- However, the skills that can be acquired or increased are limited to those of adventurer skills, and general skills cannot be acquired or increased. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00449))_
- This should be understood as purchasing skills with experience points . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00452))_
- It is also possible to increase the level of skills that have already been acquired. You may even acquire 2 or more levels at a time, so long as there are enough experience points to spend. Note, however, that experience points are spent at each level attained. For example, if you do not have the ranger skill and you want to acquire 2 levels in ranger at once, you must spend 500 + 1000 for a total of 1500 experience points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00453))_
- Only adventurer skills can be acquired or increased using experience points. General skills cannot be acquired or increased with experience points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00455))_
- In addition, starting experience points do not have to be used up. Leaving them in place will give you an advantage in the growth of your character after the game. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00456))_
- Ducard II has 2500 experience points. With these, for example, we could acquire 1 level in sorcerer (25002000= 500 remaining) or increase fighter to 2 levels (2500-1500= 1000 remaining). In addition, we can even acquire 2 levels in thief at once (2500-1000-1500= 0 remaining). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00458))_
- Here, we'll increase fighter to 2nd level and also acquire 1 level in ranger. This leaves us 500 points, which we'll save for future use. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00459))_
- When you have the sorcerer skill, there's a few things to be careful of. This skill is closely related to the sage skill and requires fewer experience points when acquiring both. If a character has neither the sorcerer nor sage skill, they can acquire 1 level in both the sorcerer and sage skills by spending 2000 experience points. You may acquire sorcerer only, but it's better to avoid this, as it will put you at a disadvantage during the game. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00489))_
- If a character has 1 level in the sage skill (so if they're a human or half-elf, and if merchant/scholar or noble was rolled on Table 1-5: Backgrounds), they can acquire 1 level in the sorcerer skill for 1500 points. Of course, having the sage skill does not mean that you're required to have the sorcerer skill. The sage skill is useful enough on its own. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00490))_
- It is important to note that it is against the rules for a character who has neither the sorcerer nor sage skills to acquire only the sorcerer skill for 1500 points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00491))_
- Table 1-7: Experience Points by Skill (at Character Creation) is almost identical to a segment of Table 8-1: Experience Points by Skill, which is used for character growth as explained in chapter 8. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00492))_

### 1.4.5 Character Growth via Starting Experience Points / Required Strength

- When buying weapons and armor, one must be aware of their required strength . Even swords of the same shape are sold in a variety of sizes to match the physical strength of the person using it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00633))_
- In real life, when a baseball player chooses a bat for himself, he chooses one that matches his physical strength. For an adventurer, the first step to survival is to choose a weapon most suited to their strength. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00634))_
- Basically, the most suitable weapon is one whose required strength equals the character's strength . As long as you choose that, you are guaranteed the same attack capability, even if you use a different type of weapon. Think of a sword, ax, or spear with a required strength of 14 as the same in terms of game value. Therefore, it's a good idea to choose one based on the image of your character. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00635))_
- However, most weapons have a limited range of required strength. It's a constraint that comes with the general size and nature of the weapon. Therefore, if the required strength of the weapon you're buying on Table 1-8: Weapons does not match your character's strength, you should not buy that weapon. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00636))_

### 1.4.5 Character Growth via Starting Experience Points / One-Handed and TwoHanded Weapons

- A two-handed weapon will deal more damage to an opponent than a onehanded weapon of the same required strength. Of course, if you have a twohanded weapon, you cannot hold a shield, which will make your character less capable of defense. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00638))_
- To determine whether a weapon is one-handed or two-handed, see the usage column on Table 1-8: Weapons. 1H stands for one-handed . 2H stands for two-handed . 1~2H means the weapon can be wielded with one or both hands. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00639))_
- 1~2H means the weapon can be wielded with one or both hands. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00639))_

### 1.4.5 Character Growth via Starting Experience Points / Projectiles

- On Table 1-8: Weapons, weapons with made for throwing or shooting after the weapon's name are strictly projectile weapons. Note that these cannot be used in melee. Throwable weapons are basically used in melee, although they can be thrown when the need arises. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00641))_

### 1.4.5 Character Growth via Starting Experience Points / Armor

- The principle of choose one whose required strength equals the character's strength applies to armor as well. There are several types, but choose which one you think is appropriate (Table 1-9: Armor). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00753))_

### 1.4.5 Character Growth via Starting Experience Points / Shields

- In the case of shields, there are only two types: small shields (required strength 1) and large shields (required strength 13). If a character's strength is 13 or greater, a large shield can be chosen. However, in terms of increasing a character's defensive ability, the effects of small and large shields are basically the same in this game. Therefore, it is safe to choose the less expensive small shield (Table 1-10: Shields). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00755))_
