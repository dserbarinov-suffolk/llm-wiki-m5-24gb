## Statements

- In Sword World RPG , the monster side (game master) rarely rolls dice during combat. This reduces the burden on the game master and allows him or her to focus on other parts. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09889))_
- However, some game masters may also want to roll the dice. By rolling the dice yourself, you can feel like you're playing the game. When both sides roll the dice, the range of numbers will expand and the enjoyment can increase. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09890))_
