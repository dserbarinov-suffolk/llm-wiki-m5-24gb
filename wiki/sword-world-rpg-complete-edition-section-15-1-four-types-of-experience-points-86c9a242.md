## Statements

- In Sword World RPG , there are four ways to gain experience points. These are accomplishing missions , fighting monsters , success rolls , and special bonuses . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09992))_

## Statements by subsection

### 15.1 Four Types of Experience Points / Accomplishing Missions

- Most experience points are earned by accomplishing missions. When creating an adventure scenario, the game master sets one goal. If you're going into ruins to search for treasure, the goal is probably the treasure hidden there, and if you're rescuing a captured dignitary, it' s to bring that person back safely. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09994))_
- This may be different from what it appears to be upon entering the scenario. Upon closer examination, a request to transport an item may turn out to be stolen goods, and in a change of situation, must be returned to its rightful owner. At this time, the mission will only be accomplished if it's returned to its rightful owner. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09996))_
- If you were to become one of the thieves' transport partners without realizing they're stolen goods, your mission would not be accomplished. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09997))_
- At this time, the mission will only be accomplished if it's returned to its rightful owner. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09996))_

### 15.1 Four Types of Experience Points / Fighting Monsters

- You can also earn experience points by defeating monsters. Monster level x 10 ÷ number of adventurers experience points will be awarded for each monster. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10001))_

### 15.1 Four Types of Experience Points / Success Rolls

- This depends on how much work the adventurers did during the adventure. How assertively the adventurers acted is reflected by the number of dice rolls made by the players. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10003))_
- However, it can be difficult to remember every time the dice were rolled, so only the number of times a player rolled double ones during a success roll should be recorded. Double ones when making checks for damage dealt in combat or armor defense effects in combat should also be added to the number of times. The adventurers will earn number of double ones x 10 experience points. However, double ones after a critical during damage checks are not included. Also, experience points should not be awarded for meaningless success rolls. Unlike the previous two types, experience points earned in this way will vary depending on the adventurer. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10004))_

### 15.1 Four Types of Experience Points / Special Bonuses

- The game master is free to award bonus experience for particularly outstanding play. However, this should be done as sparingly as possible. Giving away a large amount of experience points is harmful to the enjoyment of the game. It'd be most appropriate to keep it to about 1/10th of the experience points earned from accomplishing a mission. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10006))_
