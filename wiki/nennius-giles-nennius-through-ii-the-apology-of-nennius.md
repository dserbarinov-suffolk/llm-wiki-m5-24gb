- Nennius humbly requests readers to overlook the inelegance of his words and focus on the truth of his account. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- Nennius acknowledges the importance of truth over eloquence and encourages readers to value accuracy over presentation. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
- Nennius expresses his concern about the fading memory of the Britons and his determination to preserve their legacy. (raw/nennius_giles.pdf)
