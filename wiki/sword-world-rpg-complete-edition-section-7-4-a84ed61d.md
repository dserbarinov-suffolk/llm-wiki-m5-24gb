### Healer Skill

- Since there's magic in the world of Sword World , medicine has not made much progress. However, there are not that many rune masters, so in most cases it is the job of doctors called healers to heal injuries and illnesses. Healers are knowledgeable in basic medicine and herbs, and are able to provide appropriate treatment for injuries and illnesses.
