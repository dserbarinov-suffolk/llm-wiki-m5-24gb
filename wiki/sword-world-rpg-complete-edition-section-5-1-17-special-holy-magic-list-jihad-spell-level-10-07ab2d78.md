## Statements

- This spell is used when a high priest of Pharis discovers a great evil that absolutely cannot be overlooked. Gather as many Pharis believers as you can to a temple, and cast this in front of them. As a result, anyone who agrees to the spell, including the person who cast it, will experience the following effects. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03786))_
- 1. Temporarily increase fighter skill level by 2 (+2 bonus to attack power, evasion speed, and damage. Those with fighter skill 0 will be temporarily given 2 levels) 2. Absolute obedience to the caster's commands (even if it is a suicidal act) _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03787))_
- It has no impact at all on those who do not agree to the spell, but no devout follower of Pharis would not agree to the high priest's spell. The caster can also exclude anyone from the spell, even if another person willingly agrees. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03788))_
