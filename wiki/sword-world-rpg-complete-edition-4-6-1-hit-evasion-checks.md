# 4.6.1 Hit (Evasion) Checks

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This check is also made with a success roll. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- The target score is still different for each monster, but it's called the monster's attack points . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- The baseline score is the character's evasion speed . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- evasion speed + 2D < monster's attack points → evasion failure (monster's attack hits). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- ※ Double sixes will automatically evade, and double ones will automatically fail. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- Since this is a success roll, double sixes and double ones are automatic successes and automatic failures , respectively. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- Ducard II has an evasion speed of 5 (fighter level 2, agility bonus +2, has a shield). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- On the other hand, the goblin has 10 attack points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- 5+4= 9 , which is short of the target score (the goblin's attack points) of 10. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
- The player rolls the dice to determine if they evade the goblin's attack. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.46)
