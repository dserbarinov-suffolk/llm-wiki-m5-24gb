## Statements

- Just as there are many different kinds of people in the real world, strong (or weak), smart (or dumb), etc., there are many different kinds of people in Forcelia. In Sword World RPG, these basic characteristics of an adventurer are represented by numbers called ability scores . There are six types of ability scores: dexterity , agility , intelligence , strength , life force , and mental power . The higher the number for each, the better the ability. Ability scores apply not only to humans, but also to the various non-human races that become adventurers. Human ability scores range from 4-24 (14 on average) for each. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00138))_

## Statements by subsection

### 8. Game Master Screen / Ability Scores / 【 Strength 】

- Strength represents an adventurer's muscular strength. If you want to use large weapons, heavy armor, etc., high strength is required. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00146))_

### 8. Game Master Screen / Ability Scores / 【 Life Force 】

- Life force represents how well an adventurer can withstand physical injury. The higher this number, the more robust the adventurer is. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00148))_

### 8. Game Master Screen / Ability Scores / Loss of Ability Scores and Its Impact

- While adventuring, adventurers often suffer physical and mental injuries and fatigue. In Sword World RPG , this is represented by a loss of life force and mental power (point reduction). If either life force or mental power fall to 0 or less, the adventurer will fall unconscious and be unable to act. The four other ability scores of dexterity, agility, intelligence, and strength are rarely reduced, but if this does happen and these ability scores do fall to 0 or less, the adventurer will still become disabled. At this time, whether they're conscious or not depends on the situation. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00152))_
- If life force and mental power are reduced by wounds or exhaustion, they will recover once the wounds are treated or the exhaustion is wiped away. There are various conditions for these reductions and recoveries, which you will discover as you read through this book. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00153))_
