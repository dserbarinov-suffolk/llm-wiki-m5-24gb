## Statements

- Just as there are many different kinds of people in the real world, strong (or weak), smart (or dumb), etc., there are many different kinds of people in Forcelia. In Sword World RPG, these basic characteristics of an adventurer are represented by numbers called ability scores . There are six types of ability scores: dexterity , agility , intelligence , strength , life force , and mental power . The higher the number for each, the better the ability. Ability scores apply not only to humans, but also to the various non-human races that become adventurers. Human ability scores range from 4-24 (14 on average) for each. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00138))_
- Dexterity represents the dexterity of an adventurer's hands. It relates to a variety of tasks that involve the use of one's hands. This is especially important when wielding weapons in combat. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00140))_
- Agility represents an adventurer's physical balance and agility. It's related to working with the whole body. In combat, it is important when evading enemy attacks. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00142))_
- Intelligence represents the speed of an adventurer's mind and the amount of knowledge they hold, or in other words, their so-called smarts. It also represents the ability to perceive things, such as finding hidden objects and sensing abnormalities spur of the moment. Above all, it is important when using magic, a major attraction in fantasy. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00144))_
- Strength represents an adventurer's muscular strength. If you want to use large weapons, heavy armor, etc., high strength is required. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00146))_
- Life force represents how well an adventurer can withstand physical injury. The higher this number, the more robust the adventurer is. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00148))_
- Mental power represents the adventurer's willpower and perseverance. Using magic is hard work, and one cannot hope to cast much magic without a high mental capacity. Mental power is also related to magic resistance. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00150))_
