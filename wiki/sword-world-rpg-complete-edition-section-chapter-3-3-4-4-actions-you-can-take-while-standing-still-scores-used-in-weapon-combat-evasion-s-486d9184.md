## Statements

- In combat, characters are not the only ones who can attack. Naturally, the enemy will also attack the characters. To evade an enemy's attack, a score called evasion speed is necessary. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01201))_
- A character with excellent evasion speed will be able to evade enemy attacks more easily. No matter how formidable the weapon, it's not that scary if it doesn't hit. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01202))_
- evasion speed = fighter (thief) skill level + agility bonus If fighter (or thief) skill level is 0 , then evasion speed is 0 regardless of the character's agility bonus. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01203))_
- A character holding a shield receives a +1 bonus to evasion speed. The size of the shield (whether it is a small or large shield) is completely irrelevant unless you introduce the optional rule 16.2: Weapon and Armor Modifiers (see p. 262). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01204))_
- A fighter (or thief) skill level of 0 does not prevent you from receiving this shield bonus. Therefore, the evasion speed of such a character will be 1 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01205))_
