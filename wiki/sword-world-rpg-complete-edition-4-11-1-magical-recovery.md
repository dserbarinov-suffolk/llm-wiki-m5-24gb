# 4.11.1 Magical Recovery

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Life force that a character has lost due to damage is relatively easy to recover through the use of magic or items with magical powers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.54)
- The degree of this recovery can be found in the descriptions of each spell or magic item. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.54)
