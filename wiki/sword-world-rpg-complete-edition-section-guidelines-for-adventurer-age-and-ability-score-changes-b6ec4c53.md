# Guidelines for Adventurer Age and Ability Score Changes

From [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]].

## Statements

- In the case of non-human races, they are considered equal to humans until they reach adulthood. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10480))_
- After reaching adulthood (ability scores decrease after the age of 50), please modify as follows. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10481))_
