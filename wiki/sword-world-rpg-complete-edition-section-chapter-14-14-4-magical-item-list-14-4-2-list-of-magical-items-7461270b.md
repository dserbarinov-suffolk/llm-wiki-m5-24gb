### Chapter 14: / 14.4 Magical Item List / 14.4.2 List of Magical Items / 《 Undead Ruler 》

- Rarity=18 Enchanter=Jagrilis, "The Master of Death" Form=Mage's staff engraved with ancient runes Required Strength=5 (Strike power 10) Base Trade Price=400,000 gamels (complete)/27,000 gamels (incomplete) Powers=+1 to attack power and bonus damage. Makes undead monsters obey _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09104))_
- This staff grants its owner the power to make undead monsters obey. When the staff is incomplete , the undead monsters that can be made to obey are limited to those whose total monster level is less than or equal to the owner's mental power . Make a success roll using sorcerer skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score, and reveal the final score. If the score exceeds the target undead monster's mental point resistance, they will obey. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09105))_

### Chapter 14: / 14.4 Magical Item List / 14.4.2 List of Magical Items / 《 Blaster 》

- This magic sword can use unlimited shockwaves (strike power 10, magic power 10), equivalent to the holy magic Force (however, magic expansion is not possible). It can also be used as a magical catalyst. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09178))_

### Chapter 14: / 14.4 Magical Item List / 14.4.2 List of Magical Items / 《 Brain Blade 》

- This sword has knowledge about enemies and speaks to the user's mind, telling him or her the enemy's name and strength in concrete scores. The contents of this knowledge are limited to only what concerns the enemy. Also, the sword's language is low ancient, so a character who cannot speak low ancient cannot obtain this knowledge. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09185))_
- The contents of this knowledge are limited to only what concerns the enemy. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09185))_

### Chapter 14: / 14.4 Magical Item List / 14.4.2 List of Magical Items / 《 Mind Eater 》

- Anyone whose life force has been dealt damage by this sword will suffer 2 points of damage to their mental points unless they succeed on a mental power resistance roll against target score 11. This damage cannot be reduced by adventurer level, similar to that of Mental Attack . Also, even if the sword does hit, this effect does not apply if damage is not dealt to life force. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09194))_
- Anyone whose life force has been dealt damage by this sword will suffer 2 points of damage to their mental points unless they succeed on a mental power resistance roll against target score 11. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-09194))_
