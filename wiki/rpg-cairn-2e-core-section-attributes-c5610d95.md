## Statements

- Attributes are not universal descriptors. A character with a low STR is not necessarily hopelessly weak; they can still attempt to lift a heavy door or survive a deadly fight! Their risk is simply higher. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-ad2c341f-00103))_
