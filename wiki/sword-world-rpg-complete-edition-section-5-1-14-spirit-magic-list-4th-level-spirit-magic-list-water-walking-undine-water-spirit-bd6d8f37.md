## Statements

- Countered (Negated) By: Sink Expansion=Final score, duration, distance, targets Resist=Negated effect This spell allows you to walk on water. Those who've had this spell cast on them can walk on water as if they were walking on solid ground. Of course, it doesn't matter how heavy their equipment is. However, when transporting someone else, you must always carry that person on your back. This spell is also a counter spell to Sink , so if you cast this spell after Sink has been cast, the effects of both will be negated, just as if they had been removed by a Dispel Magic spell. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03029))_
- If you cast this spell on something underwater, it will immediately rise to the surface. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03030))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / 4th Level Spirit Magic List [ Water Walking ] (Undine/Water Spirit) / [ Sink ] (Undine/Water Spirit)

- Effect=Eliminates the buoyancy of water, causing floating people and objects to sink Type=Counters (Negates): Water Walking Countered (Negated) By: Water Walking Countered (Overpowered) By: Create Ghost Ship _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03035))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / 4th Level Spirit Magic List [ Water Walking ] (Undine/Water Spirit) / [ Sleep ] (Sandman/Mind Spirit)

- This spell has the effect of putting the target into eternal sleep. The target will remain asleep forever unless it's removed by Dispel Magic , another mind spell, etc. but in the meantime, the target does not starve or grow. They remain asleep forever in the same state they were in when the spell was cast. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03048))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / 4th Level Spirit Magic List [ Water Walking ] (Undine/Water Spirit) / [ Tunnel ] (Gnome/Earth Spirit)

- This is a spell that creates a hole with a 1 meter radius in walls and ground made of stone or earth, as well as the sides and ceilings of caves. This spell allows you to create holes up to 10 meters in length. However, if the wall is less than 10 meters thick, the spell's effect ends there. In other words, you can't open holes in two walls with one spell. The effect of this spell can be expanded regarding the size of the hole being made (target volume). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03057))_
- Also, this spell has absolutely no effect on walls made of wood. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03058))_
- If this spell is used at the feet of a human, etc. those present can attempt to resist. If the resist is successful, the hole will open, but those on top will be able to immediately jump out of the way and prevent themselves from falling into the hole (assuming they have a place to escape, of course). If the resist fails, the hole will open slowly and they will not suffer damage from the fall. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03059))_
- Once the duration has expired, the hole will slowly return to its original state. At this time, those at the bottom of the hole will not be buried alive. This is because the bottom of the hole will rise back up. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03060))_
- This is because the bottom of the hole will rise back up. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03060))_

### 5.1.14 Spirit Magic List / 4th Level Spirit Magic List [ Water Walking ] (Undine/Water Spirit) / [ Fear ] (Shade/Dark Spirit)

- This spell has the effect of instilling fear in the target. An opponent who suffers the effect of this spell will feel an immeasurable fear towards the caster. For those affected, please determine the specific effect according to Table 5-5: Fears. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03079))_
- This spell's effect can be expanded. If expanded, make an equivalent number of checks on the Fears table, then apply which result the caster finds most desirable. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-03080))_
