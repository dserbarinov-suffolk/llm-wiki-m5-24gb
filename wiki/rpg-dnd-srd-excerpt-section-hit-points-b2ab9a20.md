# Hit Points

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Those with fewer hit points are more fragile. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00410))_
- Creatures with more hit points are more difficult to kill. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00410))_
- Hit points represent a combination of physical and mental durability, the will to live, and luck. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00410))_
- A creature's current hit points (usually just called hit points) can be any number from the creature's hit point maximum down to 0. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00411))_
- The loss of hit points has no effect on a creature's capabilities until the creature drops to 0 hit points. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00412))_

## Technical atoms
