# Converting Character Data to Monster Data

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Procedure

- Using the rules presented here, a game master can convert data created as a character into the format of a monster . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- There are two advantages to doing this. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- The number of times the game master has to roll dice during combat will be drastically reduced ]. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- It'll also give you more time to provide guidance to players who are unfamiliar with the rules. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- The game master will be freed from the labor of rolling dice, and will be able to manage enemy data in combat more easily. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- This is especially effective when there are many enemies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- If you change the enemy character's data to that of a monster , the damage will be a fixed score called strike points , so this kind of frustration should be less frequent. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- Rarity seems unnecessary, so we'll omit it. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- Agility and movement speed use the character's agility ability score as it is. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- Attack points are the character's attack power + 7 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- For strike points , apply strike power to Table 13-1: Key Number = Expected Equivalents , then add bonus damage to the result. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- A weretiger is a lycanthrope with the power to shapeshift into a tiger. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- A redcap is a patient suffering from the disease of the same name, red cap , and whose depth has reached 2 or greater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.233)
- 262) to have variation in a weapon's critical target, please look at the appropriate column. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.234)
- Evasion points are the character's evasion speed + 8 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.234)
