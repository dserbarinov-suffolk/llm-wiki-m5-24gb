## Statements

- This ability is almost the same as the sage skill's ability of the same name, but it only applies to speaking, not reading. In other words, bards can converse in languages other than their native language, but they cannot read (or write, of course) text. Unlike the sage skill, there is no restriction on the languages that can be learned. However, of course, you cannot learn runes. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04112))_
- For detailed rules regarding languages, please refer to 12.1: Rules Regarding Language (see p. 157). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04113))_
- Musical instruments are always required to sing spellsongs. Being able to sing is also a necessary condition, but there is an exception if you choose a flute as your instrument. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04116))_
- A bard can learn one spellsong per skill level. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04117))_
- For detailed rules regarding spellsongs, please refer to 5.3: Spellsongs (see p. 109). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04118))_
