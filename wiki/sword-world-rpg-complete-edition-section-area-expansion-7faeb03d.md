- If the area is doubled or tripled ...... the mental power consumed will also be doubled or tripled ....... You can also expand an area by a fraction (x1.5, x8/3, etc.), in which case the fraction is rounded up when determining the multiplier for mental power consumed. So for expansions such as x 1.5 and x 8/3 , the cost would be double and triple , respectively. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01642))_
- Since it's the area that gets expanded, the radius of a circle, a side of a square, etc., can only be increased by the square root of the expansion rate. Please see Table 5-1: Area and Size Expansion. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01643))_
