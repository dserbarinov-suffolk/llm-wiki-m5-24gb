## Statements

- Damage dealt to a character's life force can be recovered in a variety of ways. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01469))_

## Statements by subsection

### 4.10 Excellent Weapons and Armor / and Armor / 4.10.4 Mithril Silver Weapons and Armor / Damage Recovery / 4.11.1 Magical Recovery

- Life force that a character has lost due to damage is relatively easy to recover through the use of magic or items with magical powers. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01471))_
- The degree of this recovery can be found in the descriptions of each spell or magic item. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01472))_

### 4.10 Excellent Weapons and Armor / and Armor / 4.10.4 Mithril Silver Weapons and Armor / Damage Recovery / 4.11.2 First-Aid

- A character with 0 or negative life force will recover up to 1 (positive) point if given treatment by someone with the ranger skill, healer skill, or similar. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01474))_
- This treatment takes 30 minutes . It can also instantly recover life force to 1 point, no matter how great the negative, but it cannot recover life force to 2 or more points. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-01475))_
