## Statements

- Even the most difficult acts can succeed by a quirk of fate. On the other hand, no matter how simple something may seem, it may fail due to some misfortune. This is represented by the automatic success and automatic failure rules. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00937))_
- On a success roll, if a player rolls a 12 , i.e., both dice roll 6 ( double sixes ), the action is a success , no matter how low the baseline score nor how high the target score. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00938))_
- Basically, automatic success and automatic failure are not definitive success or definitive failure. It simply means that you succeeded or failed, not that you succeeded very well or failed very badly . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00942))_
