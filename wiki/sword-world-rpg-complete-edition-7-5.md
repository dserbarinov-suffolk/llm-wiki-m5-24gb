# 7.5

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Healers can help a wounded person recover their life force naturally. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
- This is the ability to appropriately treat illnesses and injuries and aid in their recovery. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
- The patient can recover life force equal to the result plus the healer's intelligence bonus. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
- When a healer is nursing, use the Rating Table with healer skill level as the key number. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
- If the result when using the Rating Table is double ones , there will be no recovery at all (the treatment method was poor, and the result was not good). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
- Normally, natural damage recovery is 1 point per day (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
- It takes at least one hour to use this ability. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.127)
