## Key supported claims

- Arrays generated by expressions like [1, 2, 3] are unique even if they have identical contents. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Floating-point numbers like 042 and 34 have the same internal representation. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Floating-point numbers are not suitable for representing monetary amounts due to precision issues. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Arithmetic operations can be combined into complex expressions like 2 * 5 + 1. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
- Numbers are internally represented as floating point, allowing non-integer values. (raw/javascriptallonge.pdf)
