## Statements

- This spell creates a puppet golem called a bone servant from the bones of a living creature. The bones used for this spell can be human or animal, but they must be of a certain size and consistency, such as ribs or femurs, as very small bones (such as teeth) or fragments are of no use. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02185))_
- A bone servant appears in the form of a skeleton. The form it takes depends on the bones used in the spell. If you use human bones, it'll take the form of a human skeleton, such as a skeleton (see p. 215) or a skeleton warrior (see p. 223), or if you use animal bones, it'll take the form of an animal skeleton. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02186))_
- See p. 223 for bone servant details when using human bones. If bones of other animals are used, the game master is responsible for creating the details. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02187))_
