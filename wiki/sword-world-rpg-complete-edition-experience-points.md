# Experience Points

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- As long as they've lived a certain number of years, they must have had some experience before entering the life of an adventurer. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.18)
- Once ability scores have been determined, the next step is to determine the adventurer's preadventure experience. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.18)
- Even an adventurer who goes on an adventure for the first time is not a baby. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.18)
- Experience points are points given to characters as an indicator of how much experience they have gained as a result of their adventures. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.130)
- Character growth is done using these experience points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.130)
