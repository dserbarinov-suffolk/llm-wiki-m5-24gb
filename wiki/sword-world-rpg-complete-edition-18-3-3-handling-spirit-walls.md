Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- When using these rules for destroying structures to destroy the spirit magic Spirit Wall, please do so as follows. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.279)
- Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's stone. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.279)
- Without expansion, defense points are 15 and destruction points are 20. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.279)

- Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's ice. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.279)
- Without expansion, defense points and destruction points are both 15. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.279)
