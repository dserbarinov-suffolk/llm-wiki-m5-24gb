# 1.3.2 Skill Types

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Skills are grouped into several types. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- There are eight of these: fighter , thief , priest , sorcerer , shaman , sage , ranger , and bard . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- First, skills are divided into two types: adventurer skills and general skills . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- Some of these are useful during an adventure, and it is well worth it for an adventurer to acquire general skills. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- General skills are skills of an ordinary person as opposed to an adventurer, such as merchant , farmer , etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- Each skill consists of a number of abilities appropriate to the skill name, and having a skill level (1 skill level or higher) means that you can demonstrate those abilities. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- The table below shows the names and types of each skill (Table 1-2: Skill Types). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- Half-elves are not a single generation of mixed blood, but are free to have offspring. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- There is no such thing as inheriting only 1/4 of the characteristics of one or the other. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- The child will be a full elf, a half-elf, or a human. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- Even among humans, elves and halfelves can be born from humans who have had elven blood in their past due to intermittent inheritance. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
- These children are called changelings and are looked down upon in human society. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.14)
