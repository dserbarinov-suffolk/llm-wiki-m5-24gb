# Combat Step by Step

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Determine surprise. The GM determines whether anyone involved in the combat encounter is surprised. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00242))_
- Establish positions. The GM decides where all the characters and monsters are located. Given the adventurers' marching order or their stated positions in the room or other location, the GM figures out where the adversaries arehow far away and in what direction. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00243))_
- Roll initiative. Everyone involved in the combat encounter rolls initiative, determining the order of combatants' turns. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00244))_
- Begin the next round. When everyone involved in the combat has had a turn, the round ends. Repeat step 4 until the fighting stops. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00246))_
- The GM decides where all the characters and monsters are located. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00243))_
