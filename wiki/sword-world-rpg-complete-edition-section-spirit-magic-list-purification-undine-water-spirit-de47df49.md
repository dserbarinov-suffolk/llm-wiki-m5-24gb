## Statements

- When you chant this spell, any kind of muddy or poisonous water instantly turns back into pure water. You can also target magical potions (liquid medicine) and poisons, but you must attempt a success roll to do so. Unless otherwise specified, the target score for this type of potion is 15 . In the case of poison, its toxicity score is the target score. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02837))_
- This spell cannot be used on a creature's bodily fluids as a way to deal damage to that creature. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02838))_
- Unless otherwise specified, the target score for this type of potion is 15 . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02837))_
