# Wisdom Checks

What [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]] covers about wisdom checks:

## Statements

- A Wisdom check might reflect an effort to read body language, understand someone's feelings, notice things about the environment, or care for an injured person. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00208))_
- You also make a Wisdom (Animal Handling) check to control your mount when you attempt a risky maneuver. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00209))_
- The GM might ask you to make a Wisdom (Survival) check to follow tracks, hunt wild game, guide your group through frozen wastelands, identify signs that owlbears live nearby, predict the weather, or avoid quicksand and other natural hazards. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00213))_
- The GM might call for a Wisdom check when you try to accomplish tasks like the following: _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00214))_
- Or you might try to spot things that are obscured or easy to miss, whether they are orcs lying in ambush on a road, thugs hiding in the shadows of an alley, or candlelight under a closed secret door. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00212))_
- For example, you might try to hear a conversation through a closed door, eavesdrop under an open window, or hear monsters moving stealthily in the forest. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00212))_

## Technical atoms

> When there is any question whether you can calm down a domesticated animal, keep a mount from getting spooked, or intuit an animal's intentions, the GM might call for a Wisdom (Animal Handling) check.
_(source: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00209))_

> Your Wisdom (Insight) check decides whether you can determine the true intentions of a creature, such as when searching out a lie or predicting someone's next move.
_(source: rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00210))_
