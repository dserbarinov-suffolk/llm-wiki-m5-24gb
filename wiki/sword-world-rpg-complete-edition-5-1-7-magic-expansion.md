5.1.7 Magic Expansion
You  can  increase  the  effectiveness and  certainty  of  magic  by  consuming extra mental power. This is called magic expansion .
Magic expansion is done by doubling or tripling...... the amount of  mental power  consumed.  At  this  time,  you  do not double  or  triple  the  base  mental power  cost.......  Instead,  you  divide  the mental power by your skill level, rounded up , or in other words, the mental power you would consume when not expanding is doubled, tripled...... etc.
Example:
Suppose a sorcerer with sorcerer skill level  2  doubles  the  range  of  his Energy Bolt spell  and  casts  it  on  two  enemies. The  base  mental  power  cost  of Energy Bolt is 5 .  For  this  reason,  the  mental power consumed without expansion is 5 ÷  2,  rounded  up  to 3 .  Therefore,  for  a doubled expansion, the amount of mental power consumed would be 3 x 2 = 6 . Y ou would not calculate (5 x 2) ÷ 2 = 5.
