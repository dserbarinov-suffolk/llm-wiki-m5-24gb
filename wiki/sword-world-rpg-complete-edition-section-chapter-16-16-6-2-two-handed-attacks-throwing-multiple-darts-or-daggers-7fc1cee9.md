- The number thrown at the same time becomes a penalty to attack power. For example, if you throw three at the same time, that'll incur a -3 penalty to your attack power for each hit check. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10318))_
- This optional rule increases the chance that a high-level character will defeat a low-level character or monster in one blow. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10319))_
- You cannot choose multiple targets. There must always be one target. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10320))_
