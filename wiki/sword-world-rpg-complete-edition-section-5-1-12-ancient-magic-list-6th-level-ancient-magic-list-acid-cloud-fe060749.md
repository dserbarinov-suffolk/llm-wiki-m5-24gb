- When you cast this spell, you can turn the air within its area of effect into highly acidic poison gas, dealing strike power 30 damage to everything within it. Acid is fleeting and primarily harms the mucous membranes and respiratory organs of living things. It does not affect armor, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02312))_
