Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Metal armor other than ring mail may not be used, even if the required strength (half of strength) is met. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.40)
- A thief also has the ability to fight, but only with light equipment. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.40)

4.2 Weapon Combat Skills
To  be  good  at  weapon  combat,  a character must have skills. The skills that can be used for this purpose are limited to the following three:
Fighter skill , thief skill , and ranger skill .
【 Fighter Skill 】
The fighter skill is the most basic skill for  weapon  combat.  In  the  following explanation, we will focus on this skill.
4.3
