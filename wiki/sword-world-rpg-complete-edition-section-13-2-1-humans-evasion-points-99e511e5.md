## Statements

- This represents the degree to which the monster can evade attacks. In order for a character to hit a monster with an attack, they must succeed on a success roll using these evasion points as the target score and their attack power as the baseline score. If you fail, your character's attack will miss. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05947))_
