## Statements

- If the attack hits, roll 2D. If it's double ones, the damage will be equal to the strike points of the attacking monster. Otherwise, the damage equals the attacking monster's strike points minus the defending monster's defense points. If the difference is 0 or less, only 1 point of damage will be dealt. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-04752))_
