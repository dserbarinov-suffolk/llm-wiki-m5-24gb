## Statements by subsection

### Chapter 16: / 16.9 Special Weapons / Cranequin Crossbow

- A hoist is attached to a crossbow so that the string can be tightened strongly with a small amount of force. However, it takes time to wind up, so there are significant restrictions on continuous firing. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10340))_

### Chapter 16: / 16.9 Special Weapons / Boomerang

- When this weapon is thrown at an enemy and misses, it returns to the user's hand at the end of the round. However, it will not return if you roll double ones on your hit check or if the boomerang hits the enemy. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10354))_

### Chapter 16: / 16.9 Special Weapons / Shotel

- This weapon has a large S-shaped curve at the tip of the blade. Therefore, you can attack through an enemy's shield. When attacking a shield-wielding enemy with a shotel, you can ignore the shield's evasion bonus (even if it's a magic shield!). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10357))_

### Chapter 16: / 16.9 Special Weapons / Sap (Blackjack)

- This weapon has the potential to cause fae, youma, or animals of human size or smaller to lose consciousness when you attack them unnoticed from behind. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10361))_
- When struck by such an attack, the target must make a life force resistance roll. The target score is the final score from when the attack hit . If he or she fails, they'll fall unconscious and will not wake up for 2D x 10 minutes . Normal damage is dealt regardless of success or failure. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10362))_
- The attacked opponent gains a +4 bonus to his or her resistance roll if he or she is wearing metal armor. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10363))_
