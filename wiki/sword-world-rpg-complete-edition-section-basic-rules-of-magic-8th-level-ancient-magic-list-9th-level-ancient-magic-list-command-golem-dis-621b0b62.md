- Targets who have this spell cast on them and fail to resist will, after a moment of brightness, be broken down into the smallest unit of matter they were composed of, literally disappearing. It's impossible to regenerate a target that has become like this. If it was a living thing, it can never be revived again, and cannot be revived by Resurrection . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02594))_
- Those who fail to resist this spell, no matter what they're made of, will instantly be turned to stone. Only creatures with natural life (humans, plants, animals, mythical beasts, magical beasts, fae, youma, and daemons) can be targets of this spell, with the exception of magical creatures such as spirits, undead, and golems. A petrified target will cease all life activity until they're released, so they will not age. If it's removed with Dispel Magic , etc., they will be revived in the same state they were before being petrified. However, if a petrified statue is destroyed in the meantime, it will be revived while broken apart at the time the spell is removed, so it will naturally die instantly (or suffer wounds or irreversible injury). _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-02605))_
