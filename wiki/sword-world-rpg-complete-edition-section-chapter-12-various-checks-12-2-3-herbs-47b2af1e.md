## Statements

- Below is a list of herbs known in Forcelia . Each has a provided rarity. Those with the sage, ranger, or healer skill can discern herbs by looking at them if they succeed on a success roll using rarity as the target score and skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05683))_
- Each herb has a base trade value . If adventurers are in a large city, they can purchase them at the prices provided here. If an adventurer is lucky enough to obtain these items and sell them, a merchant will buy them for half the prices provided here. The prices are for one day's worth for one adult. You basically have to take them every day to receive a bonus on illness progression/healing checks. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05684))_
- This is a perennial plant that's often found in high mountains. Each plant has 2 to 3 flowers. The flowers can alleviate fever, and when used on a patient suffering from a fever, they'll receive a +2 bonus on progression/healing checks. One flower is enough for one day for an adult. The flowers can only be stored for 2 to 3 days. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05688))_
- This is a tall tree that has strong fire spirit power and can be found in areas with high average temperatures. The bark of this tree is a type of poison that causes feelings of vomiting. It's not useful for treating illness, but is effective when accidentally taking other poisons, giving you a +2 bonus to resistance rolls. However, it can be dangerous if used too much. A large amount of bark can be removed from one tree and can also be stored. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05692))_
- This is a vine-like shrub that can be found in highlands. Its seeds are ground and mixed with oil to make an ointment that's used to treat skin diseases. When used on illnesses that cause severe inflammation of the skin, such as green slime rot, it provides a +2 bonus on progression/healing checks. Dozens of seeds can be harvested from one plant, and 2 to 3 seeds are enough for one adult per day. Seeds can be stored for long periods of time as long as they're in good condition. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05696))_
- This is an herb that grows in the wilderness and is the staple food of the cockatrice, a magical beast. This herb is useful as a preventative against monsters with the ability to petrify and Petrification magic, but it's also a wonder drug for the dreaded stone skin . If you take this herb daily, you'll receive a +4 bonus to progression/healing checks against stone skin . One henruda plant is enough for one adult for one day. This herb cannot be stored and if it's not used within three days of being collected, it will have no effect at all. For this reason, it' s rarely sold in town apothecaries. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05700))_
- The roots of young mandrake are prized as an ingredient in sedatives. If used against an illness that stimulates going berserk, you'll receive a +2 bonus on illness progression/healing checks. One mandrake is enough for one adult to take for one week. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05704))_
- Depending on the refining process, mandrake can also be used as an ingredient for aphrodisiacs, poisons, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05705))_
- This is an amber stone that comes from the body of the mythical beast lynx. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05708))_
- If you crush, brew, and drink this stone, you will receive a +4 bonus on progression/healing checks for illnesses that affect the mind (such as dance macabre). One lignia stone harvested _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05710))_
