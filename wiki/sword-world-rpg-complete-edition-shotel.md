# Shotel

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- This weapon has a large S-shaped curve at the tip of the blade. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
- When attacking a shield-wielding enemy with a shotel, you can ignore the shield's evasion bonus (even if it's a magic shield!). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.271)
