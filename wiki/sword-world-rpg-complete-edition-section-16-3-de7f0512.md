## Statements by subsection

### Defense Modifier

- Defense power modifier is the defense power bonus you receive while wearing that armor. Metal armor generally has a higher defensive effect and grants a bonus to defense. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10212))_

### Critical Target Variation

- Critical target variation indicates that the armor reduces the chance of being dealt a strong blow from some weapons while wearing it. If this field says yes , the opponent's critical target will be +1 when they attack you with a sword, spear, etc. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10214))_

### Shield Modifiers

- If you ready a shield, it'll be easier to avoid your opponent's attacks. With this rule, if you ready a large shield, your evasion speed will be +2. However, if you do use a large shield, it's inevitable that you'll have to neglect your attacks. Therefore, when using a large shield, you will suffer a -1 penalty to your attack power . However, large shields with a magical power of +1 or greater do not have this penalty. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-10220))_
