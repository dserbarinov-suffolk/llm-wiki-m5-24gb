# Ranged Attacks in Close Combat

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Aiming a ranged attack is more difficult when a foe is next to you. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00379))_
- When you make a ranged attack with a weapon, a spell, or some other means, you have disadvantage on the attack roll if you are within 5 feet of a hostile creature who can see you and who isn't incapacitated. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00379))_
