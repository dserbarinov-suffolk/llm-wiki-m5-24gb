# Shoving a Creature

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- If you're able to make multiple attacks with the Attack action, this attack replaces one of them. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00399))_
- If you succeed, you either knock the target prone or push it 5 feet away from you. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00400))_
- The target must be no more than one size larger than you and must be within your reach. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00400))_
- You succeed automatically if the target is incapacitated. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00400))_
- Instead of making an attack roll, you make a Strength (Athletics) check contested by the target's Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) check (the target chooses the ability to use). _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00400))_
- The target must be no more than one size larger than you and must be within your reach. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-95e73d53-00400))_
