- It' s believed that this illness is caused by the power of the mind spirit sandman becoming too strong. As the illness progresses, the patient will begin to complain of extreme fatigue with even the slightest amount of exercise, and the amount of time spent sleeping each day will increase (use depth x 3 + 9 as a standard). The patient will then die when it reaches the fatal depth of 5. The reason for the outbreak of the disease is unknown, but there are many known cases where excessive use of the spirit magic Sleep has brought the disease to shamans. It may also be triggered by an unnatural sleep caused by the ancient magic Sleep Cloud . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-05624))_
