# Wilderness Events

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about wilderness events:

## Statements

- The Warden should roll twice on the Wilderness Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00331))_
- Each character can choose one Wilderness Action per watch . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00302))_
- Either choice will likely trigger a dungeon event roll." Freya : "Damn. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00603))_
- The Warden narrates the results and then rolls on the Wilderness Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00322))_
- Flesh out a character's history without relying on events tied to specific Backgrounds. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00629))_
- Although the description of these events is delivered consecutively, the results happen roughly at the same time. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00434))_
- Depending on the unfolding events in the game, the Warden is also empowered to introduce new Milestones or discard existing ones. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00365))_
- If the party spends too long in one place, acts without caution, makes noise, or enters a new area, they risk a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00565))_
- After each of the following Wilderness Actions, the party rolls 1d6 to see if they get lost, and the Warden rolls on the Wilderness Events Table . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00610))_
- Players can use their action to move up to three times that distance though that will increase the chance of triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00256))_
- However, it is often more interesting to allow the player to ask as many questions as they like about it and (eventually) to simply tell them how it works . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00539))_
- Moving quickly or without caution may increase the chance of encountering a wandering monster, springing a trap, or triggering a roll on the Dungeon Events table. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00257))_
- In Cairn, wilderness exploration and travel is mostly abstract, handwaving away the minutiae of the journey while still giving the party ample opportunities for interactivity and character agency. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-f6f3256b-00607))_
