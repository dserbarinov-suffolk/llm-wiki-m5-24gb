## Statements

- Resist=None This spell reflects magic cast on the caster directly back onto their opponent. If magic is cast on the person who casts this spell, that magic will rebound back to the opponent who cast it. At the same time, the effect of Magic Reflection will disappear. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02584))_
- However, this can only reflect magic which has an area of effect of one . In the case of magic that affects a space, Magic Reflection has no effect. Also, since the reflection is indiscriminate, even magic that's favorable towards the caster will be reflected. In addition, spells that nullify magic, such as Dispel Magic , cannot be reflected. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02586))_
- If a caster using Magic Reflection casts other magic on his or herself, the effect of Magic Reflection will be lost at the same time the new magic is cast. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02587))_

### 5.1.12 Ancient Magic List / [ Magic Reflection ] / 8th Level Ancient Magic List

- As the name suggests, this is a spell that creates an illusionary beast to attack the target. In the round in which the spell is cast, an illusionary beast will appear in front of the target, and begin attacking on the next round. This attack can only target the person targeted by the spell. The attack will continue until the illusionary beast itself is defeated, this spell is removed, the duration expires, or the target dies. This illusionary beast can only be seen by the target. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02599))_
- In order for anyone other than the target to discover the illusion, they must have knowledge of this spell. Sorcerers can always discover it, but those who do not must attempt and succeed on a success roll with the sage skill's knowledge . Its target score is 16. However, even if you cannot discover it, it's possible to determine that the target is being attacked by an invisible monster, so although you cannot attack this monster directly , you can still support the target indirectly (casting an Enchant Weapon spell on the target's weapon, healing their wounds with a Healing spell, etc.). Damage dealt by an illusionary beast is not an illusion, but is actually treated as a reduction in life force. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-02600))_
