# Common Adventurer Abilities

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- These abilities that any adventurer can acquire are called common adventurer abilities . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- These common abilities use adventurer level + related ability bonus as the baseline score when making success roll checks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- However, when jumping over obstacles, engaging in combat or similar intense movements, if necessary (every round during combat), use adventurer level + agility bonus as the baseline score and make a success roll against target score 7. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- If you fail, you will fall off the horse. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- An average thief should be able to avoid taking any damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- Of course, the height of a horse itself is less than 3 meters, but this is taking into consideration the speed of the horse and the impact of being thrown off. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.120)
- Basically all adventurers can swim. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Therefore, you can swim across any pond that is about 25 meters in size. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- The player must make a success roll using adventurer level + agility bonus as the baseline score, and if they fail, they drown. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Also, in order to swim, you need to be as light as possible. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- To do so, you must succeed on a success roll using adventurer level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- The target scores are 13 for ring mail, hard leather, and mithril chain mail , 15 for chain mail , and 19 for lamellar armor, splint armor, and plate armor . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Those who sink due to the weight of their armor can attempt to remove it underwater. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Each round is 10 seconds long, so this success roll can only be made a number of times equal to your life force ÷ 6, or in other words, your life force bonus. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- If you can 't take off your armor in time, you'll drown. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- An adventurer can only hold their breath underwater for a number of rounds equal to their life force. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- If you're not rescued within 30 minutes, you will automatically die. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- If you're alive, you'll be able to breathe again if you're rescued and treated within 30 minutes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Adventurers can handle small boats using oars or poles. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- However, sailing ships require the full-fledged sailor skill , and adventurer level cannot be used as a substitute. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- In special cases such as avoiding collision with an approaching ship or surviving rapids , the adventurer operating the ship must succeed on a success roll using adventurer level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Any adventurer can operate a small vessel (boat, raft, etc.) that is operated using oars or poles. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- If you fail, the boat will capsize. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Climbing is both a common adventurer ability and a thief skill ability. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- The latter allows for more advanced climbing challenges. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- As a formality, a success roll should still be made, but a failure only occurs on double ones. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- First of all, climbing, which is a common ability of adventurers, always requires assistance such as rope. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- When someone with the thief skill climbs without a rope, or someone without the thief skill climbs with a rope, set the target score using Table 6-2: Determining Climb Target Scores and make a success roll check. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- The baseline score is thief skill level + agility bonus or adventurer level + agility bonus . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Determining the target score is almost the same in both cases. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- Note that when a thief climbs up or down a wall or cliff without using a rope, the conditions marked * are not possible. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- The baseline score is adventurer level + agility bonus = 6 . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- In other words, if he rolls 2D and gets a score of 10 or greater, he'll succeed at climbing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- The target score is 10+2+4= 16 taking into account the conditions of vertical cliff with a foothold and chain mail. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121)
- At this time, only double ones will result in a failure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.121-122)
- If you fall, please refer to Falling Damage below to determine the damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- A check must be made every 10 meters. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- If the result of adding 2D-2 meters is greater than or equal to the distance required for the climb, consider the climb a success. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- The first and second attempts are successful, but the third attempt fails. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- In this case, a success roll is attempted three times. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- 5 - 2 = 3 meters is added to the 20 meters from the two attempts, which means he has climbed 23 meters . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- Now he rolls 2D and gets a 5. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- If the result of the 2D rolled to check the distance the adventurer climbed was double ones or 3 , the adventurer would've fallen from a height of 20 or 21 meters . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- If an adventurer falls, damage will be determined as follows. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- First, fall height x 3 is the base damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- The adventurer will suffer this damage to their life force. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- If the defense roll comes up double ones , all damage reduction is lost, including adventurer level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- However, an adventurer can reduce this damage depending on their adventurer level and armor. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- The base damage minus this damage reduction is the actual damage the adventurer suffers. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- Falling damage = fall height x 3 (defense roll result + adventurer level). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- On the other hand, if you have something like spikes sticking out, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- Base falling damage (fall height x 3) is for falling on normal ground. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- If the result of this subtraction is 0 or less, even if the thief falls, they can land cleanly on their feet or stand up in one motion. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- Owners of the thief skill can greatly reduce this damage with their tumble ability. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- A thief with thief skill level 3 and dexterity bonus +2 can fall from a height of 5 meters or less without suffering any damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- The latter allows you to jump farther and higher. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- Long jump and high jump are common adventurer abilities, and are also abilities of the thief skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- The baseline score is thief skill level + agility bonus or adventurer level + agility bonus (Table 6-3: Determining Long Jump Target Scores). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- In this case, the distance you can jump will be further improved. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- In addition, the thief skill allows you to attempt pole long jumps and pole high jumps using a long pole. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- To attempt a pole high jump, you will need a pole that is about the same length as the height you are attempting to jump (Table 6-4: Determining High Jump Target Scores). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- However, to attempt a pole long jump, you need a pole that is at least half as long as the distance you are attempting to jump. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122)
- An adventurer with adventurer level 4, agility bonus +2, wearing chain mail, is in a cave beneath a volcano. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122-123)
- At this time, considering the following conditions: 7 meter width , chain mail , the rocky area is too narrow to make a proper run-up , and death is inevitable if you fall , the target score is 20+4+2+2= 28. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122-123)
- Below is boiling lava. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122-123)
- The baseline score is 6 , so anything other than double sixes is a failure. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.122-123)
- The adventurer throws away his luggage and takes on a light outfit with only a short sword strapped to his waist. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- This will allow him to utilize his thief skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- The target score at this time is 9 + 0 - 2 + 2 + 2 = 11 , taking into account the following conditions: 7 meters wide , no armor , no luggage , insufficient run-up , and danger . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- The adventurer has 1 level in the thief skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- The baseline score is 3, so it's a success if he rolls 8 or greater on 2D. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- Thinking it'd be impossible to jump like this, he takes off his heavy chain mail. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- However, this can be a problem if the ground you jump over is significantly lower than the ground you took off from. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- It usually doesn't cause much damage, and if you're a thief, you'll be able to land it perfectly. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)
- Jumping over obstacles with a high jump does not guarantee a safe landing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.123)

```
The riding rules here apply when dealing with trained horses, such as riding horses, war horses, etc. When trying to ride a wild horse, you must first succeed on a success roll while attempting to mount it. At this time, the target score is 9, but since the wild horse is not wearing a rein, a -4 penalty will be imposed. You can think of it as actually 13 . If you want to control a wild horse in combat, etc. the target score is 11. If you consider the penalty for not having reins, it's effectively 15 .
```
