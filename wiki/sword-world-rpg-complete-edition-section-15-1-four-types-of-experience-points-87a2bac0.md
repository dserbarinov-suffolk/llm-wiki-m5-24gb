## Statements

- In Sword World RPG , there are four ways to gain experience points. These are accomplishing missions , fighting monsters , success rolls , and special bonuses . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09905))_

## Statements by subsection

### 15.1 Four Types of Experience Points / Fighting Monsters

- You can also earn experience points by defeating monsters. Monster level x 10 ÷ number of adventurers experience points will be awarded for each monster. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09914))_

### 15.1 Four Types of Experience Points / Special Bonuses

- The game master is free to award bonus experience for particularly outstanding play. However, this should be done as sparingly as possible. Giving away a large amount of experience points is harmful to the enjoyment of the game. It'd be most appropriate to keep it to about 1/10th of the experience points earned from accomplishing a mission. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09919))_
