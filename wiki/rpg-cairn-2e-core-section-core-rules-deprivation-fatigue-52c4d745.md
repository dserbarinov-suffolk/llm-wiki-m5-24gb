## Statements

- A PC that lacks a crucial need (such as food or rest) is Deprived . Anyone Deprived for more than a day adds Fatigue to their inventory, one for each day. A Deprived PC cannot recover HP , Attributes, or item slots from Fatigue . _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00171))_
- A PC may also be forced to add Fatigue after casting spells or due to events occurring in the fiction. Each Fatigue occupies one slot _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00172))_
- and lasts until the PC is able to recuperate (such as with a full night's rest in a safe spot). _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00173))_
- If a character is forced to add Fatigue to their inventory but they have no free slots, they must drop an item from their inventory. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00174))_
