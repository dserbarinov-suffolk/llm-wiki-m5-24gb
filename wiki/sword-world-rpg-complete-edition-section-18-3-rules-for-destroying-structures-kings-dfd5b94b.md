- The king is an absolute ruler. All national policies are determined by the king, and as the head of judicial power, he also presides over trials. In a nation, defying the king is not permitted. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10648))_
- However, this is only on the surface if a king were to govern poorly, movements to overthrow such a king would inevitably arise. This could also create opportunities for foreign invasion.
