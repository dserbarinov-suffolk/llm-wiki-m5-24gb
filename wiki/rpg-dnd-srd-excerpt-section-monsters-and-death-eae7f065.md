# Monsters and Death

From [[rpg-dnd-srd-excerpt]].

## Statements

- Most GMs have a monster die the instant it drops to 0 hit points, rather than having it fall unconscious and make death saving throws. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00446))_
- Mighty villains and special nonplayer characters are common exceptions; the GM might have them fall unconscious and follow the same rules as player characters. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-2a314be6-00447))_
