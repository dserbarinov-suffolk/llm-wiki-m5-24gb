## Statements

- When you chant this spell, a faint glow of light inhabits the target's armor or shield, covering the target's entire body in protective light. The protective light absorbs all damage up to 50 points in total, whether it's caused by weapons or by magic. The protective light thus fades each time you take damage, and disappears when it's dealt more than 50 points of damage. When the protection runs out, any damage beyond that (such as suffering 20 damage when the protection has only 10 remaining) will not reach the target. Any damage is first absorbed by this protective light, so until it vanishes, virtually no damage can be dealt directly to the body. When damage is dealt to this protection, reductions due to armor or adventurer level are not taken into account. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03157))_
