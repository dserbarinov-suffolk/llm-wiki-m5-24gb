# Requiem

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The effect begins on the fourth round after starting the spellsong. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.111)
- For undead that fail to resist, roll 2D and look at Table 5-10: Requiem to determine the effect it has on them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.111)
- With a song that mourns the souls of the dead, it has the effect of weakening the undead. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.111)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
If this spellsong is sung to the deceased for over an hour during their funeral, the deceased will never become undead.
```
