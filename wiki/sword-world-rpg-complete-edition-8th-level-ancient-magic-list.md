# 8th Level Ancient Magic List

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The attack will continue until the illusionary beast itself is defeated, this spell is removed, the duration expires, or the target dies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- As the name suggests, this is a spell that creates an illusionary beast to attack the target. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- In the round in which the spell is cast, an illusionary beast will appear in front of the target, and begin attacking on the next round. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Sorcerers can always discover it, but those who do not must attempt and succeed on a success roll with the sage skill's knowledge . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Its target score is 16. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- In order for anyone other than the target to discover the illusion, they must have knowledge of this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Effect=Paralyzes the target's body within the area of effect, causing them to lose consciousness. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Unlike Sleep Cloud , a creature who loses consciousness from this spell cannot be woken up by damage, kicking them or shaking them, they will remain unconscious for one hour. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- However, if you remove it with Dispel Magic , they will regain their consciousness. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- All creatures within the area of effect must attempt to resist this spell. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- As a result, most physical attacks should be nullified, but you will be wounded if your defense roll is double ones , or if you receive more than 30 points of damage. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- However, it cannot prevent damage from pure magic, such as Energy Bolt or Fireball , nor damage from fire, acid, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- The caster can also add their own damage reduction to this. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- This spell is invisible around the caster's body, moreover it creates a magical barrier that changes shape with the caster's movement. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Even if you fall from a very high place or a huge piece of rubble falls on you, you will still be wounded if the damage you receive exceeds 30 points. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Type=Target score, damage (Electric-type) Expansion=Final score, duration, distance, targets, damage certainty Resist= Reduced effect. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Its strike power is 20. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- Lightning Bind is a spell that entangles the target in a net of electricity, completely sealing their movement. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.80)
- However, in this case, they will take a -4 penalty to the final score of all success rolls, because they must fight against the pain and binding they receive from the electric net. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- This spell works on targets slightly larger than a human (such as an ogre or a manticore), but has no effect on targets of a larger size than that (such as a dragon or a roc). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- The same applies if your body is destroyed (your life force or life points become 0 or less) while you are a wraith. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- In this case, the body becomes nothing more than an empty husk without a soul, so it cannot be brought back to life by any means. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- A caster who casts this spell will be able to separate their spiritual body from their physical body, allowing only their ethereal body to move around freely. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- Wraiths are a kind of undead, but they can resume their life activities once they return to their own bodies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- If the wraith itself is destroyed, the caster dies. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
- This ethereal body is called a wraith. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.81)
