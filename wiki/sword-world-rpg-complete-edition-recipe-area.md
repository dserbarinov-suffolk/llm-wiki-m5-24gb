## Applicability And Rationale

- This represents the target that the magic affects. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01840))_
- If it says caster , the magic works only on the caster who cast the spell. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01841))_
- It is also possible for casters to cast it on themselves. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01842))_
- There are many other ways to write this, but it should be clear if you read the description. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-01844))_

### Atom 1: `worked-example`

If it works on a specific space or area , then everything within that area is targeted. For example, a Fireball explosion deals explosion damage to everything within a 3 meter radius. It makes no difference whether it's the caster's enemies, allies, or the caster.
