# 6.4

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- The sage skill represents a comprehensive understanding and depth of knowledge in all academic fields. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- Academic fields in Sword World are undifferentiated, and owners of this skill are skilled in all disciplines. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- For more information, please refer to Chapter 8: Character Growth (see p. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- Many sorcerers have this skill. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- This includes knowledge of the various monsters that live in Alecrast. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- Sages have a wide range of knowledge. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- Using sage skill level + intelligence bonus as the baseline score, make a success roll with this rarity as the target score, and if you succeed, you know the monster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- Each monster has a score called rarity . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- If the check fails, the character has no knowledge of the monster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)
- Sages can learn different languages depending on their skill level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)

## Rules

- Sages can learn different languages depending on their skill level. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.118)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
There is no requirement that you have to have the sage skill and the sorcerer skill at the same time, but if you do acquire the sage skill and the sorcerer skill at the same time, you will receive a bonus on experience points consumed.
```

<a id="atom-2"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
When a sage sees a monster or hears its name, this ability may allow them to discover its true identity.
```

<a id="atom-3"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
Even if the player knows, they must treat it as if they had no prior knowledge.
```
