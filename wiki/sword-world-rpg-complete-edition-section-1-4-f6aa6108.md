### Character Creation / 1.4.1 Choose a Race

- First, the player character's race must be chosen. There are five types to choose from: human, dwarf, grassrunner, elf, and half-elf. Each race has their own characteristics and limitations. Each also has strengths and weaknesses in the ability scores that form the basis of the character. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00292))_
- Each race has their own characteristics and limitations. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00292))_

### Character Creation / 【 Human 】

- Humans have the most average ability scores and can do almost anything without a hitch. They have no restrictions on skill selection. This is probably the most suitable race for a beginner to choose. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00294))_

### Character Creation / 【 Dwarf 】

- Dwarves excel in strength, life force, and mental power. On the other hand, they tend to be less agile and less intelligent. In terms of skills, sorcerer and shaman cannot be chosen. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00296))_

### Character Creation / 【 Grassrunner 】

- Grassrunners have excellent dexterity, agility, and mental power. Their strength, on the other hand, is decidedly inferior. Their intelligence and life force are average. Rune master skills cannot be acquired. At the same time, their low strength does not let them benefit much from acquiring the fighter skill. It's a good idea to advance the thief skill acquired from the beginning. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00298))_

### Character Creation / 【 Elf 】

- Elves excel in dexterity, agility, intelligence, and mental power. On the other hand, their strength and life force tend to be low. There is a restriction that they cannot acquire the priest skill. It would be better to advance the shaman skill acquired from the beginning, or to acquire the sorcerer skill. They are also a suitable race for thief. They're not very suitable for fighter. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00300))_

### Character Creation / 【 Half-Elf 】

- Half-elves are halfway between human and elf in terms of ability. Therefore, their dexterity, agility, intelligence, and mental power are slightly higher, while their strength and life force are slightly lower. However, they do not deviate very far from the average and are fully capable of handling all skills. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00302))_

- Therefore, their dexterity, agility, intelligence, and mental power are slightly higher, while their strength and life force are slightly lower. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00302))_

### Character Creation / 1.4.2 Determine Ability Scores

- Once you've chosen a race, you can determine your ability scores. First, you must roll the dice to determine subability scores A~H . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00308))_
- A character's six ability scores are each the totals of two of these eight subability scores. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00310))_
- Depending on whether a half-elf was raised by humans or elves, the skills they have from the start and the limitations on the skills they can acquire differ. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00311))_
- On the table, "D" stands for dice . 1D stands for one die , 2D stands for two dice , and 1/2D stands for half the result of one dice (rounded up) . In addition, a notation such as 1D+6 means to roll one dice and add 6 to the result . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00343))_
- Once ability scores are determined, sub-ability scores are never used during the game. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00347))_
- Now, let's determine ability scores. Since our race is human, all we have to do is roll 2D, that's two dice, from A-H, all the way down. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00350))_
- Our rolls are 9, 7, 5, 8, 6, 8, 10, and 4. These can be considered average, with neither extremely high nor low rolls. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00351))_
- The average for a human is 14 in all ability scores, so other than a slightly higher life force, this character seems to be pretty average. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00366))_
- Once the ability scores have been determined, also find the ability bonuses and write them down on the Character Sheet. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00367))_
- For this character being created, the bonuses are dexterity +2, agility +2, intelligence +2, strength +2, life force +3, and mental power +2. Since the average human ability score is 14, +2 is a very average ability bonus. This character can be considered fairly mediocre except for the excellent life force bonus. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00370))_
- Depending on whether a half-elf was raised by humans or elves, the skills they have from the start and the limitations on the skills they can acquire differ. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00311))_
- In addition, a notation such as 1D+6 means to roll one dice and add 6 to the result . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-00343))_
