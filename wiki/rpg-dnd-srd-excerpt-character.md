## Statements

- (A character's starting skill proficiencies are determined at character creation, and a monster's skill proficiencies appear in the monster's stat block.) _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00102))_
- The character who's leading the effort-or the one with the highest ability modifier-can make an ability check with advantage, reflecting the help provided by the other characters. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00114))_
- The AC of a character is determined at character creation, whereas the AC of a monster is in its stat block. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00358))_
- Mighty villains and special nonplayer characters are common exceptions; the GM might have them fall unconscious and follow the same rules as player characters. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00448))_
- The GM calls for an ability check when a character or monster attempts an action (other than an attack) that has a chance of failure. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00091))_
- Sometimes one character's or monster's efforts are directly opposed to another's. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00098))_
- Each ability covers a broad range of capabilities, including skills that a character or a monster can be proficient in. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00102))_
- So a character who has proficiency in the Stealth skill is particularly good at Dexterity checks related to sneaking and hiding. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00103))_
- If the character lacks that proficiency, he or she just makes a Strength check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00107))_
- For example, if a character attempts to climb up a dangerous cliff, the GM might ask for a Strength (Athletics) check. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00107))_
- For example, if a 1st-level character has a Wisdom of 15 and proficiency in Perception, he or she has a passive Wisdom (Perception) score of 14. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00111))_
- Here's how to determine a character's total for a passive check: 10 + all modifiers that normally apply to the check If the character has advantage on the check, add 5. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00111))_
- Moreover, a character can help only when two or more individuals working together would actually be productive. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00115))_
- In such a situation, the characters who are skilled at a particular task help cover those who aren't. _(rpg_dnd_srd_excerpt.pdf (source-range-b7d08967-00117))_
