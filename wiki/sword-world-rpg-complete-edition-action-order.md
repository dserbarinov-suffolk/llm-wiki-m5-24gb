# Action Order

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Once action announcements are complete, it is time to resolve both friends' and foes' actions. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- Combat will then actually take place. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- Therefore, the goblin will act before Ducard II. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- This is done in order of agility , friend and foe alike. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- Therefore, it is possible to have a mixture of friends and foes, as in a1 → b1 → a2 .... (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- If, as a result of the action of someone with higher agility, someone with lower agility is unable to perform their intended action , it means that their action is no longer possible . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- If two have exactly the same agility, their actions are considered to be simultaneous . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
- Ducard II is fighting a goblin. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.36)
