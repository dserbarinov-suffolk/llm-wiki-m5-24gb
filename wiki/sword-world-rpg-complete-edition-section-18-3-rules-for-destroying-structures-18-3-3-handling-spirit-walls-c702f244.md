## Statements

- When using these rules for destroying structures to destroy the spirit magic Spirit Wall, please do so as follows. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10552))_
- Treat it as a stone wall. Without expansion, defense points are 15 and destruction points are 20. Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's stone. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10554))_
- Treat it as an ice wall. Without expansion, defense points are 15 and destruction points are 20. Its minimum destruction points are 1 because it's ice. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10556))_
- Treat it as a vegetation wall. Without expansion, defense points and destruction points are both 15. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10558))_
- Spirit Wall: Dryad has the power to regenerate even if it's destroyed. Each round, Spirit Wall: Dryad regenerates 1 defense point and 1 destruction point. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-10559))_
