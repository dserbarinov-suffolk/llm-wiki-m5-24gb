# Double Sixes and Double Ones

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- If a roll comes up double sixes or double ones during combat between characters, the situation is resolved as follows:. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- If the attacker's roll is double ones , the attack fails without question. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- However, if the defender's roll is also double sixes, the attack fails . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- If the attacker's roll is double sixes , it' s basically an automatic hit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- If the defender's roll is double ones , it' s basically an automatic hit. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- However, if the attacker's roll is also double ones, the attack fails . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- It doesn't matter even if the attacker rolls double sixes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
- Please refer to Table 4-4: Attack Checks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.48)
