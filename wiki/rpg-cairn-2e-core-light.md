# Light

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about light:

## Statements

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Actions

- A light source and a safe location are required to rest . Present or oncoming danger makes rest impossible. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00284))_

### Procedures / Dungeon Exploration / Dungeon Elements

- Characters without a light source may suffer from panic until their situation is remedied. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00294))_

### Procedures / Terrain Difficulty / Wilderness Elements

- Torches and other radial sources of light illuminate 40ft ahead of the party, but beyond that only provides a dim outline of objects. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00363))_

- Characters without a light source may suffer from panic until their situation is remedied. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00364))_

### Saves / Traditional Saves / WIL

- Warden : "In the dim light of the early dawn, you notice a note slipped through the street vent. It reads, 'Speak the following, and you will be free: "The Pale Man Longs To Die." You have but one chance.'" _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00541))_

### Dungeon Exploration / Dungeon Exploration Example / The Courtyard

- Warden : "Alright, you move to the rubble adjacent to the statue. It looks extremely heavy and difficult to move. In the light from Wolfram's torch, you can see something red and shiny glinting in the cracks of the pile. What do you do?" _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-9b4fff26-00641))_
