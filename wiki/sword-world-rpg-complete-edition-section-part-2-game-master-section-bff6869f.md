## Statements

- This is the part for the game master. A game master requires a deeper mastery of an RPG compared to players. When you're a game master, you will have to remember rules that you wouldn't have to remember when you're a player. Rules that aren't necessary from a player's point of view are included here. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00084))_
- Therefore, if you're solely a player, you don't have to force yourself to memorize the rules of this part. Conversely, a game master must understand the rules included here in addition to those included in Part 1. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-00085))_
