- Resist=None Enables communication with dragon creatures such as wyverns, wyrms, and dragons (elder, lesser, ancient). If the opponent is intelligent enough, you can also negotiate. - Wings shaped like dragon wings grow on your back. The size depends on the caster's body. Using these wings, the caster can fly freely in the sky.
