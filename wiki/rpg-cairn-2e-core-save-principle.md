# Save Principles

What [[rpg-cairn-2e-core]] covers about save principles:

## Statements

- If the answer is "I don't know," then a roll with the Die of Fate may be necessary. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00481))_
- If the answer is "no," then a save is usually warranted. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00481))_
- When in doubt, ask whether the party has the time, resources, skills, or advantage to overcome a problem. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00481))_
- If the risk is so great that the outcome might be fatal, make sure the player understands what they're getting into. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00483))_
- Character death should never come as a surprise. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00483))_
- Always provide multiple viable options for the PCs to choose from. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00484))_
- The more choices, the more interesting the situation becomes. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00484))_
- You can tailor the results based on the totality of input: the effort, forethought, resources, and skill that went into the roll. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00485))_
- The outcome of a save need not be a simple binary. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00485))_
- This doesn't mean revealing all of the world's secrets, but provide each player with the maximum information their character might have. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00486))_
- Avoid requiring a dice roll whenever possible. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00487))_
- Simplifying results to a single roll can increase tension while maximizing player input. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00488))_
- Replace multiple dice rolls with a single dice throw. _(rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00488))_

## Technical atoms

> Context: When in doubt, ask whether the party has the time, resources, skills, or advantage to overcome a problem. If the answer is "no," then a save is usually warranted. If the answer is "I don't know," then a roll with the Die of Fate may be necessary.
_(context: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00481))_

> If the party cannot proceed due to circumstances beyond their control, give them another option to consider.
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00482))_

> Context: The outcome of a save need not be a simple binary. You can tailor the results based on the totality of input: the effort, forethought, resources, and skill that went into the roll.
_(context: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00485))_

> If a PC can solve a problem using purely narrative components, do not ask for a roll .
_(source: rpg_cairn_2e_core.pdf (source-range-bc2972ae-00487))_
