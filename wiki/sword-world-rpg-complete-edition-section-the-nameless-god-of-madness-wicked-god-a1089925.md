## Statements

- He is the god of madness, and because of his madness, he is a deity whose name cannot be identified. It is said that he exists in the star world and causes people to go mad, and it is believed that those who come into contact with the light emitted by this mad god on a full moon's night will be afflicted with madness. His followers are few and he has no temples either. The priests of his cult teach that madness frees its believers from bondage and gives them freedom. It is said that he gives sudden inspiration that is not based in logic, and for this reason he is also considered the god of art and invention, so there are sometimes believers in this god among artists and sages. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03315))_
- She is feared as the goddess of destruction and the goddess of the undead, and is considered to be the goddess who controls the end . Believers consider destruction and slaughter to be their mission anyway, as they believe that without destruction there can be no new beginning. Due to the nature of her faith, she has no temples, etc. but her doctrines have been continually passed down throughout the past and present, so she has believers. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03317))_
- He is the god of the dead of the sea, and teaches that life is pain and sin, and that death is the only way to be saved from it. Mirleaf believers believe that new challenges and efforts to survive (as exemplified by voyages) create and prolong suffering, and that a prompt death is the only mercy for those who do such things. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03319))_
- He has almost no temples. Even if he did, they would never appear publicly. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03320))_
- Game masters can also create original gods and spells in addition to these. This is a good thing, but it would probably be safer not to delve into actual religious issues. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03321))_

- This is the ability to pray and communicate with a god, asking whether or not one's actions are right. Occasionally a god will give you a revelation (given at the game master's discretion). However, a god's words can only be understood abstractly by humans, and you cannot ask detailed questions to the gods. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-03325))_
