## Statements

- The scope of this ability is very wide. It's for checks regarding whether you know or don't know about things in all fields, from trivia to politics to science. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04142))_
- There are two methods of making checks. One is one where the game master judges whether or not the player knows based on their sage skill level and tells them, without providing a rarity . The other is to provide a rarity for each piece of knowledge, and ask for a check via success roll, similar to monster check . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04143))_
- Please use whichever method the game master deems appropriate for each situation. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04144))_

### Chapter 6: / Sage Skill / [ ④ Knowledge [Intelligence]] (Not retriable) / [ ⑤ Research [Intelligence] ] (Not retriable)

- When a sage fails a success roll on a check such as monster check , treasure appraisal (described below), or knowledge and is unable to figure it out, the sage can later find out by researching literature. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04147))_
- Research basically takes about one week for each item (this may be increased or decreased at the game master's discretion). During this time, you cannot do much other than research. Also, if you don't have a place with a wealth of literature (the best place is the academy of sages = sorcerers' guild in each city), research itself is impossible. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04148))_
- If the research is successful, the sage may make another success roll. When something they didn't know (something that failed) becomes a success, the sage has found a description of it in the literature and has access to that knowledge. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04149))_
- Each bard carries their favorite instrument in hand. Bards are free to choose any instrument, but only one type. If you use other instruments, you won't be able to use abilities that require an instrument.
