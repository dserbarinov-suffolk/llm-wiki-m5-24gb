# 16.6.2 Two-Handed Attacks

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- If a character has weapons in both hands, he or she has three options for attacking. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- Also, the required strength for one of the weapons is limited to half your own strength (rounded up) . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- There's no need to reduce one to a quarter. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- If you attack with only one hand, there is no penalty, and if you attack with both hands, the penalty is only -2 for each. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
- When a character is ambidextrous, there is no opposite hand. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.268)
