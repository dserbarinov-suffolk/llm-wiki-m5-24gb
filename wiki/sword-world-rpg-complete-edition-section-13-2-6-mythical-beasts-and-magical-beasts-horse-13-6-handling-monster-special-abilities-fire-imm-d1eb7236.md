## Statements

- Fire attacks are completely ineffective. Magic that deals damage with fire ( Fire Bolt and Fireball ) is completely useless, and the strike power bonus of Fire Weapon is also not added. If one of these monsters also has the ability normal weapon immunity , it cannot be wounded by a weapon that had Fire Weapon cast on it. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08767))_
