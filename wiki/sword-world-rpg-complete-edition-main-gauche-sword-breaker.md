# Main-gauche/Sword Breaker

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Y ou'll gain a +1 bonus to evasion speed only if your opponent's weapon is a dagger , rapier , or short sword . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
- A main-gauche is a dagger held in the left hand (right hand for left-handed adventurers) for parrying. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
- It has a similar bonus to evasion speed, and moreover, if used skillfully, can break or drop an opponent's weapon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
- If you roll an 11 , and you evade your opponent's attack, you can make your opponent drop his or her weapon. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
- A sword breaker is a type of maingauche with serrated blades lined up along the back. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
- Of course, your opponent's weapons are limited to daggers , rapiers , and short swords . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
- Main-gauche and sword breaker have the same attack scores as a dagger . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.269)
