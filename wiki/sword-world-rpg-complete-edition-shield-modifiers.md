# Shield Modifiers

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- With this optional rule, there are differences in the way small and large shields are handled ( Table 16-2: Shield Optional Rules ). (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- Therefore, when using a large shield, you will suffer a -1 penalty to your attack power . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- However, large shields with a magical power of +1 or greater do not have this penalty. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- With this rule, if you ready a large shield, your evasion speed will be +2. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- However, if you do use a large shield, it's inevitable that you'll have to neglect your attacks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
- If you ready a shield, it'll be easier to avoid your opponent's attacks. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.263)
