# 13.2.6 Mythical Beasts and Magical Beasts / [ Horse ] / 13.6 Handling Monster Special Abilities / 《 Mental Attack Immunity 》

From [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]].

## Statements

- Monsters, etc. that do not have intellect or mind have this ability, so magic with mental effects ( Fear , etc.) is ineffective. If they do not have mental points (expressed as -), magic that deals damage to mental points ( Shade , etc.) is likewise ineffective. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-08759))_
