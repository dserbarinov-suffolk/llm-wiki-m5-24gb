# 6th Level Basic Dark Magic List [ Mesmerize ]

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- Decay, which normally takes two to three days, will be reduced to half an hour. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- However, this has no effect on metals or rocks, and it has no effect on living creatures at all. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- As long as an object is perishable, it will decay, even if under conditions where decay would normally not progress, such as the environment being below freezing, or being soaked in a saturated brine. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- This spell has the effect of accelerating the rate of decay and causing the target to rot faster. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- Objects that had this spell cast on them will decay very quickly. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- Targets are limited to non-living meat, plants, fibers, etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- If double the normal amount of mental power is consumed, the target will decay in 15 minutes, and if triple , the target will decay in 10 minutes. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- Even if the spell is removed with Perfect Cancellation , etc. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)
- the target will not return to the state it was in before it decayed (it will return with the counter magic Preservation . (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.146)

**Atom:** example

```
This spell hypnotizes the opponent, rendering them unable to actively think. The caster can control the hypnotized subject at will. However, this cannot be used to make them do intense movements such as combat, or casting magic. It can only be as much as making them walk slowly in the direction you want or bring you something in their hands. You also cannot give complex commands that require the person to judge the situation and have them carry them out. For example, if you were to command someone to go to a certain house and steal some jewels , it wouldn't be possible because those under the spell's influence wouldn't be able to think of a way to break into that house. Furthermore, you cannot command actions that directly encourage suicide. However, it is possible to make someone walk off a cliff and fall to their death.
```
