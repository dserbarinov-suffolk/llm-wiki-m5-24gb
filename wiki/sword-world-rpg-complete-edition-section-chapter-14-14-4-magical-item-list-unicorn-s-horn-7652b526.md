## Statements

- Those who have this horn will be able to cast all spirit magic sourced from the power of the unknown spirit of life, such as Healing and Restore Health . The horn stores 10~200 mental points, and each time you use magic, the base mental power cost of the magic is consumed. If you need the horn's magic power, set it to 10. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-e5870dca-09897))_
