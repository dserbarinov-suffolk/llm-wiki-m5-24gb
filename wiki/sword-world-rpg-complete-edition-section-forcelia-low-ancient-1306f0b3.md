## Statements

- Low ancient is the normal language used in the ancient kingdom of Kastuul, which once flourished throughout Alecrast. Although it is now a dead language, its words are still passed down among people and monsters connected to the ancient kingdom. Also, those called sages and sorcerers study low ancient, considering it an almost essential language. This is because you cannot begin to understand the great civilization of the ancient kingdom unless you know low ancient. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-05393))_
