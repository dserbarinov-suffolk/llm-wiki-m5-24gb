## Applicability And Rationale

- Basically all adventurers can swim. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04205))_
- Therefore, you can swim across any pond that is about 25 meters in size. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04205))_
- The player must make a success roll using adventurer level + agility bonus as the baseline score, and if they fail, they drown. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04206))_
- Also, in order to swim, you need to be as light as possible. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04207))_
- To do so, you must succeed on a success roll using adventurer level + dexterity bonus as the baseline score. _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04208))_
- The target scores are 13 for ring mail, hard leather, and mithril chain mail , 15 for chain mail , and 19 for lamellar armor, splint armor, and plate armor . _(Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04208))_

## Technical Atoms

### Atom 1: `worked-example`

_Source: Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf (source-range-0d48087c-04210)_

```
Heavy weapons and luggage also impede swimming. If you recklessly try to swim with a sword, spear, or ax, then using adventurer level + agility bonus as the baseline score, make a success roll against target score 13 (this can be increased or decreased depending on the weight of your belongings. Also, if the distance is long or there are rapids, add a few more points to the target score), and if you fail, you will sink. However, unlike armor, weapons can be easily discarded. Even when carrying something other than a weapon, the game master should consider its weight when determining the target score.
```
