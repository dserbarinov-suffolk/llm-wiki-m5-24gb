# [ ⑬ Follow [Agility] ] (Equipment restriction)

Source: [[sword-world-rpg-complete-edition]]

## Statements

- A tie is a failure to follow. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- This is the ability to follow an opponent without them noticing. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- What action the person takes after that is up to them. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- If you fail, your opponent will realize that they are being followed. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.115)
- It cannot be used indoors or in a city. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- This handled the same way as the thief skill's ability of the same name. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)
- However, it can only be used in nature. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)

## Rules

- It cannot be used indoors or in a city. (Sword World RPG - Complete Edition.pdf p.117)

## Technical atoms

<a id="atom-1"></a>
**Atom:** rule

```
The side that's following must use thief skill level + agility bonus as their baseline score, while the side being followed must use adventurer level + intelligence bonus , and success or failure is determined by comparing the final scores of their success rolls.
```
